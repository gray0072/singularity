# Диалоги с самим собой (Dialogues with Myself) — notes

Russian original: [../proza/dialogi-s-samim-soboj.md](../proza/dialogi-s-samim-soboj.md).

- **title_ru:** Диалоги с самим собой
- **title_en:** Dialogues with Myself
- **genre:** philosophical and political essays in the form of an inner dialogue between two halves of the narrator, "Я1" and "Я2", set at his dacha
- **published:** 14.08.2023 – 15.08.2025 (four pieces)
- **source:**
  - https://proza.ru/2023/08/14/1260 — «О демократии, свободе личности и сменности власти»
  - https://proza.ru/2023/09/02/1393 — «О национализме, патриотизме и любви»
  - https://proza.ru/2025/05/25/1300 — «О главной проблеме человечества»
  - https://proza.ru/2025/08/15/1695 — «О либеральной идее и либеральных ценностях»
- **words:** ~11,000 in total (about 2,000–3,500 per essay)

## Summary

All four pieces share one frame. The narrator sits at his dacha (a terrace, a rocking chair, coffee, a sunset, birds at the
feeder) and argues with himself. **Я1** is the main, conservative voice and nearly always wins. **Я2** speaks for a mild,
"reasonable" liberal: he enjoys simple pleasures, values personal freedom, says he does not believe in God, and often
calls Я1's arguments "demagoguery". Each piece ends with a domestic exit instead of a conclusion. The essays are listed
here in order of publication. The source file lists them newest first.

### 1. On democracy, personal freedom and rotation of power (14.08.2023)

The two halves come back from a general meeting of the gardening association (СНТ), annoyed by people who demand new
projects and lower fees in the same breath. Я2 defends democracy as the least bad system. Я1 takes it apart.
- The most "democratic" Russian rulers, Gorbachev and Yeltsin, wrecked the country. The most useful ones, Peter I and
  Stalin, were despots.
- Interests clash and cannot all be protected (the neighbours Serdyukov and Gavrilov fighting over trees on the
  boundary), so any government hurts someone.
- Despotism concentrates power and gets things done, and armies and parent–child relations are despotic everywhere.
  Freedom works only in a society mature enough to restrain itself. Otherwise "overgrown children" must be forced into
  doing what they ought to.
- Rotation of power is a roulette wheel and an ideological template. Nobody worried about Elizabeth II's term in office.
- A 51:49 win is a strange model of "the majority rules". Zelensky won honestly on a promise of peace and then did the
  opposite. Paid campaigns make candidates serve their investors. The US version of democracy is the power of the
  American people over everyone else (the Stalin–Roosevelt joke).
- Case study from the СНТ: five crooked chairmen in a row were elected by honest direct vote, because the low salary
  attracted only thieves. Then one honest man worked unpaid, saved the association and was re-elected, while an
  ex-crook appealed to "rotation of power" to get rid of him.

Я1 says he is no fan of despotism. He would like to live in a civil democratic society but thinks it needs conditions
that are not yet in place. His caution comes from his own past: he believed in Marxism to the point of self-sacrifice
and in the 1970s organised an underground cell. Hence his rule: be a fanatic of no ideology. Я2 retorts that this
principle also looks like an ideology. Ending: "we'll find out which comes first, communism or true democracy", then
lunch, slippers, and the empty rocking chair still swaying.

### 2. On nationalism, patriotism and love (02.09.2023)

Sunset, plaid blanket, chocolates. Я2 refuses to say he "loves" sweets. Loving sweets means pleasure, and true love is
the opposite: transcendent and illogical, ready for discomfort and sacrifice. Example: once, after years in places with
no birch trees, he saw a birch wood from a train window and wept. An acquaintance, Fyodor Sorokin, says love of the
motherland is a propaganda slogan. The reply is Yesenin («Не надо рая, дайте родину мою»), the soldiers who died with
"For the Motherland!", Gastello and Matrosov. Sorokin is called an egoist who confuses love with consumption, even with
his wife and children. The argument then turns: **patriotism rests on love and nationalism on hatred and separation,
so they are opposites.** The proof: Soviet soldiers saved the children of their enemies (the Soviet soldier memorial in
Berlin), and the narrator's grandmother told how locals fed German prisoners of war. The second half is a practical
guide to *learning* to love by suppressing egoism through small habits: give the sweet away, give up your seat, hold
the door, never jump the queue, give alms, smile. Giving makes you spiritually richer. A footnote recommends his song
«Куда уходит счастье» (stihi.ru, 2017). Here the roles are partly reversed: Я2 delivers the doctrine and Я1 asks the
questions.

### 3. On the main problem of humanity (25.05.2025)

Winter, frost patterns on the window. A long, carefully watched scene at the bird feeder (shaped like Baba Yaga's hut
on chicken legs, with a decorative owl on the perch): one bully chaffinch drives off sparrows and other chaffinches,
although the hopper refills itself. Then the narrator talks to his reflection in a mirrored wardrobe. Я1 lists the
miseries of wild creatures and ancient people. Humanity's main problem is **not valuing what it has**, and it drives
family breakdown, social conflict and wars that could end humanity. Я1 now says outright "I believe in God", against
Я2's "I believe in people".
- The insatiable "**хочучелы**" (want-addicts, his coinage) go from having enough to wanting to be God, all-powerful,
  immortal. His list runs Genghis Khan, Roman emperors, Napoleon, Hitler, and now Obama and Brzezinski. Plans
  "Dropshot" and "Unthinkable" and lab-made infections are part of it (COVID-19 is called artificially created).
- Я2 objects that conflict historically merged small groups into bigger ones ("gatherers of the land": Ivan III,
  Genghis, Æthelstan, Louis the German), so globalisation may be natural evolution. Я1 answers with Darwin plus Lamarck:
  diversity is preserved in nature, Ashby rediscovered and proved the same law in cybernetics, and so any bid for world
  hegemony is a "perpetual motion machine" doomed by the laws of evolution.
- Ordinary people are as guilty. They forget outdoor toilets, carrying water from the well and public baths, and they
  grumble while living among the world's top 15% (in 73% of countries the standard of living is below Russia's, he
  says, citing UN statistics). A long Somalia tableau follows. Discontent with the authorities could bring the same
  banditry here.
- Childfree people: humanity is ~5 million years old, 25-year generations give **200,000 generations** of unbroken
  thread, and one brainwashed descendant cuts it. Religions call life for pleasure satanic. Lonely old age awaits.

The cure: be grateful, if not to God then to fate. Watch the scramble for power and money with a smile, like ants
around an apple core, and remember you are already at the summit. His wife comes back from a friend's ("Who are you
talking to?"). He waves to the mirror. Each half goes back to his own world, one into reality and the other into the
looking-glass. Footnote: the photo shows "that very feeder from our dacha".

### 4. On the liberal idea and liberal values (15.08.2025)

Summer terrace, coffee, birdsong. Я2: "this is what life is for". Я1: liberals make their "wants" the meaning of life.
- A **true goal of life is one worth dying for.** Pleasure fails this test, so it is a false goal. **Value is measured by
  the price paid for it**, and liberal "values" cost nothing and are infantile whims.
- Truth is not settled by vote. **The test of a belief is whether it helps survival.** The liberal idea is "mortal",
  because what does not strive to survive does not survive (the instinct of self-preservation as a selection effect).
- His examples are same-sex couples, childfree people, and couples who put off children for comfort. The richest
  countries have the lowest birth rates, so the cause is the ideology, not a lack of state support. He glances at the
  neighbour's plot: a Central Asian migrant labourer digging a cesspit has eight children, and the childfree owner has
  none. "Evolution will show objectively who was right."
- Humans who see extinction coming and keep going are worse than dinosaurs, who could not know: "social-suicidal
  tendencies".
- The alternative is an **idea of service**: duties over wants, continuing oneself into the future through children,
  each generation caring more for the next than for itself. "To chase your wants you don't need to be human, a monkey
  will do."

Ending: a cold wind and a cloud. Я1 says "let's strive for comfort, it's warmer indoors", a self-ironic jab at his own
thesis.

## The narrator and the author

As presented in the texts (the narrator is the author speaking directly, not a fictional character):
- Lives at least part of the year at a **dacha** in a gardening association (СНТ), all seasons. He sits through general
  meetings and knows the association's history: five crooked chairmen and then one honest, unpaid one. He feeds birds
  in winter and photographs the feeder. He is married, and his wife appears in passing.
- **Former true believer in Marxism and communism.** He was ready to die for it, and in the 1970s organised an
  underground cell against corruption and careerism in the system. Later came disillusionment ("the ideals were not
  worth believing in"). Details are in chapter 19 «Прозрение» of his documentary-mystical novella «Подпольщики».
- Spent **many years in places without birch trees**, and seeing birches again from a train made him weep.
- His **grandmother** told him how villagers fed the German prisoners who were rebuilding what they had destroyed.
- Remembers, or says his generation remembers, the rural Soviet way of life: outdoor toilets in hard frost, buckets
  from the well, public baths, water heated on the stove.
- Writes **songs and poems** (stihi.ru, «Куда уходит счастье», 2017) and links his texts to one another with footnotes.
- Has an acquaintance called Fyodor Sorokin (name as in the text), whom he treats as a model egoist.
- In these texts he **believes in God** (Я1 in essay 3; implied in essays 1 and 4). The sceptical non-believer is Я2,
  the other half of the same man.
- His political position in 2023–2025 is openly pro-Russian state and anti-American. He refers to the war in Donbas and
  to "defending, at the cost of many lives, the right of our country and of all humanity to exist".

## Worldview

- **Wants (хочу / не хочу, хотелки) against duty.** This is the axis of all four essays: pleasure against meaning,
  rights against obligations, comfort against children, freedom against social survival. He judges every social
  question by the balance between «хочу» and «нельзя», «не хочу» and «надо».
- **Survival as the criterion of truth.** "Practice is the criterion of truth" from the blog is now applied to values.
  An idea is correct if the society holding it survives. Evolution is the judge, "objectively".
- **Value = price.** The only real values and goals are those worth one's life: children, kin, country, love.
- **The collective above the individual,** and humanity above states. "Free but dead is worse than limited but alive."
- **Politics.** Democracy is "fantasy", an imitation that serves funders. Rotation of power is a "roulette" and an
  ideologeme. Despotism is not good in itself, but it is more effective and right for an immature society, and Peter I
  and Stalin are praised for results. The USSR's collapse was a disaster caused by "democrats". The US seeks hegemony;
  liberalism and childfree are "Western propaganda".
- **Moral conservatism.** Family and children are the purpose of marriage. Sex for pleasure is "fornication".
  Same-sex couples are "a deviation". Divorce and serial marriages come from ingratitude.
- **Love.** Love is transcendent and illogical and is the only source of happiness. Egoism and love exclude each other,
  and love can be learned by habit. Patriotism is love; nationalism is hatred; true love extends even to enemies.
- **Religion.** Я1 believes in God. Being drawn to God means being drawn to the continuation of life. God is also cited
  as an argument against democracy: an unelected "despot" with no term limits, whose believers call themselves "slaves of
  God".
- **Anti-dogmatism (declared).** Do not be a fanatic of any ideology, a lesson he took from his communist youth.
  Я2 points out that this too is an ideology, and the text leaves the point standing.
- **Gratitude.** Modern people, Russians included, are among the world's privileged few. Discontent is ingratitude, and
  it is dangerous because it opens the way to chaos.

## Links to the singularity blog

**Continuities**
- **Wants steer behaviour.** The blog's «хотелки» are still the main explanatory term. "Хочучелы" is a new coinage for
  people ruled by wants. The idea that reaching a want only resets the bar ("you get used to good things fast") matches
  the blog's account of the motivation treadmill.
- **The same logic at every scale.** Bird feeder → person → family → СНТ → state → humanity. The СНТ is used exactly
  like the blog's homely models: a small system that explains the large one ("now imagine the cooperative grew to the
  size of a country").
- **Systems science vocabulary.** Darwin plus Lamarck, Ashby's law of requisite variety, the "perpetual motion machine"
  and the law of energy conservation as an analogy for hegemony. Self-preservation instinct as survivorship.
- **Man is part of nature.** When Я2 says "people are not beasts", Я1 insists the laws of nature apply, the same move
  against human exceptionalism as in the blog.
- **Arithmetic on the knee:** 5 million years / 25 = 200,000 generations; top 15% of the world; 73% of countries;
  1 in 20 employed in Somalia.
- **Demographic decline in rich countries** is still central, as in post 11 and the SETI talk.
- **Hostility to the wish for immortality** ("I want to be immortal" is on the хочучела's list) matches the blog's view
  that human immortality would stop evolution.
- **Weapons able to destroy humanity many times over** and still being "improved" continue the blog's theme of
  self-destruction and mass aggression as instruments of apoptosis.
- **Socratic questioning about criteria** ("better by what criterion?") survives, especially in essay 1.

**Shifts and contradictions (2011–2012 → 2023–2025)**
- **AI and the technosphere have disappeared.** Four essays written as late as 2025, in the middle of the AI boom, never
  mention AI, robots, automation or the technosphere. Modern comforts (piped hot water, transport, fridges, computers)
  are listed as privileges to be grateful for. When Я2 says "we made all this with our own hands", Я1 does not
  answer with the blog's thesis that the technosphere creates more than 95% of value. The future of humanity is now a
  question of morals and reproduction, not of being displaced by technology.
- **Fatalism has become exhortation.** In the blog the demographic transition and short-sighted wants were nature's
  program (apoptosis), with "no villains, no remedies". Here low birth rates are blamed on liberal ideology and Western
  propaganda, and the cure is offered: the idea of service, gratitude, learning to love. This contradicts the blog's
  strict determinism and its line that "whoever scares you and sells salvation is a con man".
- **Villains have returned.** The blog explicitly rejected conspiracy and class explanations. Now there are named
  culprits (Obama, Brzezinski, "хочучелы", liberals, egoists such as Sorokin), Cold War plans, and COVID as a weapon.
  The tone toward opponents is harsher: "wounded in the brain", "possessed liberals", "infantile neurotics".
- **A softer anti-anthropocentrism.** Phrases such as "the high title of HUMAN", "our unique chosenness in nature" and
  "a monkey will do" sit awkwardly beside the blog's attack on human exceptionalism. They are moral claims, not claims
  about mind, but a reader of the blog will notice.
- **Religion.** The blog avoided religious framings and leaned on Christian ethics only quietly. Now Я1 declares faith
  in God and quotes religious morality ("satanism", "fornication"). The doubting half is the atheist.
- **Market and the state.** The blog was pro-market and called "social justice" a form of envy. The new texts praise
  despots who achieved big goals (Peter I, Stalin's industrialisation), and the communist past is treated as a
  sincere mistake, not as foolishness.
- **What has not changed:** survival of the system as the measure of right and wrong. In the blog this measure led to
  humanity being replaced. Here it leads to "have children and serve society". The concept of humanity's life cycle,
  which would make these appeals pointless, is not mentioned.

## Voice and devices

**The self-dialogue compared with the campfire dialogues**

| | Campfire dialogues (2011–2012) | Dialogues with myself (2023–2025) |
|---|---|---|
| Speakers | Andrey (teacher) and Oleg (pupil), two people | Я1 and Я2, two halves of one man; no names |
| Setting | Evenings by a fire, a series | Dacha terrace, rocking chair, coffee, sweets, feeder, mirror; one essay each |
| Who leads | Andrey asks more than he asserts and leads by questions | Я1 mostly asserts in long monologues. In essay 2 Я2 is the expounder |
| Opponent | Oleg objects but gradually learns and is praised | Я2 objects sharply ("демагогия", "you exaggerate as usual", "slave mentality") and is mostly cornered. He rarely concedes, and sometimes gets the last ironic word ("this principle is an ideology too") |
| Emotion | Calm, "Мрачновато. — Зато объективно." | Heated: CAPS, "???", Я1 "breathes fast", Я2 "jumps up". Indignation instead of serenity |
| Scope | One chain of reasoning across the series, with teasers | Self-contained essays, no teasers, cross-links by footnote to the novella and a song |
| Endings | Bold summary and "до завтра" | A domestic exit with self-irony: going in where it's warm "to strive for comfort", the wife's "who are you talking to?", the mirror waving back, an empty rocking chair still swaying |
| Self-awareness | Andrey is the author's mask | The form admits openly that the opponent is himself. The liberal is part of him, which softens the polemics a little |

**Recurring devices**
- **The nature-scene overture.** Each essay opens with a carefully seen scene of the dacha and its birds. The best is
  the bully chaffinch at the feeder, with the narrator's inner remarks to the birds ("You'll burst! Let others eat
  too!"). The scene becomes the parable for the argument.
- **The everyday model.** The СНТ meeting stands in for the state, the neighbour's trees for the conflict of interests,
  the sweet for the difference between pleasure and love, the child on the windowsill for necessary despotism.
- **Rhetorical traps.** He asks a question and then turns the answer back on the speaker: the 99% against 51% ballot,
  "would you die for pleasure?", "is anyone elected by family referendum to be the child?", "why does nobody worry
  about the Queen's term in office?".
- **Reductio by the opponent and a laugh.** Я2 mimics Я1's laugh. Я1 helps Я2 out of a rhetorical silence ("the
  question is rhetorical anyway").
- **Lists and accumulation:** the hardships of wild animals, the conveniences of modern life, the Somalia tableau,
  the chores of babies (teething, nappies, nursery). They run long, half documentary and half sermon.
- **Coinages and folk speech:** «хочучелы», «хотелки», «качать права», «пипл хавает», «лапша на уши», «сам не гам и
  другому не дам», «ей сто лет в обед», «надавать по рукам», «ранены в мозг».
- **Emphasis by capitals:** ЦЕНОЙ, ЦЕННОСТЬ, СЛУЖЕНИЯ, ДОЛЖНЫ, ЧЕЛОВЕКА, ПОКОЛЕНИЙ.
- **Pet terms:** "мортальный/мортальность" (mortal, death-bound) as the opposite of what survives; "инфантильный";
  "великовозрастные дети".
- **Self-irony at the close,** where the conclusion is undercut by a small act of comfort or by daily routine.
- **Hedges remain** ("по-моему", "я полагаю", "насколько я понимаю"), but they are rarer than in the blog, and there
  is more certainty.

## Use for the series

- **Biography for Andrey:** a 1970s communist true believer who led an underground cell and later saw through it. This
  explains his distrust of any "self-evident" ideology, the singularity included, and gives him a strong personal answer
  when Oleg accuses him of dogma ("I was like that myself"). Other details to use: the dacha and СНТ, winter
  bird-feeding, the wife calling him in, the birches seen from a train after years away, the grandmother's story about
  German prisoners, writing songs.
- **Bring back the self-dialogue as a device inside an evening.** Andrey can tell Oleg how he argues with himself, or
  an evening can open with Andrey "half-talking to his reflection". The Я1/Я2 split fits the blog's thesis that wants
  and reason pull against each other.
- **Use the feeder and the СНТ as homely models.** The bully chaffinch with a self-refilling hopper is a ready-made
  image for post-scarcity under the technosphere: plenty for all, yet wants still fight. The СНТ chairman story models
  incentives and governance. "Now scale it up to a country" is his own move.
- **Add new vocabulary:** «хочучелы», «мортальный», «великовозрастные дети», "value = price", "a true goal is one worth
  dying for", "survival as the criterion of truth". These follow naturally from the blog's wants-and-life-cycle logic.
- **Let Oleg be sharper,** as Я2 is: "demagoguery", "you exaggerate as usual", "your principle is an ideology too".
  The 2023–2025 author is used to an opponent who refuses to give in.
- **Endings:** keep the series' bold summary and "до завтра", but add a small self-ironic domestic beat, such as going
  in where it's warm, the fire dying down, or a call to dinner.
- **Bridge the gap between the blog and the essays.** He has not written about AI since 2012. A new evening could ask
  how the 2025 theses (demographic decline from comfort, "chase your wants and a monkey will do", gratitude for comfort)
  read in light of the blog's apoptosis: is the "liberal" turn toward comfort itself nature's program, made possible by
  the technosphere? That reconciles determinism with the moral appeal, and Oleg can press the contradiction.
- **Handle with care:** the essays' sharp current-politics content (Zelensky, Obama, COVID as a lab weapon, same-sex
  couples as "deviation", praise of Stalin) sits outside the singularity chain and goes against the blog's "no villains"
  stance. Use the underlying argument patterns (criteria, scale models, survival test), not the partisan targets, unless
  the series deliberately shows the author's later evolution.
- **Tone:** allow more heat than in 2011–2012 (CAPS, "???", a sharp "Ты так на самом деле полагаешь?") at moments of
  real conviction, then cool down with humour.
