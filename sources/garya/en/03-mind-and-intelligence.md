# 03. Mind and Intelligence — notes

## Summary
Andrey opens by noting that reference sources, Wikipedia for example, define intelligence as a property of "individuals", that is, of humans only, which rules out finding it in anything like Solaris. Against "thinking is a function of the brain" he uses the Venus flytrap: without nerves, muscles or a digestive system it senses prey selectively, snaps shut in a fraction of a second, digests, reopens slowly, and skips digestion when the insect has escaped. Saying "thinking is a function *only* of the brain" confuses form with content, just as "movement is a function only of muscles" would. Mind is the ability to find cause-and-effect links and use them for one's own goals by acting ahead of events, and plants have it to a degree. So the split into "reasonable" and "unreasonable" is itself unreasonable. There are degrees: a fly beats against a window pane, a cat goes around it, a human goes further. Logical behaviour alone is not mind, though: Deep Blue beat Kasparov at chess but not in politics or business. He therefore coins "domain logicality" (ареал-логичность), comparable within one domain through competition, and "aggregate logicality", the union over all domains, as the measure of the degree of mind. He lists six factors behind it (stored knowledge, recognition speed, analysis speed, analysis depth, execution speed, synthesis speed) and illustrates each with chess players. Only a measuring system at least as logical as the systems being compared can compare them, so a mind higher than ours may be right under our nose and look illogical to us. Of three meanings of "thinking", he settles on the process that passes through a "point of attention": inner speech, sequential and limited to one thought at a time, while the brain's neural network works massively in parallel as a hierarchy of pattern recognizers. Intelligence is the potential, or the limit, of mind set by the physical platform: memory, speeds, model. Twins raised apart (one as a Mowgli) share intelligence but reach different degrees of mind. Memory is catastrophically scarce: about 10^15 synapses, each one elementary cause-effect link, so synapse count is the rough measure of intelligence. Artificial intelligence therefore already exists since the first trainable neural nets and is growing. The fixed conclusion: mind and intelligence come in degrees.

## Key theses
1. Defining intelligence as a property of humans only is anthropocentric. Function must not be confused with a particular organ (form vs content).
2. Mind is the ability to find cause-and-effect links and use them for one's own goals through anticipatory behaviour. Plants (the Venus flytrap) have it to a non-zero degree.
3. Mind is not binary. The fly, the cat and the human differ by degree, and none of them is at zero.
4. Domain logicality is comparable only within one domain, through competition. Aggregate logicality over all domains characterizes the degree of mind and cannot be measured reliably across different domains.
5. Six factors determine domain logicality: knowledge in memory; speed and accuracy of recognition; speed of analysis; depth of analysis; speed of physical execution; speed of synthesis of new links. The result is victory in competition: maximum gain at minimum resources in limited time.
6. A system can only assess logicality up to its own level. A higher mind may exist "right under the nose" and look simply illogical to us.
7. Human thinking in the narrow sense (through the "point of attention") is sequential inner speech, one thought at a time. The bulk of the brain does parallel hierarchical pattern recognition, so the "we use 10% of the brain" claim is wrong.
8. Intelligence is the potential (or limit) of mind set by the platform. Mind is how much of it is realized (twins vs Mowgli; supervised learning vs self-learning).
9. Memory is critically scarce. Images carry "significance coefficients" and weak ones are erased, which is why a Mowgli cannot learn to speak later.
10. Synapse count (about 10^15 in a human) is a rough measure of a neural net's intelligence. For nets on the same model, compare synapse signals per unit time.
11. AI has existed since the first trainable artificial neural networks. Its degree of intelligence is still small but growing fast.
12. **Dividing systems into reasonable and unreasonable, or into having and not having intelligence, is incorrect: mind and intelligence come in degrees.**

## Staging and devices
- Opening: Andrey grins that reading sources on the subject you inevitably "step into cow-pats of anthropocentrism", which is a callback to the previous night. There is no fire scene in the opening lines.
- Central example: the Venus flytrap, with an animated image of the trap closing. Details: fast closing, slow opening, no wasted digestion on escaped prey, selective response (not to raindrops).
- Other examples: flowers closing before rain, tendrils exploring space, leaves following the sun; a fly against glass vs a cat; Deep Blue vs Kasparov; chess players illustrating each of the six factors (openings known, poor eyesight, slow thinking, depth 2 vs 4 moves, a paralysed player, inventing the "fork" mid-game); identical twins and a Mowgli; walking as controlled cyclic falling forward; "brain resources are like money".
- Pun: dividing things into reasonable and unreasonable is "unreasonable".
- Oleg's objections and quips: he cannot imagine thinking without a brain; a plant's scope of causes is orders of magnitude smaller; is logic the same as mind?; too many parameters; footballers vs cooks and "deep slow-thinkers vs shallow fast-thinkers"; "what a horror that we can't detect what doesn't exist"; the flytrap doesn't think like me; we only use a small percent of the brain; "so one can be as reasonable as an oak and as intelligent as Einstein?" (Andrey: exactly).
- Andrey concedes points readily (yes, orders of magnitude less; no, the flytrap doesn't think like a human) and then shows that the concession does not save the binary.
- Ending: "enough for today". The main thought is stated as a formula. Next time: the history of AI systems and how neural nets work in more detail.

## Terms

|---|---|---|
| разум | mind (also "reason") | the ability to identify cause-and-effect links and use them for one's goals; present in degrees everywhere |
| разумность, степень разумности | degree of mind (sapience) | how much mind a system actually realizes; measured by aggregate logicality |
| интеллект | intelligence | the *potential* (or the *limit*) of mind set by the system's physical platform |
| мышление | thinking | in his preferred narrow sense: processing that passes through the "point of attention"; sequential inner speech |
| точка внимания | point of attention | the narrow serial channel of conscious processing |
| ареал-логичность | domain logicality | logicality within one field of activity, comparable through competition |
| совокупная логичность | aggregate logicality | union of all domain logicalities; characterizes the degree of mind |
| причинно-следственные связи | cause-and-effect links | the basic unit of knowledge and of mind |
| упреждающее поведение | anticipatory behaviour | observable sign of mind: acting on a forecast |
| иерархия систем распознавания образов | hierarchy of pattern-recognition systems | his model of the brain: micro-images → objects → systems; sounds → phonemes → words → meaning |
| микрообраз | micro-image | what a single neuron recognizes |
| коэффициент значимости | significance coefficient | weight that decides whether a memory survives or is erased |
| обучение с учителем / самообучение | supervised learning / self-learning | learning from accumulated human knowledge vs alone (Mowgli) |
| синтез | synthesis | discovering new cause-effect links |
| синапс | synapse | holds one elementary cause-effect link; counting them estimates intelligence |
| маугли | Mowgli (feral child) | case of the same intelligence realized as much less mind |

## Voice and personality
- Engineering mindset: mind is decomposed into measurable parameters (memory, speeds, depth, bit depth, platform), with computing vocabulary (computational power, memory capacity, dynamic range, "platform").
- Coins terms when existing ones carry bias (ареал-логичность, совокупная логичность). He defines carefully and admits the limits of measurement.
- Dissolves binaries into continua: the master move of the series so far.
- Concrete, everyday illustrations: chess, a fly at the window, walking. He likes to show hidden complexity in something trivial (walking as computation).
- Confident, provocative claims stated calmly ("AI was created long ago").
- Playful: puns, and he lets Oleg score witty lines without losing the thread.
- A connectionist worldview: the brain as a hierarchical pattern-recognition net, with memory scarcity as the central constraint.
- Characteristic quotes:
  - «Заглядывая в различные источники по такой теме, неизбежно наступаешь в лепехи антропоцентризма»
