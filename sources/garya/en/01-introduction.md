# 01. Introduction — notes

## Summary
A short programmatic preface, not yet a dialogue. The author says the blog is about the technological singularity, which he calls an especially important question for humanity, and tied to IT because IT is at the front edge of scientific and technical progress. He claims the topic bears directly on the personal fate of most readers, and that many people, the world's scientific elite among them, cannot judge the consequences of what is already happening; the first thing he will explain is *why* that judgement fails. He warns that the conclusions will feel unexpected, even absurd, and will provoke protest. He stresses that they shocked him too and that he did not like them, but after weighing arguments for and against he decided they are probably right. His motive: humanity faces harder times than any in its history, and he wants to help those who can "take the veil off their eyes" to prepare and survive. The material grew out of a long forum thread where the arguments were sharpened; he thanks both opponents and supporters. He explains the format (a semi-literary dialogue between his supporter and his opponent), the cumulative order (each article rests on the conclusions of earlier ones), and asks readers to be patient: many questions will be answered only in later parts, and in the comments he will often just say so.

## Key theses
1. The technological singularity is the most important question facing humanity, and it concerns the reader personally, not some abstract future.
2. Even top scientists misjudge the consequences; the reasons for this blindness must be explained first (this becomes anthropocentrism, article 02, and the human psyche, article 08).
3. The conclusions are unpleasant and counter-intuitive, even for the author; they are offered to those who prefer bitter truth to sweet lies.
4. The method is a strictly cumulative chain of logic: each article is a building block for the next, and readers are invited to point out logical gaps.
5. **People are heading into extremely hard times, the hardest in human history; understanding what is happening and why may help some of them prepare and survive.**

## Staging and devices
- Not a fireside scene yet: a direct address to the reader in the first person.
- Credibility move: the author presents himself as someone who was shocked by his own conclusions and accepted them reluctantly, not as an enthusiast.
- Pre-emptive patience requests ("don't let thoughts outrun logic"), plus advance notice that in the comments he will answer "this will be covered later".
- Explains why he chose dialogue: easier to take in, and he can voice opponents' arguments himself, promising not to make his own side sound biased.
- Ends with the series title in capitals, "ДИАЛОГ У КОСТРА" (Dialogue by the Campfire), and a signature, "Андрей Гордиенко". The protagonist of the dialogues is called Andrey, so the author's voice character shares his first name.

Tone: brief and polite. He accepts the advice but immediately marks his conceptual distance.

## Voice and personality
- Sense of mission and moral seriousness: he writes "sincerely, as a person who cannot be indifferent" to humanity's fate.
- Builds trust by admitting his own shock and dislike of his conclusions; truth is presented as something he submitted to, not something he wanted.
- He frames his readers as the few who can face bitter truth, which is a mildly prophetic, separating stance.
- Strong commitment to formal logic and sequence; he invites correction of logical errors and treats objections as useful (he thanks his forum opponents).
- Habitual hedge "Я полагаю" ("I believe"), used again and again; a measured, slightly formal register.
- Characteristic quotes:
  - «Я намерен их озвучить для тех, кто предпочитает горькую правду сладкой лжи, привести доказательства и обоснования.»
  - «Многие выводы, к которым я пришел, стали очень большой неожиданностью для меня самого, они мне не понравились и привели меня в состояние шока»
  - «Однако, я не считаю глобальный экономический кризис результатом чьей-либо авантюры.» (comment)
