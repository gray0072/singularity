# The GF2045 international congress — notes

## Summary
A short informational post outside the dialogue series, not a continuation of the articles. The author reports on the "Global Future 2045" congress held in Moscow on 11 February 2012. He finds it a little strange that world-famous scientists do not see what his blog is about, and takes this as further proof that he was right to start the blog: people must know what awaits them. He singles out Alexander Panov's talk as containing the most serious objection to his concept and promises to answer it in the next article. He apologises that a "crazy rush" at work leaves him little time for the blog, and embeds the three talks he found most interesting: Ray Kurzweil (in English), Alexander Panov and Vladimir Pirozhkov.

## Key theses
1. Leading scientists at GF2045 do not see the danger the blog describes — the technosphere displacing humanity.
2. That blindness confirms the need for the blog: people must be told what is coming.
3. Panov's talk contains the most serious objection to the author's concept; it deserves a dedicated answer.
4. **The post itself highlights: this is only an announcement; the real answer to Panov will come in the next article.**
