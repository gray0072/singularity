# Source material

Material for writing new articles and translating existing ones. Not published content of the series.

- [`garya/`](garya/) — everything by Andrey Gordienko (Garya), the author of the original articles 01–15:
  - [`sqlru/`](garya/sqlru/) — the source: his blog on sql.ru, every post as published, with the comments under it
    (Russian originals; the author's comments in full, readers' shortened as context). sql.ru closed in 2022, so
    these pages and the [web archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20181022050755/http://www.sql.ru/blogs/garya/)
    are the only copies; addresses on sql.ru are kept as plain text;
  - [`proza/`](garya/proza/) — his later prose from proza.ru: «Подпольщики» and «Диалоги с самим собой» (Russian);
  - [`en/`](garya/en/) — English analysis: one note per post, notes on the prose, and
    [`author-profile.md`](garya/en/author-profile.md), the synthesis of his worldview, logic and voice.

- [`continuation-notes.md`](continuation-notes.md) — the record of the continuation 16–30, which was written for
  this repository and not by the author.

The originals are kept in Russian exactly as written and imported by the scripts in [`tools/`](../tools/)
(see [SPEC.md](../SPEC.md), sections 5–6).
