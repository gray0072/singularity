# Source material

Material for writing new articles and translating existing ones. Not published content of the series.

- [`garya/`](garya/) — everything by Andrey Gordienko (Garya), the author of the original articles 01–15:
  - [`sqlru/`](garya/sqlru/) — the source: his blog on sql.ru, every post as published, with all the comments under
    it in full, the author's and the readers' (Russian originals), and the surviving pages of the forum thread the
    blog grew out of. sql.ru closed in 2022, so these pages and the
    [web archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20181022050755/http://www.sql.ru/blogs/garya/) are the only copies;
    addresses on sql.ru are kept as plain text;
  - [`proza/`](garya/proza/) — his later prose from proza.ru: *The Underground Men* and *Dialogues with Myself* (in Russian);
  - [`en/`](garya/en/) — English analysis: one note per post, notes on the prose, and
    [`author-profile.md`](garya/en/author-profile.md), the synthesis of his worldview, logic and voice.

- [`continuation-notes.md`](continuation-notes.md) — the record of the continuation 16–32, which was written for
  this repository by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic) in the author's style, and not by the author.

- [`next-evenings.ru.md`](next-evenings.ru.md) — a proposal for the next cycle of evenings (33–66), drafted in
  Russian for the keeper by Claude Fable 5.2 (Anthropic). It is a plan, not part of the series; the English version
  is made once the list is agreed.

The originals are kept in Russian exactly as written and imported by the scripts in [`tools/`](../tools/)
(see [SPEC.md](../SPEC.md), sections 5–6).
