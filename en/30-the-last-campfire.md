# The Last Campfire

<!-- import:pending -->

> The final evening. The fire is burning down, and Andrey and Oleg sum up the whole chain from anthropocentrism to the technosphere. The technogenic life that takes humanity's place will have its own life cycle and its own limit, and it too will one day give way. The evolution of matter does not end with us or with it. What is left for a human being who has understood this?
