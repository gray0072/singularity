# Who Sets the Tasks

<!-- import:pending -->

> In his answer to Penrose, Andrey argued that the essence of mind is setting tasks, not solving them. Today's AI agents break goals into subgoals and choose their own next steps. Where do their goals come from, and where do ours? If wants set human tasks, what plays the role of wants for a machine?
