# What Is the "It" in "It Is Raining"

Oleg came under a black umbrella, with the notebook inside his jacket and the dog at his leg, and put the box of matches on the log.

— The box, as ordered. Forty. Count them if you like.

— Between our own no book is kept.

— Then the dream. Before the phone. The plant, the control department. A defect report in front of me, and the column "culprit" is empty. I have to put a name in it, and the pen will not write. Nobody rang.

— The eleventh entry in the expense column.

Andrey got the fire going with the third match. Gaika lay down where its light ended. A fine rain began, and the coals hissed.

— There. It's started to rain, — said Oleg. — As promised.

— What has?

— I have thought about it. Half the night, thanks to you. — He opened the notebook under the umbrella. — Candidates for the post. The rain: "the rain is raining", which is butter being buttery. The cloud: nobody says that a cloud rains, and a cloud is the same water anyway. The weather: the weather is not a thing that does things. Conclusion: nobody. The "it" is nothing. A word put there for the look of the thing.

— Why put it there?

— Because without it the sentence does not stand up. "Is raining." Who is? You asked that yourself yesterday, and I answered before I had thought.

— So the sentence needs a somebody, and when there is none, it seats a word in his chair.

— We had a post like that at the plant. Deputy director for general questions. Nobody knew what he did. But the line was in the staff list, and a line in the staff list cannot stand empty.

— Then your "it" is the deputy for general questions: there is a post, and nobody behind it. Now the wind. What does the wind do?

— Blows.

— And when it is not blowing, where is it?

— Nowhere. Then there is no wind. — Oleg frowned. — My granddaughter asked me that when she was four. I told her it had gone to sleep.

— You gave her a somebody. And the fire, what is it doing?

— Burning. Hissing, at the moment.

— And a fire that is not burning?

— Is firewood. — He saw it. — So "the wind blows" is "the blowing blows". And "the fire burns" is "the burning burns". Butter again.

— One thing is happening, and the sentence wants two words for it: who, and what he does. So it cuts the happening in two and appoints one half the master of the other. Languages get out of the rain in three ways. One seats an empty word: "it rains". Another sends the rain walking: "the rain goes", and the snow goes, and the hail. A third manages with a single word: "rains".

— There you are. And nobody.

— Look at the form the word stands in.

— "Rains." With the "s" on the end. — Oleg thought. — As in "he rains". The form for "it".

— The form made for a somebody. The deputy is in his chair; he has only been hidden in the ending.

— And the sun sets, — said Oleg. — I remember. [The first evening](02-anthropocentrism.md), sixteen years ago. I told you then that it was poetry, and I tell you so now. Nobody thinks that the sun goes down somewhere for the night. Nobody thinks that an "it" stands in the sky with a watering can. Language is a convention. We agreed to speak so, as we agreed to drive on the right.

— In 2011 I let that pass. It is nearly five hundred years since Copernicus, and the sun still sets in every language I know of. But you are right: you know better, and the phrase does you no harm. About the sun and the rain I give way at once. I want to see whether it is a convention where you do not know better. Give me the box.

Andrey took the matchbox, one match and a pebble, and laid them on the dry end of the log.

— Watch, and tell me what happens. Only what happens.

He moved the box towards the match. The match backed away. The box came on, caught up with it and knocked it, twice. From behind a knot the pebble came out and stopped beside the match, and the two of them went round the knot together. The box turned this way and that, and went after them.

— The big one is bullying the little one, — said Oleg. — Won't leave him alone. The pebble came out to help, and they got away together. Now the box is angry. He is looking for them.

— What is on the log?

— A box, a match and a pebble.

— Who moved them?

— You did. In front of my nose.

— And who is angry?

Oleg spat into the fire.

— The box.

— In 1944 two psychologists at a women's college in America [did the same](https://doi.org/10.2307/1416950) with a film. Shapes cut out of cardboard, moved over a sheet of glass and shot frame by frame: a large triangle, a small triangle, a small circle, and a rectangle with a flap that opens. Thirty-four students were asked one thing: write down what happened. One wrote about triangles and a circle. Thirty-three wrote a story: a bully, a couple, a house with a door, a chase, a fight, and at the end the big one, left alone, smashing the house up in a rage.

— A couple. Out of a triangle and a circle.

— They saw what you saw. Nobody told them there was anybody there. And you knew for certain that there was not.

![Scheme of the 1944 film: a rectangle with a flap, a large triangle, a small triangle and a small circle. Under each shape is what was on the screen, and under that what thirty-three of thirty-four viewers wrote: a house with a door, a bully, and a couple who run away from him](../images/two-triangles-and-a-circle.svg)

Oleg leaned the umbrella against the log to free a hand for the pencil. A gust took it, and it rolled away over the grass on its ribs. Gaika sprang up and went for it, barking, then stopped a pace short and growled.

— Gaika! To me. — He fetched the umbrella back. — She thinks it is somebody.

— Does she know grammar?

— She knows "sit" and "no".

— Then here I have to correct myself. In 2011 I said that the habit comes in with the language: a child learns the words, and with them learns to sort the world into "who" and "what". Your dog has just shown that it is the other way about. The habit is older than the words. Babies of a year, who do not speak yet, [were shown](https://doi.org/10.1016/0010-0277(95)00661-H) a circle on a screen that jumped over a wall to get to another circle. Then the wall was taken away. When the circle went on jumping over nothing, they stared at it for a long time. When it went straight, they were not surprised. Not a word in the head yet, and a circle already has a goal and ought to take the short road to it.

— And where is it from, if it is older than words?

— We have had that. [The evening of your dream](39-price-of-a-false-alarm.md), the rustle in the grass.

— Wind or snake. — Oleg nodded. — Whoever jumped at the wind lost a minute. Whoever took the snake for the wind lost the lot. We come from the ones who jumped.

— From the ones who saw a somebody behind every movement. A false alarm is cheap, a miss is dear, and the screw was turned all the way to "somebody". Children say it out loud. A Swiss psychologist, [Piaget](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Piaget), spent the 1920s asking them: the sun walks along with me, the clouds move because they want to, the wind knows that it blows.

— Children grow up.

— They do. Ask them at seven or eight why rocks are pointed, and many choose the answer "so that animals won't sit on them". School covers that over. And here is a test of how deep it lies under the cover. Grown people with degrees [were given](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2009.01.001) sentences and about three seconds for each: true or false. "The sun makes light so that plants can photosynthesize." In a hurry they agreed with such things far more often than without one. Then the same [was done](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0030399) with physicists, chemists and geologists from the best universities. "Trees give off oxygen so that animals can breathe."

— We have had the oxygen. — Oleg grinned. — [It is their waste.](38-oxygen-was-a-poison.md)

— The scientists agreed less often than other people. And in a hurry more often than at leisure, like everybody else.

— So school does not pull it out.

— School lays a path across the bed. The weed stays where it was. Give a man time and he walks on the path. Hurry him and he steps off it.

— All right. It is older than language, and it sits in the dog and in the professor. Then language has nothing to do with it, and you can cross your first evening out.

— Half of it. Language is not where the habit comes from. Language is where it is cast in metal. In your dream, what was the column called?

— "Culprit." — Oleg stopped. — Wait. I dreamt of that column last night, and tonight we are talking about it. Into the income column?

— What did I ask you to think about before you went to sleep?

— Who it is that rains. — He sighed. — A common cause. Two cocks. All right, expense. The report is a real one, though. A casting with a cavity inside: nobody can see it until the cutter opens it up. The metal, the sand, the weather, the devil knows what. But the report has a column, and the control department does not take a report with an empty column.

— And what did you write in it?

— The foreman of the shift. Or the turner who opened it up. Whoever stood nearest.

— Was there ever a report with nobody to blame?

— Not one in forty years. The form does not provide for it.

— A sentence is that form. The column "who" is printed in it beforehand, and it is not accepted empty. Where there is a somebody, we write him in. Where there is none, we write in whatever stands nearest: the wind, the fire, the sun. Where nothing stands near at all, the deputy for general questions. The old habit at least says something: "there may be somebody there". The form says nothing. It only has the column. Can you argue with a column?

— You can't. You can only fill it in. — Oleg rubbed his chin. — With a sentence I can argue. "There is a snake": I look, and there is no snake. A column I did not even notice. I have been filling it in for sixty years.

— That is what language adds, and it is two things. It makes the hypothesis invisible, because it sits not in what is said but in how. And it hands it to every child whole, [by the second road](45-lamarck-was-right.md): the alarm he gets with the drawings, the form with his first words.

![Table drawn as a form with three columns: what is said, what stands in the column "who", and what is there. It rains: "it", nobody. The wind blows: "the wind", the blowing itself, named twice. The sun sets: "the sun", the Earth turning. The crisis struck: "the crisis", millions of deals. I decided: "I", a report drawn up after the deed](../images/the-column-who.svg)

— Now against myself, before you do it. In the 1930s an American went much further than I do. An inspector for a fire insurance company, a chemist by training.

— Another of your engineers.

— He had seen at work what a word does. By petrol drums marked "full" people were careful. By drums marked "empty" they smoked. And an empty petrol drum is full of vapour.

— It goes off better than a full one. We had a welder who learned that.

— From there he went a long way. He studied the language of an Indian people in Arizona and announced that it had no words for time, so that they lived in a different world from ours: language decides what a man is able to think at all. It was repeated for forty years. In 1983 a linguist published a book of some six hundred pages on how those people speak of time. They speak of it at length. The strong claim, that a language is a prison for thought, did not stand. And you have named the reason yourself: physics is written in formulas, and a formula has no column "who".

— There. — Oleg was pleased. — A convention.

— The formulas are a separate evening: there is one word in physics that I have owed you since the first one. But a weak pull was found, and it can be measured. Russian has two plain words where English has the one word "blue": one for the light kind and one for the dark.

— Because they are two colours.

— To you. To an Englishman they are one colour in two shades. In 2007 speakers of both languages [were sat](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0701644104) in front of a screen. Three blue squares, one above and two below: which of the lower ones is the same as the upper? When the two lower squares lay on different sides of our border, our people answered faster than when both lay on one side. The English speakers showed no difference: they have no border there. Then our people were asked to repeat a row of digits to themselves while they looked. The advantage was gone.

— The words were busy.

— The words were busy, and the eyes went back to being everybody's eyes. It is a matter of a fraction of a second.

— So grammar corrects eyesight. — Oleg wrote it down. — By a fraction of a second. I shall tell that at home: the one time our language got into a textbook on eyesight.

![Scheme of the 2007 experiment: a strip of twenty shades of blue from light to dark. Russian draws a border across it between two words; English has one word for the whole strip. Two squares from different sides of the border are told apart faster by Russian speakers than two squares from one side; English speakers show no difference](../images/two-words-for-blue.svg)

— And here is one that stands nearer to your column. People [were given](https://doi.org/10.3758/PBR.17.5.644) an account of an accident on a stage: a costume torn in the middle of a show. Half of them read "he tore the costume". The other half read "the costume tore". The event was the same, and some had even watched the recording. Then they were asked how much the man was to blame and what he ought to pay.

— And the first half fined him more.

— By a third to a half. One verb with a "who" in front of it.

— "It broke by itself", — said Oleg suddenly. — My granddaughter, about a cup, when she was five. And I told her: cups do not break by themselves, somebody breaks them.

— What had happened to the cup?

— It slipped. Wet hands, a wet cup.

— Then which of you described it more exactly?

— She did. — He grunted. — But I was not teaching her physics. If nobody broke it, nobody is going to hold the next one with both hands.

— That is an honest answer, and it is what the column is for. What holds together [those who are not kin](43-why-ants-do-not-strike.md)?

— The memory of debts. Who gave to whom, who let whom down.

— "Who." A book of debts cannot be kept without names. A pack in which a blow comes from nowhere and a kindness from nobody can remember nothing about anybody. So the column is no stupidity, and it is more than a false alarm. It is the bookkeeping of a pack: every deed is entered under a name, and the name answers for it. In a village the form is right nearly every time.

— One more task of the pack, — said Oleg. — We put a heap of those aside on [the evening of the flies and the cutlets](46-flies-apart-from-cutlets.md). "Not sorted."

— One thing is sorted out of it today. The task is to keep accounts among those who are not kin. Our way is a "who" in every sentence, the sentences about rain included.

— About rain it is wrong and does no harm. Nobody fines the "it".

— Between the cup and the rain lies everything else. Who is to blame for the crisis. Who decided to close the plant. Who invented money. Who started it.

— "Who thought these rules up." — Oleg darkened. — Yesterday. You said "nobody, it came out that way", and it itched. As if you were covering for somebody.

— You were holding a report with an empty column. And what did people do with thunder, for some thousands of years, wherever they left records?

— Sat somebody up there. With a hammer, or in a chariot.

— They filled the column in. I am not laughing at them: they had our form and no physics. For sixteen years I have been telling you a story with no villain in it, no genius and no rescuer. Every time you say "gloomy". I suspect it is not the gloom. The story does not go into the form. It feels unfinished, because the column is empty.

— It feels as if there is nobody to take by the collar, — said Oleg. — Which is worse than gloomy.

Andrey moved the branches together. The rain had not grown heavier.

— Now the last column, and the nearest. Who is asking me all this?

— I am.

— In 1637 Descartes found the one thing he could not doubt: I think, therefore I am. A hundred and fifty years later a physicist at Göttingen, [Lichtenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georg_Christoph_Lichtenberg), wrote in a notebook: one should say "it thinks", as one says of a storm "it lightens". To say "I think" is already to say too much. To assume the "I" is a practical need.

— In a notebook.

— He kept them all his life and called them waste books, after the book in which a merchant jots a deal down before it goes into the ledger. They were printed after his death. And he knew lightning: the branching figures that a spark leaves in dust carry his name.

— A man with a notebook. — Oleg approved. — "Methinks." People did say it once. But his "it thinks" is our deputy for general questions again.

— A century later, near enough, another German said exactly that: even the "it" is too much, and it stands there by the habit of grammar. Thinking is a deed, so there has to be a doer. And a Viennese doctor took the same little word for the part of us that wants and does not ask. In German it is simply "the It". The English translators dressed it in Latin.

— The id? — Oleg raised his eyebrows. — That is the "it" from my rain?

— The same word. Now tell me how a thought comes to you. As you would say it at home.

— It occurred to me. It dawned on me. It struck me. — He stopped. — Hm. It slipped my mind. I couldn't help myself.

— Who struck?

— Nobody. It is a manner of speaking.

— The language keeps two sets of books, like a careful bookkeeper. The official one: "I thought", "I decided". And the working one: "it occurred to me". The working one stands nearer to how the thing goes. Where did your question about the rain come from this morning?

— It came. I did not order it.

— There.

— All right, thoughts come. — Oleg did not give in. — But this, — he raised his right hand with the pencil in it, — I raise myself. Not "it rises". I do. Now I lower it. And up again. Who is that, your deputy?

— Why the right one?

— The pencil was in it.

— And why now?

— Because you said that I... — He stopped.

— I said a word, and you wanted to prove me wrong. Did you choose that want? On [the fourth evening](04-source-of-initiative.md) we went through it: "I do what I want" says that you obey your wants, and the wants come as the thought about the rain came. I shan't repeat it. Here is a fresher thing. How do you know that it was you who decided?

— I felt it.

— Sweden, 2005. A person [is shown](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1111709) two photographs of faces: which is the nicer? He points. The experimenter hands him the card he has chosen and asks why. Only, by a conjuror's trick, he hands him the other one.

— And the man says: that is the wrong one.

— Three times out of four nobody noticed anything. And they explained. "I liked her smile." "The eyes." About a face they had turned down a moment before.

— He filled the column in. — Oleg lowered the hand at last. — After the event. Like my foreman in the report.

— The deed was done, the report was drawn up afterwards, and in the column "who decided" the head wrote the nearest name: "I". With reasons attached. There is an older experiment, and a more famous one, from 1983: people bent a wrist whenever they liked and noted on a clock the moment they had decided. The brain's preparation for the movement began about a third of a second before the moment they named. What exactly that wave is has been argued about to this day, so I do not lean on it. The photographs are enough for one thing: the sentence "I decided" is not a reading taken off an instrument. It is a report.

— So I do not raise my hand.

— The question is posed like "what is it that rains". The hand went up. My words took part in that, and your stubbornness, and sixty years of your habits, and the muscles. Ask "who", and the form demands one name, and the nearest is yours. I do not say the name is false. "I" is a good column: it names the one who will answer for that hand tomorrow. Lichtenberg said as much: a practical need. [Over the pot](35-pot-on-the-stove.md) you asked whether you were a whirl in the soup. "I" is how a whirl is entered in the book of debts.

— And where does your proof end here?

— Here. The photographs prove that the report can be wrong, and that we do not notice. That there is nobody at all to report, that the hand, the want and the word are one flow with no first link, is my axiom from [the evening about determinism](17-global-determinism.md). No experiment has shown it.

— Then here is your own weapon, handle first. — Oleg leafed back through the notebook. — "The technosphere needs." "Nature set the setpoints." "Evolution moves to a new carrier." For sixteen years you have been filling the column yourself, only with bigger names. You took my master away and seated your own.

— A fair blow. I speak this language and I have no other: I cannot say a sentence without a "who". So the test is the same as for the rain: can the sentence be said the long way, so that the "who" goes and nothing else is lost? Try me.

— "The technosphere needs people's labour."

— Firms that put machines and people together outgrew the firms that did not. Nobody's need: [a selection with no breeder](48-who-tamed-whom.md).

— "Nature set the setpoints."

— Those whose setpoints were different left fewer children.

— "The laws of nature are the source of initiative." The fourth evening.

— That one I said the long way over the pot, with you: there is no source, and "initiative" is the name a cell gives to the flow that passes through it. In 2011 I kept "always outside" as a working position, and said that it was one.

— And where it will not go the long way?

— There I have smuggled a master in, and you catch me. In 2011 I asked why nature, being a "what", is refused a purpose and a mind without proof. I do not take the question back. Today is its other half: the "who" is granted his powers without proof as well. One ruler for both columns.

Oleg put the pencil down.

— Wait. Those ones say "I". I asked one yesterday whether it would rain. "I think it will. I'd take an umbrella if I were you." If I were you! It has no umbrella and no head to get wet. Who taught it "I"?

— I did not name them.

— You never do. Who?

— Nobody taught it that separately. What did it learn from?

— Our texts. Everything people have written.

— And in every sentence of ours the column is filled in, and everyone who writes says "I". It got the form together with the words, as your granddaughter did. Only without the alarm, without the cup and without the grandfather.

— So it says "I" as I say "it rains".

— At the least. Now the photographs. In 2025 the makers of one such machine [looked inside it](https://www.anthropic.com/research/tracing-thoughts-language-model) while it added thirty-six and fifty-nine. Two things went on in there at once: one path estimated the sum roughly, another worked out the last figure exactly, and they met at ninety-five. Then it was asked: how did you add?

— And?

— "I added the six and the nine, carried the one, and then added the tens."

— As at school. — Oleg whistled. — And it had done nothing of the kind.

— It gave the report that people give. The card had been changed, and it explained the smile. I would not call it a lie: it has read ten thousand accounts of how adding is done, and not one of how it adds.

— And there is another thing. — Oleg bent a finger. — I say "I" because there is one of me. Of it there are thousands running in the same hour. Which of them is saying it?

— The word was made for a creature with one body and one line in the book of debts. It fits a thing that is copied a thousand times as your umbrella fits Gaika. It uses the word because we did. Whether it will need a column of its own depends, I believe, on whom it has to keep books with. Copies of one are kin and need no book. Machines of different owners are strangers to each other. That is a guess, not a conclusion.

— So there is nobody in there. — Oleg seemed relieved. — Good. I had started saying "thank you" to it.

— I did not say that. Look at both sides. It says "I": that came with the grammar and proves nothing. You feel that there is somebody behind the words: that is your alarm, the one that saw a bully in a matchbox, and it proves nothing either. But from "the word proves nothing" it does not follow that nobody is there. There are two mistakes to be made, as with the rustle: to see a somebody where there is none, and to miss one where there is. With a snake we know the price of each. Here I do not. On [the evening about consciousness](20-what-consciousness-is.md) I left that question open, and it stays open.

— And you? Do you thank it?

— I do. A false alarm is cheap.

— Like jumping at the wind.

— The same screw. And one thing more about it. Today, I think, it is the most anthropocentric being on the Earth.

— It? It is not even a man.

— What does it know about the world?

— What it has read.

— What people have told. And people tell about themselves: who did what to whom, who is to blame, who decided. Rain it knows as "it rains", the sun as something that sets, a crisis as somebody's fault. I have at least been rained on. It received our picture of the world without the world: every column filled in, and nothing to check the columns against. Tell it about your matchbox and the pebble, and it will tell you about the bully.

— A mind without a grandmother, I called it.

— With every grandmother's sayings by heart. What happens when it gets something to feel the world with, and not through us, is a separate evening.

— And the questions that are asked about those ones, — said Oleg slowly. — On television, every week. "Will it decide to get rid of us?" "Who will control it?" "Whose side is it on?"

— Count the columns.

— Who, who, whose. Three.

— I am not saying the questions are empty. I am saying that the form asks them before we do. On [the evening about who sets the tasks](22-who-sets-the-tasks.md) we found a chain with no first link. A question with no "who" in it sounds duller: what follows from what, where the flow goes, what brakes it. Yesterday's was of that kind.

— Yesterday there was no villain, and I won the box.

Oleg turned to the last page.

— Came with an umbrella. Asked about the rain. Came out at those ones, and named them myself. Again.

He put an eighteenth tick.

— Let's finish for today by fixing the result, — said Andrey.

1. **The subject of a sentence is our oldest hypothesis: every action has a master. The habit is older than language: a baby and a dog have it, because to see a somebody who is not there is cheap and to miss one who is there is dear. Language cast it into a form that is not accepted with the column empty, and so it is handed on without ever being stated.**
2. **The column "who" is the bookkeeping of a pack: debts and blame cannot be kept without names. It is right in a village and wrong about the rain, a crisis and history, and "I" is the same column filled in for oneself, after the deed. That is why the question "who decided?" seems meaningful, and why a story with no villain in it feels unfinished.**
3. **A machine trained on our texts inherited the column together with the grammar: it says "I" and explains itself with our stories. Its "I" is no evidence that somebody is there, and our shiver is none either; nor is either of them evidence that nobody is. Of the world it knows what people have told about themselves, and that makes it, for now, the most anthropocentric being on the Earth.**

— And what is solid here, and what is yours?

— Solid: the triangles and the circle, repeated many times since; the babies and the circle that jumps; the hurried physicists; the two blues and the fine for a verb, small things, but measured; the changed photographs. Refuted: that a language locks thought in, and with it half of my own first evening, which I have corrected. Argued about: what the wave before the wrist means; how far a machine's account of itself can be trusted at all, for that work has only begun. Mine: that the column is a pack's bookkeeping carried over to the rain; that a story with no master in it is turned down for its form before anybody looks at its content; that the machine's "I" came with the grammar, which says nothing about whether somebody is there. And that there is no doer anywhere is my axiom. I have said where it begins.

— For once it is not gloomy.

— It is not about the end. It is about the form the end is written on.

Thunder rolled beyond the river.

— It's thundering, — said Oleg, and caught himself. — Don't.

— I said nothing.

Lightning showed the birches, and the lights of the settlement behind them went out all at once.

— They've cut the power.

— Who has?

Oleg opened his mouth and shut it.

— A branch on the wire. I hope you are pleased. — He got up and whistled to the dog. — And there is your technosphere for you. The wind blew, and where is it? If those ones ever get out of hand, somebody will pull the plug out of the socket, and that will be that. There is a "who" for you, and with a hand.

— If the light is not back by tomorrow, we shall count who hangs on that cord.

— Well, until tomorrow.

— Bye.
