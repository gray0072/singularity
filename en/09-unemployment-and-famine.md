# A History of Unemployment, the Causes of Famine in Africa, and the Selective Short-sightedness of Reason

<!-- import:pending -->

> Andrey divides the history of unemployment into three phases. First the technosphere displaced slaves. Then unemployment was parked in the third world. Now it has become global. Again and again people act against their own interest while sure they gain, though the logic is only two moves deep.
