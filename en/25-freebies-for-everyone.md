# Freebies for Everyone?

<!-- import:pending -->

> If machines do all the work, why not hand everyone a basic income? Andrey reminds Oleg that "free" in one place means "paid" in another, and that only solvent demand brings profit. A universal income could be the form of the short parasitism phase. Who pays for it, and how long can the technosphere afford to?
