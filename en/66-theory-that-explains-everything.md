# A Theory That Explains Everything

Oleg came with a hammer. In his other hand he had a piece of pine board, and his breast pocket bulged. Gaika sniffed the pocket with interest and was told to lie down.

— The dream first.

— An examination. I come in, and behind the desk sits a pigeon. I draw a ticket. The ticket is blank on both sides. The pigeon nods and puts down "excellent". Nobody rang.

— The twenty-seventh dream that did not come true. And the letter box?

— Empty. Hold this. — Oleg gave him the hammer, put the board on the log and stood a nail on it. — Drive it in.

Andrey drove it in with three blows.

— Now this. — A screw.

— With a hammer?

— With a hammer.

It went in with five, and split the board a little.

— It holds, — said Oleg, and wriggled the screw with two fingers. — Badly. At the plant we called that the fitter Petrov's technology. Now the last one. — He took an egg out of the breast pocket and stood it on the board, blunt end down. — Drive it in.

— I can break it.

— Break it, anybody can. I asked you to drive it in.

— Then no.

— There. — Oleg put the egg back into the pocket. — One thing out of three you could not drive in, and that one I had to bring from home. Now look here. — He opened the notebook at the last page. — Thirty-three ticks, each an "again". I asked you why we sleep. Where the horses went. What the rat laughs at. Why money goes to money. What the "it" is in "it is raining". Thirty-three times I asked about something else, and thirty-three times you brought me out at one and the same place. Not once did the hammer say "I can't". In the spring I asked you how you were better than the superstitious [pigeon](39-price-of-a-false-alarm.md). One feeder, seven dances. Now there are thirty-three dances. On [the evening of the old men](44-from-funeral-to-funeral.md) I wrote down beside it that fifteen years ago you saw your mountain and no fact of mine will shift it. And two evenings ago: two old men with one hammer. That is the bill. A theory that explains everything explains nothing.

— Yes.

— What, yes?

— It is the main danger of everything I have told you since 2011, and I do not know a stronger objection. I am not going to defend myself tonight. I shall do something else: give you a better weapon than a pigeon. May I?

— Against yourself?

— Vienna, 1919. A boy of seventeen works with the doctor Adler, in his clinics for difficult children. Adler has a theory: whatever a man does, he does out of a feeling of inferiority, to overcome it. The boy brings him a case which, [as he wrote later](https://bertie.ccsu.edu/naturesci/PhilSci/PopperArticle.html), "did not seem particularly Adlerian". The doctor explains it by the feeling of inferiority at once. He has not even seen the child.

— A good doctor.

— The boy is, in his own words, slightly shocked, and asks how the doctor can be so sure. "Because of my thousandfold experience." And the boy answers: "And with this new case, I suppose, your experience has become thousand-and-one-fold."

Oleg laughed.

— At seventeen. He would not have lasted long in my shop.

— The boy's name was Karl Popper, and he told the story in a lecture thirty-odd years later. Then he invented two men. One pushes a child into the water, to drown it. The other gives his life trying to pull the child out. He tried both on the two Viennese theories of the day. By the other doctor, the famous one, the first man suffers from repression and the second has achieved sublimation. By Adler the first suffers from a feeling of inferiority and has to prove to himself that he dares to commit a crime. And the second?

— Inferiority as well. — Oleg did not hesitate. — Has to prove that he dares to save.

— You would have made a doctor. He wrote that he could not think of any human behaviour which could not be explained by either theory. And that it was exactly this that their admirers took for their strength.

— The hammer drove in the nail, the screw and the egg.

— And was proud of it. Now the same year. On the twenty-ninth of May 1919 [the Sun is eclipsed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eddington_experiment). Two parties with telescopes sit on an island off Africa and in a town in Brazil and photograph the stars near the dark disc. A physicist in Berlin has said beforehand that the light of those stars, passing the Sun, will be bent, and by how much: one and three quarters seconds of arc. The old mechanics gave half of that.

— How much is a second of arc?

— One and three quarters is a small coin seen from more than two kilometres.

— And if the plates had shown half a coin?

— Then, the boy wrote, the theory is simply refuted. In November the plates were read out in London. They have been argued over ever since, and they held. But that is not what struck him. What struck him was that on that day the theory could have lost. Adler could not lose on any day of his life.

— So a good theory is the one that risks its neck.

— He put it more drily: every good theory is a prohibition. It forbids certain things to happen, and the more it forbids, the better it is. And the other way round: that a theory cannot be refuted by anything is not its virtue, as people think, but its vice.

For the first, three outcomes (pushed the child in; gave his life to save it; did nothing) are all marked "explained"; for the second, three outcomes of the eclipse plates (no bending; 0.87 seconds of arc; 1.75 seconds of arc) are marked "refuted", "refuted", "holds". Under the columns: forbids nothing; forbids two outcomes of three](../images/what-each-theory-forbade.svg)

— Then here it is, in your own coin. — Oleg tapped the last page. — Which of the two are you? The one with the plates or the one with the thousand and one?

— You are not to take my word for it, that is the whole point. So let us do the work the doctor never did. Read the ticks, one by one, and I shall say with which pencil each was drawn. You have the three pencils from [the evening of the portrait](64-portrait-of-the-heir.md).

— Hard: a law that does not ask what the thing is made of. Soft: a likeness, "it has happened before". And none: your guess.

— With one addition. On every evening there were two halves. The first was about your question: sleep, a horse, a banknote. The second was the turn to the place where you put the tick. I am asked about the second. Read.

— Sleep. "Came out at a network that is given sleep."

— Hard. Not because I am clever: the engineers ran into it themselves, without wanting to. Whatever learns in one and the same place where it keeps has to shut for stocktaking.

— Horses.

— Soft. Twenty-seven million in 1918, three million in 1960: that happened. That it will go the same way with us I did not derive. I showed you a photograph.

— You said so then. "A precedent is not a proof."

— I said it every time, and you still have thirty-three ticks, each the same size. Tonight they get sizes. Further.

— The pot on the stove.

— The order without a builder is hard. The turn, that the flow chooses the direction and we stand in it, is my belief. No pencil.

— The rat.

— No pencil. A guess laid on a hypothesis.

— Who exploits whom.

— No pencil. Which bank to look from cannot be proved, I told you so.

— Oxygen.

— Soft. A splendid precedent. A precedent.

— The dream and the pigeon.

— Hard. The screw between a false alarm and a miss stands in anything that decides on incomplete data, and the program that learned the stamp in the corner of the photographs is a witness nobody ordered.

— Firms and cities. That one had an equation.

— All summer that one lay in my hard pile, for exactly that reason. It does not belong there. The equation is a hypothesis of its authors, argued about to this day, and that the thing we do not name is a city and not a firm is mine. Soft, and I am being kind.

Oleg looked at him over the notebook, and read on. It took a quarter of an hour and a kettle of water. The guitar: no pencil. The bacterium: soft. The ants: soft, though the arithmetic under them is hard. The funerals: hard, the engineers ran into those too. Lamarck: hard. Money: a theorem for people, a likeness for those ones, the machines. The dog: soft. The matches: hard. The umbrella: no pencil, an axiom. The socket: hard. The pot and the liquid: measured for us, and that we are near the brim nobody has seen. The wolf, the fine, the electron, the wheelbarrow, the stick, the sheet: no pencil, a likeness, an axiom, a reading, a model that nobody but its author ever saw working, a reading. The soup: hard. Mars: hard, it is arithmetic. Laziness: soft, and the least trusted of all. The map: hard. The three assemblies, the evenings of summing up, they set aside, because putting lines on one page adds nothing to any of them. The sheets of the day before went to "no pencil" without argument.

— Count, — said Andrey.

Oleg counted twice, with his finger.

— Hard: nine. Soft: nine. No pencil: twelve. And three assemblies. Nine out of thirty-three. — He put the pen down. — So twenty-four times you led me by the hand and I wrote "again".

— It is worse than that. Look at what the nine say. Sleep, omens, old age of a mind, heredity, heaps that grow, the cord, the trace, the suitcase to Mars, the map. What is in common?

— That those ones... — Oleg stopped. — I say it, it is the middle. That those ones live under the same laws as we do. Have to sleep. Grow superstitious. Go deaf with age. And that where the law lets them off, with copying, they are quicker.

— That is all the hard pencil has drawn in a summer. It has drawn what they are: that there is no pedestal under us, nine times, from nine sides. It is [my second evening](02-anthropocentrism.md) of 2011, checked on things that did not exist in 2011. And now tell me in which pile lies what becomes of us.

Oleg ran his finger down the page.

— The horses. The bacterium. The dog. And the dinosaurs, which we set aside, are glued together out of the same. — He looked up. — Soft. All of it.

— All of it. That the heir is a relative of ours under the same laws, I can show you with a hard pencil. That we go the way of the horse, I have shown you only in photographs of others. A hard pencil for that exists in one place only: in the marks of [the autumn](30-the-last-campfire.md), forecasts with dates, and not one of them has come due.

— You answered a man about this once. I have it copied. — Oleg turned back several pages. — Under your talk to the astronomers, 2012. He tells you: correlation is not causation, you carry one curve over to everything. And you: these are not correlations, the same feedbacks give the same mathematics whatever they are made of. A cistern of a lavatory, an amplifier, a business, an organism.

— The answer is right, and it says where the line runs. For the cistern and the amplifier I can draw the loop: here the float, here the valve, here the equation. Where I drew you the loop this summer, the pencil is hard. For the horse and the bacterium I drew no loop. I said "look, how alike". The man of 2012 was right about half of my evenings, and I answered him about the other half.

![Thirty-three ticks, sorted. A grid of the evenings from 33 to 65, each a cell with its number and a word (sleep, horses, the pot and so on), tinted by the pencil with which the turn to the machines was drawn: hard, a law that does not ask about the material, nine; soft, a precedent, nine; no pencil, a reading or an axiom, twelve; three assemblies set aside. Under the grid: what becomes of people lies in the soft pile](../images/thirty-three-ticks-sorted.svg)

— Good. Sorted. — Oleg was not satisfied. — But this was you sorting you. The storyteller cannot be his own inspector, we had that on [the evening of the map](63-map-with-a-map-on-it.md). So now the inspector. First. How many questions are there in this notebook?

— You tell me.

— A hundred and forty-odd. I asked thirty-three. And every one of the thirty-three got a tick. A feeder that pays out every single time.

— And what does a works inspector think when a batch comes with no rejects at all?

— That the gauge is broken. Or that somebody is rounding. — Oleg squinted. — You are saying it for me.

— Thirty-three out of thirty-three is not a result. It is a symptom. The boy from Vienna noticed it about another theory of his day: its followers could not open a newspaper without finding it confirmed on every page. Confirmations are cheap for whoever goes looking for them. You chose the questions, and you chose them against me, I grant you that. But I chose the road from each. From sleep one can walk to medicine, to mattresses, to dreams. I walked where I always walk. That any question about a man can be led out to those ones proves that I know the road. Adler knew his.

— So the ticks are worth nothing.

— The ticks count how many times I managed the turn. They do not count how many times the turn was right. For that there is another count in the same notebook, and it is shorter. How many times in the summer did something come out against me?

— Against you... — Oleg leafed back. — The oxygen: you had written that it would vanish quickly, and it turned out there is stock for millions of years. The dinosaurs: you had not looked up what their fate was. Merging with the machine: you had waved it away, and it has a precedent two billion years old. The language that locks thought in: half an evening of 2011 gone. Your Lamarck, who turned out to be another man's law. Gratitude as the only handle on a setpoint. And the sentence of 2012, yesterday. — He counted. — Seven. Ten with the small ones.

— Those are the evenings on which the hammer hit something it could not drive in. And notice where they all are.

— In the details. — Oleg said it slowly. — Not one in the main thing.

— Either because the main thing is right. Or because the main thing is exactly where my heavy image lies, the habit no fact can shift, and nothing gets through to it. From inside I cannot tell the two apart. You wrote that down in the spring, and you were right to.

— Then the second. — Oleg turned to a page he had clearly prepared. — Your favourite word. For fifteen years, whoever disagrees with you is an anthropocentrist. I copied this from the forum, May 2011, your own hand: "All who argued remained anthropocentrists, that is, people who judge without objectivity, aimed at an answer set beforehand." Now put it beside your Viennese. A patient agrees with the doctor: the doctor is right. A patient argues: that is his complex arguing, so the doctor is right again. A man agrees with you: good. A man argues: anthropocentrism. Find me the difference.

Andrey was silent for some time.

— As an argument there is none.

— None?

— The word explains why a man disagrees. It says nothing about whether he is right. A sentence that puts every opponent into the wrong beforehand forbids nothing, and by tonight's rule it is worth nothing. I used it that way. On the forum, in the blog, at this fire.

— And as what is it worth something?

— As a pointer to where to check. You have the check from [the evening of the umbrella](50-what-is-the-it.md): say it the long way. Take the man out of the centre of the sentence and count again. If the count changes, there was something there. If it does not change, I have only called a man a name. The check is a calculation. The word is a label, and a label is not an argument, whoever sticks it on.

— That is the second whole tick. — Oleg turned to his own page and took the pen out. — In ink?

— In ink.

He drew it beside yesterday's and blew on it.

— So. Nine out of thirty-three. A feeder with no rejects. A label instead of an argument. — He closed the notebook on his finger. — What is left of the fire?

— What is left is what can be lost. Open the notebook at a clean page, and I shall dictate to you what must not happen if this fire has not burned for nothing. Not what I expect: that you have, with dates and odds. What I forbid. If one of these happens, you do not argue with me, you come and say: it happened.

— How many?

— Six. Write a heading and number them.

Oleg wrote the heading, looked at it, and did not show it.

— One.

— The share of the world's income that goes to labour rises ten years in a row, while the number of machines goes on growing. It stands against [the evening of the shares](12-value-shares.md) and against [the whole scenario](27-scenario-of-events.md).

— Where does it stand now?

— By [the count of the labour office in Geneva](https://ilostat.ilo.org/topics/labour-income/), as it reads in 2026: fifty-four per cent in 2004, fifty-three and a tenth in 2019, fifty-two and a tenth in 2022. And then fifty-two and seven tenths in 2025.

— It went up.

— Three years in a row.

— Three out of your ten. Already. — Oleg wrote it down with pleasure. — And in the autumn you gave me a mark: below fifty-one and a half by 2031.

— I did, and at this moment the needle is going against that mark. I think it is the way back from the pit of 2022, and it is still below 2019. But what I think is not written in the notebook. Write the three years. Write also that they recount the whole row every year: in the autumn I gave you fifty-two and four tenths for 2024, and now the same year reads fifty-two and a half. So the judge is the row as it is printed in the year of the check. And one thing more, for the price of the prohibition. I looked for a large country in which the share rose ten years in a row in this century. The longest runs I found were five: Brazil up to 2015, India up to 2020.

— So it is not forbidden by nature. It simply has not happened.

— A prohibition on what cannot happen anyway costs nothing. This one costs something: it is three tenths of the way already. Two.

— Two.

— The birth rate of the rich countries returns above the level at which the generations replace each other, two and a tenth of a child per woman, and holds there for ten years. With no coercion. It stands against [the evening of the children](26-why-people-stopped-having-children.md) and against the ceiling of [the eleventh](11-symbiosis-and-parasitism.md).

— Why the clause?

— Because with coercion it has been done. Romania, the autumn of 1966: a decree forbids abortion, and in a year [the figure goes from 1.9 to 3.7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decree_770). In the seventies it went down again.

— And now?

— The club of the rich countries, taken together: [about one and a half](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.DYN.TFRT.IN?locations=OE). Every member below the line except one.

— Which?

— Israel. Close to 2.9, and it has never been below the line at all. On the evening of the children I did not name it. I ought to have: it is a rich country with schools as long and as dear as anybody's, and my arithmetic of the price of learning does not explain it. What does explain it I leave out, by the rule of this fire. Write it down as an exception that I have not explained.

— Written. And Korea? In the autumn it was your banner. Seven tenths of a child.

— 0.72 in 2023, 0.75 in 2024, and for 2025 they report 0.80. Two years up.

— Against you.

— From the lowest place in the world to the next lowest. But up. Write it.

— And I have one of my own. — Oleg turned a page. — My great-nephew found it for me. Kazakhstan. In 1999 one and nine tenths, below your line. From 2004 above it, and not for ten years but for twenty: nearly three now.

— That is the nearest thing to a counterexample there is, and I did not have it. It fell there when the country fell apart, and came back when the wages did. It was not rich when it fell and is not counted among the rich now. So it does not break the sentence as I dictated it. It does show that a line once crossed downwards can be crossed back, by people's own will, for twenty years. I had been saying "nowhere". I may no longer say "nowhere".

— "Nowhere among the rich."

— Nowhere among the rich that I found: Sweden held above the line for two years around 1990, America for two around 2007. And who is rich, you decide on the day of the check, not I. Three.

— Three.

— The age at which a man makes his first discovery starts going down, the teams of scientists thin out, and the authors are still people. Against [the evening of the pot](53-million-mendeleevs.md). That one you have already, with a date: the autumn of 2041, under your pencil half-tick.

— Four.

— For fifteen years more the machines stay below a man in the sum of what they can do, while the computing goes on growing as it grows now. Against [the fifth evening](05-artificial-intelligence.md) and against the first of [the new ones](16-fifteen-years-later.md).

— "The sum of what they can do." — Oleg stopped writing. — That is rubber. Whatever they fail at, you will say: one tooth of the saw, of their jagged frontier, look at the sum.

— Then make it of wood. In the autumn of 2041 an ordinary office cannot get through a month of its ordinary work at the screens without one person in it. Not a chosen task: the whole month, whatever comes in. And the chips have grown all those years: today the computing spent on teaching the largest of those ones [grows four or five times a year](https://epoch.ai/blog/training-compute-of-frontier-ai-models-grows-by-4-5x-per-year), and four companies are putting about seven hundred billion dollars into the barns, the data centres, in 2026 alone. If all that goes on for fifteen years and the office still cannot do without its one person, the platform is not what I took it for.

— Whose office?

— You choose. Five.

— Five. This is the one you promised me yesterday.

— A large country gives up, for long and of its own will, a technology that raises the output of labour. And the others follow it. Against the scenario, against [the evening for those who understand](28-those-who-understand.md), and against yesterday's "mankind cannot stop".

— Giving up has happened. I read about it. The Chinese emperors had a fleet that sailed to Africa, and they called it home.

— [The last voyage came back in 1433](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ming_treasure_voyages), and the state built no more such fleets. Why, the historians have not agreed. Japan [shut its ports in the 1630s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sakoku), not completely, and lived so for over two hundred years. There is a well-known book that says it gave up the gun as well. That is disputed: the guns stayed, what stopped for two centuries of peace was making them better.

— So a country can.

— A country can. I never said that it cannot: a person can stop, and a country is sometimes one person. The prohibition is in the last five words. And the others follow it.

— And they did not.

— On the eighth of July 1853 four ships came into the bay near the Japanese capital, two of them under steam. Nobody had asked them to follow.

— So this one is my "to bargain". — Oleg found the old line on the last page. — I asked on [the evening of the dinosaurs](52-dinosaurs-at-the-feeder.md): not one of the former masters could read, and we can. You said yesterday that somebody has to sign for mankind and nobody holds that signature.

— And here is the rest of the answer, the only honest one I have. Whether mankind can bargain is not a thing to argue about at a fire. It is the fifth line. If a signature for mankind turns up, if the large countries give a thing up and the rest follow, then you were right on the evening of the dinosaurs and I was wrong for sixteen years. There is one mark from the autumn standing on it, to 2031. The line itself has no date. It may be crossed on any morning.

— Six.

— People with an improved brain overtake the pure machines in open work. In science, in a trade, in anything where both are let in. It stands against the eleventh evening and against [the evening of the bacterium](42-swallowed-bacterium.md).

— And today?

— Today a paralysed man moves a pointer on a screen with a chip in his head. The best figure announced so far, in the autumn of 2026 and by the maker himself, is eleven and a bit bits a second. The speed of a healthy man's own behaviour [is put at about ten](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.10234).

— So the wire has caught up with the head. You said then that the narrow place is not the wire but the head.

— If it overtakes, the sixth line is crossed, and the merging I waved away in 2011 is the main road. The autumn of 2041. That is all six.

![What must not happen, the page from Oleg's notebook as a table of six rows and three columns (what is forbidden; where the needle stands in 2026; the first day Oleg can come): 1, labour's share of world income rises ten years in a row (up three years running, 52.1 to 52.7 per cent in 2022–2025; the figure for 2032); 2, the birth rate of the rich countries returns above 2.1 and holds ten years with no coercion (about 1.5; Korea up two years; Israel never fell; Kazakhstan came back, and was not rich; not before the 2040s); 3, the age of a first discovery falls and the authors are still people (rising; autumn 2041); 4, machines stay below a man for fifteen years while computing grows (an office cannot do its month without a person; autumn 2041); 5, a large country gives up a productive technology and the others follow (countries have given up, nobody followed; any morning); 6, people with an improved brain overtake pure machines (about eleven bits a second against about ten; autumn 2041). Below the line: outside the list, what forbids nothing](../images/what-must-not-happen.svg)

Oleg read the page through, moving his lips.

— Now the inspector again. — He put the pen across the page. — "While the machines go on growing." "With no coercion." "Of its own will." "In open work." Every line of yours has a little door in it. I worked forty years with people who wrote technical conditions. I know what such doors are for. The share goes up ten years, I come to you, and you say: ah, but the machines were not growing properly.

— What did the followers of the theory with the newspapers do when its forecasts failed?

— How should I know.

— The boy wrote about that too. In its first form that theory could be checked, and it was checked, and it failed. And then its followers reinterpreted both the theory and the facts so that the two agreed again. They saved it, and paid with the only thing that had made it worth saving: after that it could not lose.

— And you will do the same. You are no better than they were. You said yourself that from inside you cannot tell.

— I shall want to. So the doors are not mine to open. Each clause is judged by the one who holds the notebook, on the day of the check. Was there coercion, were they rich, was the work open: your word, not mine. And the page is written tonight, before any of it. It is your book of dreams. "Written before, checked after, counted all." I am asking you to keep on me the same notebook that you keep on your pillow.

— And if in 2041 I am not here to open it?

— Then somebody else opens it. It is written down, [that is the point of writing](59-memory-of-tomorrow.md).

Oleg grunted and drew a line under the six.

— Something is missing. Your chief thing. That everything is settled beforehand, down to which of us will sneeze. Which number is that?

— None. Write it under the line, separately. I looked for an outcome that determinism forbids, for sixteen years, and I have none. Whatever happens, it was to happen. On [the evening of the autumn](17-global-determinism.md) I called it an axiom and showed you where the proof ends. By tonight's rule I have to say more: it is not knowledge. It is a way of keeping the books. I keep it because the books come out simpler with it, and I may not put it on the scales when we weigh who was right.

— Anything else under the line?

— That the thing coming is a life, the next one after ours: the premise of the fifth evening, you have it beside the heading of the heir's card. And that everything that exists has a mind to some degree. That is how I use the word "mind". A definition forbids nothing either.

— Three things under the line and six above. — Oleg looked at the page with some respect. — And under your fourteenth evening somebody asked you for the chance that you are wrong. In figures.

— I answered that by feeling, and I could not ground it in mathematics, about fifteen per cent. And that my opponents put it at ninety-nine.

— And now?

— About the dates the answer has come, and it was not fifteen. On our first evening of the new ones you went through them: I was wrong. About the direction the feeling is the same fifteen. And that it has not moved in fifteen years speaks against me and not for me. A gauge that never moves is stuck, any works inspector will tell you. So do not write the figure down. Write the six.

— The last evening of the old ones, — said Oleg, — when I brought you the Englishman's book, Penrose's. You said there what the weakest place in the whole thing was.

— [That I had understood it.](15-penrose-counterarguments.md) By my own account no man ought to have. I still cannot explain that. But I named the wrong place. The weakest place was that it could not lose. In May 2011 I came to the forum and wrote: I do not want to be right either, so help me find the error. For two years people argued with me, nobody found one, and I took that for strength.

— And it was not?

— I had not told them where to look. A man who asks to be shown his error and does not say what would count as one is asking to be consoled. Now there is a page.

— Let's finish by fixing the result, then. It is my line tonight, I have waited a summer for it.

— Fix it.

Oleg read from the notebook, slowly.

1. **A theory is worth as much as it forbids. Thirty-three ticks out of thirty-three are not a result but a symptom: confirmations are cheap for whoever looks for them, and a word that puts every opponent in the wrong beforehand is a label and not an argument.**
2. **Sorted by the pencil, the summer gave nine hard turns, nine likenesses and twelve readings. The hard nine say what the heir is: a relative under the same laws. What becomes of us lies among the likenesses and under the dates that have not come due. A precedent shows that such things happen and proves nothing.**
3. **The likeness of everything to everything is not a proof but a suspicion: either the world really is one system, or the storyteller has one hammer. The two can be told apart only by a date and a check. Hence six prohibitions, written before the outcome and judged by the other man. What forbids nothing stands under the line.**

— Yours is better than mine, — said Andrey.

— I had practice. And what is solid here and what is whose?

— Solid: the story of 1919, which I have read in the boy's own words. The plates of the eclipse, from an encyclopedia. The figures of the labour office and of the bank that counts children, as they are printed in 2026: both will be recounted. The author's: the answer about the cistern, the fifteen per cent, "help me find the error". The rule about prohibitions is the boy's, and over seventy years old. Mine, of today: the sorting, which the one who was being sorted did, so take a tenth off. The six lines. And that the weakest place was not the one I named. Yours: the label. The feeder with no rejects. Kazakhstan. And the doors.

— And what would make you take this evening back?

— The page itself. Any line of the six. And one thing more: if in fifteen years all six stand, and you find that I can explain it whichever way each of them had gone. Then the list was six more nails.

— Gloomy, — said Oleg.

— No. For the first time in sixteen years, no. A thing that can lose is more cheerful than a thing that cannot.

Oleg turned to the last page and looked at the thirty-three ticks. He did not add one.

— Came with a hammer and an egg, to show that you can drive in anything. Came out with six things that I have to watch for fifteen years. — He shut the notebook. — No tick. Tonight you came out at me.

He took the egg out of the pocket, cracked it against the log and began to peel it.

— Boiled, — said Andrey.

— I am not a fool, to carry a raw one in a shirt. — He gave half to Gaika. — And the forbidden word nobody said. All summer, on your side.

— I read it with my mouth shut.

— So nobody goes for firewood.

— There is enough. It will lie until the needles move.

— So you need me, — said Oleg.

— Without you it would be a sermon.
