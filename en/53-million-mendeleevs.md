# A Million Mendeleevs

Oleg came with the dog, the notebook and a school problem book without a cover, and put the book on the log between them.

— The dream first. An examination. I draw a ticket, and it is blank on both sides. I look up, and the examiner is my great-nephew. "Uncle Oleg," he says, "ask it yourself." Nobody rang.

— The fourteenth dream that did not come true, in the expense column. And the book?

— Mine, from the ninth form. I rang the boy at noon, with the picture on. Two problems, and the telephone, his pocket academician, on the table, screen down, where I could see it.

— And?

— One all. — Oleg sat down. — The first is a block on a slope, with friction. Eight minutes, and afterwards he explained it to me so that I understood, which is more than our physics master managed in 1981. The second is a ladder against a wall: how high can a man climb before it slides. Forty minutes. He drew, he crossed out, he looked at the telephone the way Gaika looks at a sausage. He did not solve it.

— And then?

— Then I let him turn it over. Two minutes. And he explained that one to me as well.

— With the screen up.

— With the screen up. So I claim half a tick. One problem he solved with his own head, and it was the academician in the telephone who filled that head: a year ago he could not tell a slope from a ski jump.

— Put it down. In pencil.

— Why in pencil?

— Because we have not yet agreed what it was that the academician filled.

Oleg put half a tick on a clean page and did not close the notebook.

— Then listen to the whole of it. In 2011 you told me that [learning had hit the ceiling](11-symbiosis-and-parasitism.md): a man learns for half his life, and the half that is left is too short to use what he learned. I let it pass, because the teachers were what they were. One for thirty heads, with a cold, and a queue at the door after the lesson. Now every head has its own. It knows everything, it is never tired, and it can be woken at three in the morning. Your ceiling was a ceiling of teachers. It has been taken off.

— That is the best thing that can be said against the eleventh evening. And I owe on it. In 2012, [after the seminar](../sources/garya/sqlru/2012-05-17-seti-talk.md), I was pressed on that limit for months, and nobody got out of me what would make me take it back. Today I shall say. But first a pot.

— Another pot? [We boiled one in the spring.](35-pot-on-the-stove.md)

— That one stood on the stove. This one is empty. — Andrey drew a pot in the ash with a twig. — What did the ladle in your foundry hold?

— Five tonnes.

— And how much metal was in it?

— That depends on when you look. Before the pour, five. After it, a crust on the bottom.

— Can there be six?

— There can. On the floor.

— And if the furnace fills it twice as fast?

— Then it is full twice as soon. Five.

— Two different numbers, then: what it can hold, and what is in it. In 2013, [on the forum](../sources/garya/sqlru/2009-09-15-apocalypse-scenario-thread.md), a man kept taking the one for the other, and I gave him a pot and a liquid. When litres did not help, I gave him a capacitor: the capacity is counted in farads, the charge in coulombs. [Intelligence](03-mind-and-intelligence.md) is the capacity of a head. Mind is what has been poured in.

— In the autumn you said it shorter: [intelligence is the ceiling, mind fills it](16-fifteen-years-later.md).

— In one line, and the line was half the truth. A ceiling is what that number looks like from a full pot. From an empty one the same number is a promise. A child of whom they say "gifted" and [an old man who is full](44-from-funeral-to-funeral.md) may have the same number.

— Farads. — Oleg snorted. — And where are they stamped on a man?

— Nowhere, and that is the trouble: we see only the liquid. Take twins. The same pot, the same head, to the gram. One grows up at home and finishes an institute. The other is lost in the forest at two and grows up in a wolf's den. Whose mind is greater?

— The first one's.

— And whose intelligence?

— By your two words... — Oleg scratched his chin. — The same.

— The same. Now we find the second at fifteen and give him your academician. Personal, patient, at three in the morning.

— He will catch up. Not at once.

— In the books he does. In 1970 in Los Angeles a girl of thirteen was found who had been kept in a locked room since infancy, and hardly a word had been said to her. [The best people the country had](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genie_(feral_child)) taught her for years. Words came, by the hundred. Sentences did not. And so as not to lean on one unhappy child: it has been [counted on the deaf](https://doi.org/10.1207/s15516709cog1401_2). Deaf children of hearing parents first meet sign language when they are brought to a school for the deaf, some at four, some at twelve. Thirty years later, having signed every day of those thirty years, the ones who began at twelve still sign worse.

— The kitten's eye. The window.

— The same window, for speech. In 2011 I said of such children that their memory was already taken. That was roughly said: the window, it seems, shuts by the calendar, whatever has or has not come in. The conclusion I keep. The liquid comes only from outside, and only on time. No teacher pours into a pot whose hour has passed.

![The pot and the liquid: on the left, two identical pots of twins, one nearly full (grew up at home, school and an institute), one nearly empty (grew up in the forest): the same intelligence, a different mind; on the right, one pot under two taps, a thin stream from a teacher for thirty and a thick stream from a teacher of one's own: it fills sooner, the brim is where it was](../images/the-pot-and-the-liquid.svg)

— My boy is no wolf-child. He got his on time. And the academician on top.

— Then to him. What is the academician for?

— So that he learns faster.

— And gets where?

— To the edge. To where nobody has been yet. Then he steps over it, and there is your discovery.

— How long did you walk to the edge?

— I never got there. I got to the plant. [Fifteen years, school and institute.](40-firms-die-cities-do-not.md) And he will be at it till thirty: I told you that myself in the spring, and you put it off.

— Till today. How old was Mendeleev when he laid out his table?

— Old. With that beard.

— Thirty-five. Newton had his best year at twenty-three. Einstein was twenty-six in 1905. Dirac wrote his equation at twenty-five. At that seminar I asked: suppose that to go further a man must know Dirac's equation at the age of three. Can you prove that there is a way to push it into a human head by then?

— With a personal academician...

— Personal academicians are not new. Who had them before?

— Princes.

— One prince had Aristotle himself, from the age of thirteen. What did the pupil discover?

— He conquered. — Oleg grinned. — Half the world.

— And died at thirty-two. Here is a cleaner case. An Englishman born in 1806. His father, a learned man, made an experiment of him: one teacher to one pupil, every day. [Greek at three, Latin at eight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Stuart_Mill), at fourteen as much as a university gives. He wrote later that he had started with an advantage of a quarter of a century over the men of his age.

— There you are. A quarter of a century.

— At twenty he broke down, and wrote about that too. His first great book came out when he was thirty-six. One of the best heads of his century, I take nothing from him. The teacher poured faster. The book did not come sooner.

— One Englishman.

— One. So let us take fifty-five thousand Americans. In 2009 an economist [found the birthdays](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-937X.2008.00531.x) of that many inventors and laid them against their patents, from 1975 to 1999. Three things came out. The age at the first patent goes up: by two thirds of a year every ten years. The inventors on one patent grow in number: 1.73 at the beginning, 2.33 at the end. And the man who invents alone moves from one field to another less and less often. The paper has "the death of the Renaissance man" in its title. Then he [took the Nobel laureates and the great inventors](https://doi.org/10.1162/rest.2009.11724) of the twentieth century. The age of the great work went up over the century by about six years. And the years are lost at the beginning: at the end of the century they start producing some eight years later than at its start.

— That is not a pot, that is a queue. — Oleg raised a finger. — [From funeral to funeral.](44-from-funeral-to-funeral.md) The chair is taken, the first big grant comes past forty, you told me so yourself. The young are not learning longer. They are waiting longer.

— For a grant one waits. For a patent nobody asks leave: an idea and a fee. And a queue would eat the years in the middle. They are gone from the start, where the school is.

— All right. Then it is simple arithmetic. One head takes longer, so take more heads. There are eight billion of us. Not one Mendeleev: a million.

— In 2012 I answered just that: take a million Mendeleevs, and at best you get the knowledge of two or three.

— Why two or three? A million heads.

— What is in each?

— Chemistry.

— The same chemistry. The same school, the same textbooks: to stand at the edge, each of the million first has to carry in everything that lies before the edge. His own goes on top, into what room is left. Add the pots up, and the common part is counted a million times and the room on top once each. And the real Mendeleev had room. Chemistry, oil, a flight in a balloon, weights and measures; on the day he laid out the table he was packed to go and inspect cheese dairies; and he made suitcases with his own hands, good ones.

— A Renaissance man.

— With the edge where it stood in 1869. Move the edge a hundred and fifty years on. The common part takes nearly the whole pot, and what is left on top is a thimble.

— Then one does not carry in the lot. — Oleg shrugged. — One takes a strip. At the plant nobody was simply "an engineer". There was an engineer for gating systems.

— In 2017, on the same forum, a man objected to me that people specialise more and more, and so need one another more. I answered that narrow specialisation is litmus paper: the system has gone into saturation, as a radio engineer says of a transistor that has nothing more to give. It is not that people have grown stupid. A full pot does not look like stupidity. It looks like a specialist.

— And what one pot does not hold?

— Is held in several. Capacitors in parallel: the farads add. In 2015 the mass of one particle [was measured](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.114.191803) by 5,154 authors. Nine pages of physics and twenty-four pages of names.

— At the plant that was called the payroll.

— Only between the pots there are wires, and the wire we measured in the spring: [thirty-nine bits a second](45-lamarck-was-right.md). Now the bill for all of it. In my trade there is [a rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moore%27s_law): the number of transistors on a chip doubles every two years, and has done since 1971. Four economists [counted](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.20180338) how many researchers that doubling takes. By 2014, eighteen times more than at the beginning of the seventies. And for the American economy as a whole: what one researcher yields halves every thirteen years, so to keep the old pace their number has to double every thirteen years.

— [The squirrel in the wheel.](40-firms-die-cities-do-not.md)

— The same wheel. There we saw that the inventions have to come sooner every time. Here is the count of who has to make them.

— Eighteen times. — Oleg reached for the pencil. — That one I shall tell at table.

![The same pace, eighteen times the people: two bars, the researchers needed to keep the number of transistors on a chip doubling every two years, 1 in 1971 and 18 in 2014; below, three signs: the age at a first patent rises by two thirds of a year every decade, the inventors on one patent grew from 1.73 to 2.33 between 1975 and 1999, the age of the great work rose by about six years over the twentieth century](../images/eighteen-times-the-researchers.svg)

— But it proves nothing about pots, — he said, having written it down. — The apples lower down have been picked, that is all. The ones that are left hang higher. Ideas are harder to find: I expect that is what your economists called it.

— They did, with a question mark. Harder for whom?

— For anybody.

— Is that branch high?

— For me, yes. For a man on a stepladder, no.

— So "high" is not a property of the apple. It is the apple measured against a ladder. "The fruit hangs higher" and "the ladder is short" are one sentence, said from two ends. And we always say it from the apple's end.

— Because the ladder is us.

— Does a dog have a pot?

— Gaika? — The dog raised her one standing ear. — She has. A decent one.

— Give her a patient teacher. Personal, at three in the morning. Will she learn the multiplication table?

— No.

— A fly?

— Stop it.

— And a man? Can he be taught anything at all, if he is taught properly?

— A man... — Oleg stopped. — I was going to say yes.

— I asked the man at the seminar how many of the six thousand languages of the Earth he had learned, and why so few. Everybody has a pot: the fly, the dog, the neighbour. And ours has no bottom. Where have we met that?

— [On the second evening.](02-anthropocentrism.md) The weed.

— It has come up in a new pot. "Man can learn anything, he was only badly taught." It sounds like a complaint about teachers.

— Wait. — Oleg turned back a page. — The pot does grow. I have read it: on those tests of wits every generation scores higher than the one before.

— About three points every ten years, through the whole of the twentieth century. Pot or liquid?

— How should I know?

— In Norway every conscript sits such a test, and has for decades. For those born after 1975 the score goes down. And it goes down [inside one family](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1718793115): the younger brother scores lower than the elder. Brothers get their pots from the same parents.

— So it was the liquid. Up, and then down.

— Food, school, what the tests happen to ask. A pot can be dented by hunger, and the dent can be beaten out. That is not growth.

Andrey threw a branch into the fire.

— Now your half tick. I do not dispute that he pours. Let us see what has been measured. Chicago, 1984: a teacher for each pupil against a class of thirty. The average pupil with a teacher of his own came out [above ninety-eight in a hundred](https://doi.org/10.3102/0013189X013006004) of the class. The author called it a problem and not a result: where is one to get a teacher for every child?

— In the pocket. Solved.

— Later counts of live tutors gave less, under half of that, which is still a great deal. Now the pocket. A large school in Turkey, nearly a thousand pupils, mathematics. [Three groups](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2422633122): a textbook; a talking machine as it comes; and the same machine told to behave like a teacher, to give hints and no answers. On the practice problems the second group did better than the textbook by 48 per cent, the third by 127.

— There.

— Then the examination. Screens down. The second group wrote it 17 per cent worse than those who had never had the machine at all. The third wrote it the same as the textbook. Not worse. And not better.

— Not better?

— Not better. And the opposite result, of the same year. Harvard, a course of physics, 194 students. The lecturers [built the machine teacher themselves](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-97652-6): one step at a time, no ready answers, think first. Half the students had a lesson with it at home, half in a good live class, and the next week they changed places. On the test after the lesson those with the machine had gained more than twice as much. And in less time: forty-nine minutes against an hour.

— So which of them is right?

— Both. What is the difference?

— In Turkey the machine solved for them, — said Oleg slowly. — At Harvard it made them solve.

— [To gnaw or to ask.](48-who-tamed-whom.md) The liquid goes in only through the gnawing. When he asks, it is poured past the pot.

— And mine asks. — Oleg looked at the problem book. — The slope he had gnawed at some time. The ladder he had asked.

— So what has the academician changed? [In the spring we counted three narrow places](45-lamarck-was-right.md) on the road from head to head. The mouth: one for thirty heads, and it could tell only a part of what it knew. That one is wide now, and patient. The ear: thirty-nine bits a second.

— The same.

— And the third: the pupil has to grow the skill himself, and [sleep on it](33-use-of-sleep.md).

— The same. One out of three.

— It pours faster, where the pupil lets it. I grant that, and by the measurements it may be a good deal faster. And now what I have owed since 2012. Suppose it is faster all the way, not one lesson but the whole school, and a third of the time is saved. Then your boy stands at the edge not at thirty but at twenty. The edge moves away by two thirds of a year in ten: ten years gained are a century and a half of that. If it comes to pass, I take the eleventh evening back. Not for ever, the pot is the same. For a long time.

— And you think it will not.

— I think three things, and they are of different strength. The first is measured: most will ask and not gnaw, because asking is cheaper, and [a skill that is not needed is not guarded](42-swallowed-bacterium.md). The second is one Englishman: what was poured in sooner did not come out sooner. The third is a question, and I have no answer to it. [A mind ages by filling](44-from-funeral-to-funeral.md), we said in the spring, and not by wear. Pour twice as fast, and when is the pot full?

— At thirty, — said Oleg, and frowned. — An old man of thirty. With heavy images, and nothing to be done about them.

— I don't know. Nobody has measured that.

![With the machine and with the screen down: a school in Turkey, grades compared with the group that had only a textbook; on practice problems with the machine, plus 48 per cent for the machine as it comes and plus 127 per cent for the machine as a teacher; on the examination without it, minus 17 per cent and no difference; a note on the opposite result at Harvard, more than twice the gain of a live class per lesson](../images/with-the-machine-and-screen-down.svg)

— All right. — Oleg turned the problem book over in his hands. — Then why carry it in at all? I'll say what the boy says. The edge is far, so don't walk to it. Let the pocket remember the road. A man keeps the thimble: the idea, the question. The scientist stands at the edge, and the machine holds everything that is behind him.

— It can be done, and it is being done. Then who made the discovery?

— He did.

— Connect a capacitor of one microfarad in parallel with one of a hundred. What is the capacity of the pair?

— A hundred and one.

— Whose is it?

— The pair's.

— And who needs whom? To the small one the large one is nearly everything it has. To the large one the small one is one per cent. I wrote that in 2013 too, and not about capacitors: about [a symbiosis](11-symbiosis-and-parasitism.md) that lasts while the gain goes both ways. The discovery was made by the system "man and machine", and the shares in it have to be counted, as we counted them [on the twelfth evening](12-value-shares.md).

— For now the one per cent is the one who asks the question. [The customer.](22-who-sets-the-tasks.md)

— For now. And there is a way to tell whose a thing is, and it is yours: the screen down. What he can explain with the screen down is in his pot. What he cannot is in the pair's.

Oleg was silent for a while, then put the book back on the log.

— Then I shall say the rest myself, since you are sitting with your mouth shut. Those ones, the machines. Do they have a pot?

— They have. In the same thread I was told that I was promising them a mind without limit, and I answered that intelligence is the limit, that the technosphere has one too and always will. The only question is whether it is larger than ours.

— And is it?

— I shall say what is known, and all of it has been said on other evenings. Ours is filled anew in every head, for twenty or thirty years; theirs is [filled once and copied](26-why-people-stopped-having-children.md). Ours has been one size for as long as our kind has lived; theirs is redrawn every year or two, [there is a drawing board](52-dinosaurs-at-the-feeder.md). And their pot fills too: the network that learns worse with every new task, we have had that one.

— And a million of them? — Oleg leaned forward. — The papers say it in so many words: a country of geniuses in a server room. There is your million Mendeleevs. Sitting in my foundry.

— By my own arithmetic of 2012 a million identical Mendeleevs know what one knows.

— Ha.

— Don't hurry. [A copy with no search is a stop](45-lamarck-was-right.md), and that is against them. But look at what is for them. Our million carried in the common part a million times, thirty years each. Theirs carry it in once. After that the million can go from the same edge in a million directions, and none of them is held to a thimble by the years of school. Whether anything will be found that way that is not in the books already, and who will understand it if it is, is an evening of its own.

— I'll bring it. — Oleg made a note. — And now the marks. You promised to say what refutes you.

— The first is slow. The age at a first invention, counted as it was counted in 2009. I say that for work done by a head, with the screen down, it will not turn down: by the autumn of 2041, three to one. If it turns down, I was wrong about the pot, or the academician has widened the road more than I believe. If it turns down only with the screen up, the discovery is the pair's, and then count the shares. And I shall say against myself at once: this needle grows muddier every year. Soon there will be nobody at the edge with the screen down.

— And the fast one?

— There is a problem set that is given with the screen down in dozens of countries at once. Fifteen-year-olds, every few years, mathematics. [The last round](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/pisa-2025-results-volume-i_73451bc5-en.html) was sat in 2025: 463 points on average over the rich countries, the lowest since the counting began; in 2022 it was 472. Those who sit it in the thirties will have had an academician in the pocket since the first form. If he pours into everybody as he did at Harvard, they will go up, and noticeably. I say that by the autumn of 2036 no round will have come out above 2022. Two to one.

— And if one does?

— Then ink over the pencil. A whole tick, and yours.

— The needle is dirty. Telephones, epidemics, who is sent to sit it.

— Dirty. I have no cleaner one.

Oleg wrote both down under the half tick, with the years and the odds, and turned to the last page.

— Came with a problem book, to show that we shall grow. Came out at who needs whom, in microfarads. And put those ones into my foundry myself. Again.

He put the twenty-first tick.

— Let's finish for today by fixing the result, — said Andrey.

1. **Intelligence is the capacity of the pot, mind is what has been poured into it. The same number is a promise while the pot is empty and a limit when it is full. The liquid comes only from outside, and only on time.**
2. **The sign of a pot near the brim is not stupidity but specialisation. Everybody first carries in the common part, and what room is left for his own grows smaller: the strips narrow, the teams grow, the first discovery comes later, and the same pace of progress takes eighteen times more researchers than it did fifty years ago. A million Mendeleevs give two or three.**
3. **A machine teacher pours faster, where the pupil gnaws and does not ask. The pot is the same. A checkable consequence: the age of a first discovery made with the screen down goes on rising. If it turns down, the limit of the eleventh evening is refuted. If it turns down only with the screen up, the discovery was made by the pair, and the shares have to be counted.**

— And what is solid here, and what is yours?

— Solid: the count of the inventors and their ages; the eighteen times; the two experiments with the pocket, each as far as it goes, one school and one course; the deaf signers; the Norwegian brothers. Argued about: why the age goes up. The economists say the burden of knowledge grows, and do not say that the pot is near the brim; in their papers a man can still take a narrower strip and a larger team for a long time. Mine, that is the author's: the pot and the liquid, the million Mendeleevs, specialisation as litmus paper. A reading and not a measurement: that we are near the brim. Nobody has seen the brim. A question: whether a pot that is filled faster grows old sooner. And two marks, which may come out against me.

— And what do I tell the boy?

— Nothing. Ask him tomorrow to explain the ladder to you. With the screen down.

Oleg whistled to the dog and got up, and then stopped.

— I'll tell you what I don't like in my foundry. Not that they are clever. I have seen clever men: the cleverer, the longer the teeth. It was not fools who made the bomb. A million clever ones under one roof... Give me a meek creature any day. A dove, with a twig in its beak.

— Have you ever seen doves fight?

— Doves? They coo.

— Then tomorrow about doves.

— Well, until tomorrow.

— Bye.
