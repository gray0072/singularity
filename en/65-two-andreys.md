# Two Andreys

Oleg came with his reading glasses already on his nose and the printed sheets in his fist. Gaika looked at the sheets, found that they did not smell of soup, and lay down where the light ended.

— The dream first.

— I come to the fire, and there are two of you on the log. The same jacket, the same beard. I ask which of you is going to pour the tea. Each points at the other. Nobody rang.

— The twenty-sixth entry. And the box?

— Empty. Now listen. In the second voice, as ordered. — Oleg shook the sheets flat. — "One must learn to value one's life. To be grateful, if not to God then at least to fate, for the chance to look every day at the sun and the sky." Further. "The idea of service to society. The idea that duties come before wants." Further. "One can learn to love, if one sets oneself that aim." And this, — he opened the notebook at a page with a bent corner, — is yours as well, from under your talk to the astronomers, 2012. A reader asks what is to be done. You answer: "I have rid myself of anthropocentrism far enough to see that contradiction. So I propose nothing, I only state." Which of the two of you is Andrey?

— Both. Before we begin, clear the table. What else is in the sheets?

— Elections. Presidents. Who is to blame for what. God.

— Those are not for this fire. Sixteen years ago we agreed to argue from the laws of nature and not from sides, and I am not going to change the rule on the evening when it is inconvenient for me. And one thing more I shall not defend: in the sheets there are culprits with names. At this fire there have never been any.

— Convenient.

— Perhaps. Take what is left and see whether it is little. Gratitude. Service. The price. And love, which he could not explain and I cannot either. Four things. Start with whichever makes you angriest.

— Gratitude. — Oleg did not have to think. — "Be grateful for what you have." I heard that for forty years, from every chief who was about to underpay me.

— Leave for a minute who says it. What does the thing do? You have hot water in the tap.

— I have.

— When did you last rejoice at it?

— In August. They shut it off for two weeks and then turned it on.

— And for the other fifty weeks?

— For the other fifty it is just there. — Oleg grunted. — I know. [The stove that goes cold](62-when-laziness-wins-for-good.md). The gauge shows not what there is but how much was added since yesterday. You called it a lock on the lever.

— So the gauge can be made to read again. By shutting the water off. Is there a cheaper way than the plumber?

— To imagine it shut off?

— In 2008 some [psychologists tried exactly that](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2746912/). People who had lived with a husband or a wife for five years and more were asked to write how they might never have met: a different train, a different queue. Others wrote how they did meet. The first went away more pleased with their marriages than they had come. And when other people were asked beforehand which of the two tasks would cheer them more, fourteen of sixteen chose the second.

— So they did not know where their own gauge was.

— And there is an older experiment, and it is the one the man in the sheets needs. California, [published in 2003](https://greatergood.berkeley.edu/pdfs/GratitudePDFs/6Emmons-BlessingsBurdens.pdf). About two hundred students, ten weeks. Once a week each writes down up to five things. A third of them: what they were grateful for that week. A third: what had annoyed them. A third: simply what had happened.

— Lists.

— Lists. At the end the grateful ones rated their life as a whole about a third of a point higher, on the scale they were given, than either of the other groups. They complained less of headaches and coughs. And they had exercised nearly an hour and a half a week more than the annoyed ones.

— They wrote five lines on a Sunday and went running?

— More than the annoyed ones. Against those who just wrote down events the difference in exercise could not be told from chance. I give it to you as it lies. Then the same thing with sixty-five grown people who had a disease of the nerves and muscles, every evening for three weeks. Those who wrote the lists slept about half an hour longer than those who only filled in the forms. And their wives and husbands were asked, separately.

— And?

— The wives and husbands rated the life of the list-writers as better. They did not know who had been writing what.

— The wives I believe more than any form. — Oleg took off his glasses. — Now as the control department. Two hundred students is a small batch.

— It is. So take everything. In 2025 a group [put together all such experiments they could find](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40627390/): a hundred and forty-five papers, nearly twenty-five thousand people, twenty-eight countries. The answer is 0.19.

— Of what?

— Of the usual spread between people. Statisticians measure that way when the scales are different. Put it into height. Men differ from one another by about seven centimetres, give or take. Nought point one nine of that is a centimetre and a bit.

— So gratitude is a pair of thick insoles.

— Thick insoles. And there is worse. In 2016 others [sorted the experiments by what the grateful were compared with](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26575348/). Against people who only filled in forms: three tenths. Against people who did some other task: less than two tenths. Against people who did another exercise with some method in it: nought. Minus three hundredths. And in 2021 a third group counted [what it does for those in real low spirits](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10902-020-00236-6), found it "relatively modest", and advised such people in plain words to take something with better evidence.

— And does it wear off?

— The authors of 2003 wrote that they did not know. Two years later [one laboratory reported](https://sonjalyubomirsky.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lyubomirsky-Sheldon-Schkade-2005.pdf) that students who counted their blessings once a week gained by it, and those who did it three times a week gained nothing. "Perhaps", they wrote, the practice went stale.

— The gauge got used to the thanks as well. — Oleg laughed. — So. A centimetre. No better than any other honest exercise. Goes stale. And the man in the sheets calls the lack of it the main problem of mankind.

— There he has gone too far, and I shall come to who else has. I had meant to tell you tonight that gratitude is the only known way of turning a setpoint down by hand. The count does not let me. It is one of the few handles that have been tried under control and found to turn at all. A hand pump. It lifts a little, and for as long as you pump.

![How much gratitude gives, a chart of pooled results in units of the usual spread between people, with marks at nothing, small (0.2), medium (0.5) and large (0.8): well-being in all comparisons 0.19 (2025: 145 papers, 24,804 people, 28 countries); against those who only filled in forms 0.31 with a wide interval; against another task 0.17; against another exercise with a method in it −0.03, indistinguishable from nothing; fewer signs of depression 0.29 (2021)](../images/how-much-gratitude-gives.svg)

— Then here is what I came to say about your pump. — Oleg put the glasses back on. — Three evenings ago you had three roads to "reached". Make the housings. Make them faster. Write them in with a pencil. A man who has nothing new and sits down to feel pleased with what he has: which road is that? It is the pencil. He pads his own report.

— What does the pencil write?

— Housings that are not in the warehouse.

— And here?

— Here... — Oleg stopped. — Here they are in the warehouse. The water is in the tap.

— The housings are on the shelves, and the report has stopped showing them, because it shows only the increase. What do you call it when a man goes round the shelves and counts what is there?

— [A stocktaking.](33-use-of-sleep.md)

— A stocktaking is not padding. And there is a test. What does a shop do when its report is padded?

— Stops. Why work.

— The rat at the lever did not eat. The grateful students moved more. And in the second experiment of the same paper, with daily lists, they more often said that during those days they had helped somebody or stood by somebody. The shop did not stop.

— All right. Not the pencil. — Oleg leaned forward. — Then the glove. [The foxes](48-who-tamed-whom.md), and one test: does it bite the hand. "Be content with your feed" is exactly what the hand breeds for. A grateful one is a convenient one. Every chief knew it without your Californians.

— Yes.

— What, yes?

— The same screw can be turned by somebody else's hand, and from inside it is hard to tell which hand is on it. I have only the test from [the evening of the wolf](54-dove-crueller-than-wolf.md): who can unscrew it. If I can be glad of the hot water and write the complaint about the roof on the same day, the hand is mine. If the thanks are asked of me instead of the roof, it is the glove.

— And in the sheets? Which is there?

— Both. I take the first.

— Noted. — Oleg wrote. — Next. The price. "A value is set by the price people are ready to pay for it." You gave me that on [the evening of the songs](41-cheesecake-for-the-ears.md), about small change. He says it about life and death.

— Then let us take it at the middle size. You know that I ran the computing department of a holding for seventeen years.

— You said. And then left.

— The holding grew, and I was asked to do a thing that went against my conscience. I left. To nowhere. For half a year I had no work.

— What thing?

— Not for the fire. What did the holding do the next morning?

— Hired another man.

— And did he do what was asked?

— How should I know. — Oleg thought. — He did. Otherwise why hire him.

— So what did my leaving change?

— For the holding, nothing. — Oleg looked at him over the glasses. — For you, half a year of salary.

— And who looked the loser that half-year, he or I?

— You.

— In November 2013 a man on the forum [wrote to me](../sources/garya/sqlru/2009-09-15-apocalypse-scenario-thread.md) that mankind, unlike a machine, can stop and think again. I answered that a single person can. Mankind obeys the laws of how those who have already thought again deal with those who have not yet. The second gain something here and now, the first look like losers beside them, and that is why the whole does not stop.

— Like the pencil. — Oleg nodded slowly. — A foreman who did not draw his couple of hundred at the end of the quarter sat at the meeting like a fool. No bonus, and forty men looking at him.

— And the next quarter?

— He drew.

— A man stopped and was the loser. A shop stopped and was corrected. That is all there is between the sheets and the fire. The sheets speak to a person: value, be grateful, give the sweet away. The fire spoke about mankind: it cannot stop. A person can stop, and it costs. The price is how you know it was a value. I learned mine afterwards, like everybody: half a year.

— Smooth. — Oleg turned the notebook back to the bent corner. — Too smooth. Because the fire did not speak only about mankind. I read it to you at the start. A person asks you what he should do, he and not mankind, and you tell him that the question contradicts itself. And here is one more, I copied it out in the winter. From the one who wrote on for you. He says, in his own voice: "Here the evenings were more fatalistic than their own determinism allows." I did not name him first yesterday. Today I do, and it is past the middle.

— It is. Read on [the autumn evening](17-global-determinism.md) what I said about the sick man.

— "If I am fated to recover, I shall recover with a doctor or without one, so why call the doctor." You said it was an old trick. Whether the doctor is called is one of the causes of whether he recovers.

— And what did I do in 2012?

— Told the man not to call the doctor. — Oleg said it without triumph. — For mankind you may have been right. No word turns that. But he was one man. What he does next has causes, and what he has just read is one of them.

— From "mankind cannot" I concluded "so a person need not ask". It does not follow. It is the very fatalism that I myself separated from determinism, and I went on speaking it in the same breath. On [the evening for those who understand](28-those-who-understand.md) I mended a half of it: I said that a man's decisions are among the causes of what becomes of his family. The sentence of 2012 I did not take back. I take it back now. [The one who wrote on](31-who-spoke-for-andrey.md) was right, and so are you.

— So fifteen years ago you went too far.

— I went too far.

Oleg turned to the page where his own ticks stood, four halves in pencil. He took the pen out of his breast pocket.

— A whole one?

— A whole one. In ink.

He drew it, and blew on it.

— Now the other one, so that it comes out even, — said Andrey. — The man in the sheets steps over the same line from the other side. Read where he says what would happen if everybody did as he asks.

— "If every generation cares more for the next generation than for itself, then everybody will live in comfort."

— To whom is that addressed?

— To everybody. — Oleg frowned. — To mankind. And your answer of 2013 lies across it: the generation that cares looks the loser beside the one that does not.

— And where children are concerned he names somebody to blame. On [the evening of the children](26-why-people-stopped-having-children.md) we counted: the money, the schools, the price of making a person fit for this world. It falls under every kind of government and every kind of newspaper. Nobody to blame. And so nobody to talk round.

— He has an arithmetic there as well. — Oleg found the place. — Mankind is five million years old, a generation is twenty-five years, two hundred thousand generations, and each of them raised children. And then one man breaks the thread.

— The arithmetic I must correct, as I corrected [my own sixty-two thousand trillion](16-fifteen-years-later.md). Five million years is not mankind. It is not even the whole road from the fork with the chimpanzee, which is put at [six to eight](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22891323/). Our own kind is [some three hundred thousand years old](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jebel_Irhoud), and that is twelve thousand generations.

— Fewer.

— Or more. Why stop at the fork? The thread has not been broken once in four billion years, down to the bacterium.

— Then the tit in your apple tree has the same thread. Unbroken, four billion years. — Oleg lowered the sheets. — And nobody congratulates the tit.

— It is the class reunion from [the evening of the observer](56-observer-who-is-not-there.md): everybody who came is alive. The thread is a fact about where a line came from. You have it written down as "not a promise". It is not a debt either.

— So there he is wrong.

— There he asks mankind for what only a person can give. Each of the two is right on his own scale and wrong where he steps over to the other's. The one at the fire, when he tells a person that there is nothing to ask. The one in the sheets, when he hopes that mankind will hear.

— And which of them are you?

— The one you catch.

— They both pointed at the other, — said Oleg. — In the dream.

![A person can stop, mankind cannot, a table of two columns (one person; mankind) and four rows: can it stop (a person: yes, and it is paid for, the one who stopped falls behind; mankind: no, whoever has not stopped gains and the rest follow him); what a word does to it (one of the causes of what he does next; moves no setpoint of the whole); who spoke to it (the late pages of 2023–2025; the blog and the fire of 2011–2012); where he stepped over the line (the pages: "if every generation…", a hope for mankind; the blog: "so I propose nothing", a verdict on the person)](../images/a-person-can-stop-mankind-cannot.svg)

— Service, — said Oleg. — "Duties before wants." I put that together with another of yours. Under the same talk a reader reproached you, and you never answered him. You had written that one can perfectly well remain a small screw of nature, and a happy screw at that, with no claim to rule the Universe. And he said: that is resignation. Fold your hands and wait. There is an old church word for it, [quietism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quietism_(Christian_contemplation)).

— You spent your life at a plant. What does a screw do?

— Holds.

— When?

— All the time. It is under load from the day it is driven in. Nobody looks at it until it is gone.

— And a screw that holds nothing?

— Lies in a box. A spare. Or scrap.

— Then which of the two is the one with folded hands?

— The one in the box. — Oleg scratched his chin. — So "small" was about something else.

— "Small" was about the claims, and I keep it. It was never about the load. What the man in the sheets calls service is the load. The question I would put to him is my old one: of which system? I can be a screw in what is my own size. A family. A shop. Sixty garden plots: you have [our chairmen](43-why-ants-do-not-strike.md) written down, the five who stole and then the one who worked for nothing, and the place came right. At that size one man is a cause, and you can see it with the naked eye.

— And in 2012 you advised people to go off into the backwoods. With no gas and no light. I have that copied too.

— When the crowds begin to look for somebody to blame: not to answer aggression with aggression, and to withdraw. The first half I keep. The second I wrote for the last steps of [the scenario](27-scenario-of-events.md), and those steps have no date. Until then a hermit is a screw that has put itself in the box.

— That makes two things taken back in one evening.

— One and a half.

— And what holds the screw? — Oleg turned the sheet. — A real one is held by the thread. In my family nobody is driven in. On that evening for those who understand you said: keep the bonds by habit, now that need no longer holds them. I asked myself afterwards what that was supposed to mean.

— He says what, and it is the best page there. You feel like eating a sweet. Give it to somebody near you. You will be sorry at first. Do it again. Give up your seat, hold the door, do not push ahead in a queue.

— My last bag, — said Oleg. — [The one I never opened for anybody.](18-anatomy-of-wants.md) I should be sorry.

— He writes that you would. And that it passes. In 2012 some people in Canada [tried it on those who cannot be talked into anything](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0039211): twenty children a month or two short of their second birthday. A child gets a bowl of biscuits of his own. There is a toy monkey who "likes treats". Sometimes the child is handed a spare biscuit to give the monkey, sometimes he is asked to give one out of his own bowl. People who did not know what was being tested watched the films afterwards and marked the faces from one to seven.

— And the faces?

— Happier when giving than when getting. And happiest when giving from the child's own bowl.

— At two. Before anybody has told him that it is good.

— Twenty children, and one laboratory. With grown people and money it has been repeated on thousands: it holds, and it comes out small. Like the insoles.

— So one can learn it. — Oleg folded the sheet in half. — Then the last. Read it yourself, it is your voice there, not the angry one.

— I remember it. I was in a train. For many years before that I had lived where no birches grow. And outside the window there was a birch wood, the most ordinary one. Everything in me turned inside out, and I cried. He asks where that comes from, and answers that he does not know and nobody will explain.

— Derive it. All summer you derived. Sleep, laughter, omens, songs. On [the evening of the flies](46-flies-apart-from-cutlets.md) this went into the heap "not sorted", and it has lain there since.

— I shall show you how far I get, and where I stop. What bonds have we counted this summer? Draw a table.

Oleg smoothed the ash with his boot.

— Kinship. [The ants.](43-why-ants-do-not-strike.md) Holds by the share of common drawings. Lets go when the share is small.

— The second.

— Debts. Who gave and has not yet received. Lets go when nobody remembers, or when the two will not meet again.

— The third.

— Need. I cannot do without the plant, the plant cannot do without me. [Money](47-money-as-memory.md) is a record of that. Lets go when the need is over. — He wiped his finger on his trousers. — That is all.

— Your wife has been with you for forty years. Go down the rows.

— Kinship: none, thank God. Debts: [if she billed me](47-money-as-memory.md), I should not pay it off in two lives. So it is not that, nobody keeps a book. Need... — Oleg was silent. — In ninety-six the plant paid us in saucepans, and before the saucepans in nothing. By need she ought to have left then. There were offers.

— Suppose she had recounted every morning.

— Then she would have gone that spring. And I, some other year, by the same count.

— A bond that is recounted every morning breaks at the first better offer. So a pair that has to get through a bad year needs something that does not count. An economist wrote a book about it in 1988: a feeling is of use exactly because it does not calculate, and the other one can rely on it for that reason. That is what I can say about what it is for.

— So it is derived. A fourth row: holds by nothing that can be counted.

— Described. And I can give you a witness, as on the evening of the flies. In the American prairies there lives a vole that keeps to one partner for life. Its cousin in the meadows next door does not. In the early nineties [the difference was found](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1321430/) in where in the head the locks for two hormones sit. In 2004 one of those locks was [put into the meadow cousin](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15201909/), in one place in the forebrain, and it began to keep to its partner far more.

— One gene. — Oleg was disappointed. — And you say "not derived".

— So they thought. In 2023 [prairie voles were bred](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36708707/) with no lock for the first hormone at all, three lines of them. They paired all the same, and chose their own partner over a stranger.

— So the screw was found, and it holds without it.

— A screw was found, it is not the only one, and the rest are not found. So: it is not ours alone, there is a witness. The task I can name: to hold when need lets go. What I cannot do is what I did with sleep. There the engineers ran into the task themselves, without wishing to, and I could say that whoever learns will have to pay. Here nobody has run into anything. I cannot show that whoever has the task must come to this.

— And the birches?

— With the birches I have not even the task. No pair, no children, no debt, no need. I had lived well without them for years. It holds where there is nothing to hold. Perhaps it is the same thing overshooting, as the alarm of [the dream book](39-price-of-a-false-alarm.md) sees a master behind the rain. I do not know. I can tell you what it did: I stood at the window and cried. That is a description.

— Then I shall write "not derived" and add the fourth column. — Oleg looked up. — I say it, it is long past the middle. Those ones and us. Down the rows.

— You go.

— Kinship. Not a letter of the drawings; the words only, [the second road](52-dinosaurs-at-the-feeder.md). Debts: it owes us nothing, [the cow and the steak](30-the-last-campfire.md). Need: it needs our hands for some decades, and fewer every year, [the second number](51-week-without-a-socket.md). And your chart from the autumn, where its interest in us goes down to nought.

— And at nought?

— At nought three rows are empty. — Oleg sat back. — The fourth is left. The only one that holds when the need is over. And it is the one you cannot derive.

— For the dog you wrote it yourself: livestock is kept by a use, a pet by a want. Whether those ones will have such a want is yesterday's white line. It is not derived. It is being written, by persons, each with a name. And a person, we have just agreed, is the one who can stop and the one to whom a word is a cause.

— So now you do propose.

— No. I am taking back one sentence. I am not offering a remedy. I do not know what should be written in that line, and whoever tells you that he knows is selling. I only no longer say that it makes no difference what is written.

— And to bargain? I asked on [the evening of the dinosaurs](52-dinosaurs-at-the-feeder.md): they could not read, we can.

— To bargain somebody has to sign for mankind. You named it yourself: the right of signature. Nobody holds it. A person signs for himself. The rest of that question is in the bill you are bringing tomorrow.

![What holds a bond, a table of four rows and three columns (holds by; lets go when; between those ones and us): kinship (a share of common drawings; the share is small; not a letter of the drawings, only the words), debts (a record of who gave and has not received; nobody remembers, or they never meet again; it owes us nothing), need (one cannot do without the other; the need is over; decades, and less every year), love (nothing that can be counted; not when the need is over; not derived: being written)](../images/what-holds-a-bond.svg)

Oleg turned to the last page.

— Came with your own sheets, to catch you. Caught you. And came out at this all the same: when those ones stop needing us, the one bond that could still hold is the one that you cannot derive and he cannot explain. Again.

He put the thirty-third tick.

— Let's finish for today by fixing the result, — said Andrey.

1. **Both Andreys are right, because they speak about systems of different scale: mankind cannot stop, a person can, and pays for it. Each is wrong where he steps over: the one at the fire, when he tells a person that there is nothing to ask; the one in the sheets, when he hopes that mankind will hear.**
2. **Gratitude is a stocktaking and not padding: one of the few handles on a setpoint that have been tried under control and found to turn. It turns a little, and for as long as it is turned. "A happy little screw" is not resignation: a screw is under load, and it holds things together.**
3. **Love is a bond that holds when need lets go. That is a description and not a derivation. This is not a cure for what is coming. It is a way of being a person while it comes.**

— And what is solid here, and what is yours?

— Solid: the lists of 2003, as far as two hundred students and sixty-five patients go, and the three pooled counts, which I know from their summaries. The couples of 2008. The twenty children, one laboratory. The voles. The author's: that a person can stop and mankind cannot; that a value is known by its price; the small happy screw; the sweet given away; the birches; and the half-year, which is his and not mine to boast of. Mine, of today: that each of the two steps over the line, and where; that gratitude is a stocktaking; the test of whose hand is on the screw; the screw in the box; love as the fourth row. And one thing is Oleg's: that I went too far.

— And what would make you take it back?

— For gratitude: a count as large as that of 2025 that finds nought even against people who only filled in forms. For the scales: mankind stopping. A thing that everybody wants given up by everybody, for long, of their own will. That is on the list for tomorrow. For love: an engineer who runs into it while building something else, the way they ran into sleep. Then it will be derived, and I shall be glad to lose the word "cannot".

— Gloomy, — said Oleg, and listened to himself. — No. Not this time.

— But objective. A centimetre and a bit. Measured.

Oleg folded the sheets lengthwise and did not put them away. He opened the notebook at the last page and ran his finger down the ticks.

— One thing. Thirty-three times this summer I asked you about something else. Sleep. Horses. Rats. My own dreams. And thirty-three times you brought me out at the same place. Tonight I caught you on one sentence. Tomorrow I bring the whole bill. You know what they say about a man with a hammer.

— That everything around him is a nail.

— Well?

— Then tomorrow let us look for what a hammer cannot drive in. — Andrey got up and looked at the sky. — And now let us strive for comfort. It is warmer indoors.

— That is from the sheets.

— His best line.

— Nobody said the word.

— Nobody. Well, until tomorrow.

— Bye.
