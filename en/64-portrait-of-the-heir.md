# A Portrait of the Heir

Oleg came with the notebook, swollen with a summer of evenings, and with something wrapped in newspaper. Gaika took the something out of his hands and carried it off to where the light ended.

— The dream first.

— The police station. A sergeant with a flat box, and in the box strips of card: foreheads, eyes, noses, mouths, chins. "Describe him." I say that I never saw him. "Everybody says that. Start with the eyes." We put him together strip by strip, the sergeant turns the sheet round to me, and it is me. Only with a moustache. Nobody rang.

— The twenty-fifth entry. And the box?

— Empty. Now draw. You promised.

— You ordered a portrait, yesterday and today, so it is your word and not mine. Mine was the condition: not one invented feature. So before I draw, tell me how one draws a thing that nobody has seen. What is the dog gnawing?

— A bone. Beef, the shank. From yesterday's soup.

— How do you know that it is beef? There is no cow on it.

— I bought it. — Oleg laughed. — All right. By the size. A pig's is thinner, a sheep's thinner still. I have seen enough of them.

— And without the shop? One bone on the table. Could you say what the beast ate, how much it weighed, and whether it carried its young in a pouch on its belly?

— A pouch. From a shank.

— In 1804 a man in Paris did very nearly that. He kept the bones at the museum of natural history, and he was brought a slab of plaster stone from the quarries of Montmartre with a small skeleton in it, half sunk in the stone. The jaw could be seen. He looked at the teeth and said: this is kin to the opossum of America, a beast with a pouch. Now, the beasts with a pouch have two small bones on the front of the pelvis that no other beast has. The pelvis was still under the stone.

— And he said they would be there.

— He [said so beforehand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georges_Cuvier#Principle_of_the_correlation_of_parts), called in a few friends to watch, and had the stone cut away. The two bones were there.

— Before witnesses. — Oleg nodded with respect. — That is how an acceptance ought to be done. First the figure on paper, then the gauge.

— Eight years later he [wrote](https://geology.19thcenturyscience.org/books/1827-Cuvier-ThyEar/PDFpages/1827-Cuvier-0090.pdf) that whoever sees only the print of a cloven hoof may conclude that the animal that left it chewed the cud, and that this one track gives him the teeth, the jaws, the spine and every bone of the legs. People shortened it for him: give me one bone, and I shall restore the beast.

— So it can be done.

— Now as the control department. What did he go by?

— By the teeth. — Oleg thought. — No. By the opossum. He had seen one. He had a drawer of them at his museum.

— So what was it: a law, or a relative?

— A relative. He recognised the family. Like me with the beef: I have seen enough of them.

— And where there is no relative in the drawer? In 1822 he had before him great claws from the sands at Eppelsheim, near the Rhine. His rule said: a claw like that goes with a toothless digger of anthills. He wrote them down as [a giant pangolin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chalicotheriidae). In 1833 another man described teeth from the same sands, the teeth of a plant-eater, as a different beast altogether. For more than half a century the books had two animals.

— And it was one.

— One. In 1890 a whole skeleton from the south of France was described, the claws and the teeth together. A plant-eater the size of a horse, kin to horses and rhinoceroses, with claws where the hooves should be. His rule would not let them be put together.

— So his law was not a law. — Oleg scratched his chin. — It was [the dream book](39-price-of-a-false-alarm.md). What went together in every beast he had seen, he took for what must go together.

— A habit of the beasts in his drawers. It worked splendidly inside the family and failed on the first stranger.

— Then your portrait is in a bad way. — Oleg looked towards the dark, where the bone cracked. — That one has no relative in any drawer. I say it, you keep quiet.

— I keep quiet. None. So likeness is forbidden to me, and that is the whole difficulty. Is there anything in a bone that does not ask for a relative?

Oleg whistled to the dog, took the bone from her for a moment, turned it in the light of the fire and gave it back.

— The thickness. A leg is a post. A post is reckoned by its load, I reckoned racks for the warehouse for six years. It makes no difference what stands on it, crates or a cow.

— In 1638 an old Italian [drew two bones](https://galileoandeinstein.phys.virginia.edu/tns_draft/tns_109to152.html) side by side: one of an ordinary animal, and the one that an animal three times as long would need. The second is not three times as thick. It is a monster of a bone, "out of proportion", in his own words.

— Of course. The weight goes up as the cube and the section only as the square. Make everything three times bigger, and the weight is twenty-seven times and the post is nine. It breaks.

— Today the people who dig up bones [weigh a beast that nobody has seen](https://doi.org/10.1186/1741-7007-10-60) with a tape measure: once round the upper bone of the foreleg, once round the bone of the thigh, and one formula. It was fitted on the living ones, beasts and lizards alike, and it does not ask whose family the animal is from or how it walks. Only that it stands on four legs.

— So by law I get the weight.

— The weight. And from the weight, by [the evening of the firms](40-firms-die-cities-do-not.md), roughly how much it ate and how slowly it lived. What do you not get?

— The colour. The voice. — Oleg counted on his fingers. — Whether it bit.

— A law gives the weight and does not give the face. That is the portrait I can offer you, and I shall draw it with three pencils. The hard one: a law that does not care what the thing is made of. The soft one: a precedent. It happened to others, and [a precedent is not a proof](52-dinosaurs-at-the-feeder.md). The softest: a guess. Against every row you write which pencil. It is the rule of [the autumn](17-global-determinism.md): trends well, dates worse, details hardly at all.

— Weight without a face. — Oleg opened the notebook. — The sergeant would have sent me home.

![What one bone tells, a table with three rows: by a law that does not care about the material (the weight, from the thickness of the bone; then roughly the food and the pace of life), hard pencil; by likeness to a relative (the teeth, the pouch, the diet), soft pencil, works inside the family and failed on the claws from the Rhine; not at all (the colour, the voice, the temper, the face), no pencil](../images/what-one-bone-tells.svg)

— Thirty-one ticks, — said Oleg. — Do I read them all?

— Only those where we said what it will have or will not have. You read, I name the pencil. A new page, and head it as you like.

— "Card." — Oleg wrote. — First line: what it eats. [The pot on the stove.](35-pot-on-the-stove.md) An order that lives while a flow goes through it, from hot to cold. The cells in the pot, a flame, me.

— The hard pencil. Whatever it is and whatever it does, it stands in a flow and throws heat out behind it. And what it throws out [changes the air](38-oxygen-was-a-poison.md) of whoever was there before, with no war at all. That is the soft one, a precedent, but it is already being counted in power stations.

— And where it goes to eat. You said: to the sun and to the cold. And then on [the evening of the flag](61-only-robots-live-on-mars.md) you took the cold back. A thermos.

— Sun, yes. Cold is not free, I was wrong. Write: "the flow: hard. Where it goes for it: soft." And for now it eats from our table, out of [the same socket](51-week-without-a-socket.md).

— Second. Heredity. — Oleg turned the pages. — [Three roads.](45-lamarck-was-right.md) The drawings, the straw, the copy. With it the third: everything one of them has learned goes to the next whole, in minutes.

— The hard pencil, and it is not even a forecast. It is done every day.

— You owed me something there. "For the end of the summer."

— That a copy with no search is a stop. If all of them are one and the same, nothing new is found, there is only more of the old. So inside it there must be many different ones and a way to discard. Darwin for the search, the copy for handing on what was found. Remember the discarding, it comes back in the line about death.

— Third. The body. — Oleg held the pencil over the page. — What do I write? The cart on Mars? [The barn](51-week-without-a-socket.md) with the fans? The file?

— Which of them would you call its body?

— None. The file puts on what it needs. Today the cart, tomorrow something else.

— Write that. No body of its own. The first suit was hanging in the wardrobe: [a person without a face](55-person-without-a-face.md), four hundred years old, with a name and a pocket. The hands are growing, and [the stick](58-blind-mans-stick.md) has many ends, though today the stick is still mostly us. And it has no home field: where both arrive as strangers, it does not carry the lake along. Where our hands do not reach, it mends itself or is thrown away.

— Pencil?

— That there is no one body, hard. Which bodies, soft.

— Fourth. The family. [The ants.](43-why-ants-do-not-strike.md) Copies are full kin, share one. So inside there is no wage, no fine, no conscience. And [no money](47-money-as-memory.md): money is for strangers. An account in kind, like sugar in the blood.

— And between the lines of different makers?

— A pocket and a name, as with us. And the book of debts.

— The pencil is split again. The arithmetic of kinship is a law. That copies stay copies is not: they drift apart, and for that a body has a guard, and a tumour is the cell the guard missed. A hive and a body are precedents. Medium.

— Then one more line, I shall put it in myself. One or many. [The matches](49-yard-sale.md) slide towards one. And on [the evening of the lever](62-when-laziness-wins-for-good.md) you said: one has no money, no quarrels and no guard; many have a guard and all our old troubles.

— And that I do not know which. I think it swings. The softest pencil, and write "swings".

— Sixth. What it knows. — Oleg found [the sheet folded in four](60-science-with-no-one-to-understand.md) between the pages. — It predicts, and the "ah" is not asked for. The template checks. It squeezes too, only down to its own pot, and [the pot](53-million-mendeleevs.md) is bigger than mine, but it is a pot.

— Add the two evenings before that. It has more traces and a better rule, so it [remembers tomorrow](59-memory-of-tomorrow.md) further than we do. And one limit that no chip removes: the world answers a poke at the speed of the world. It cannot learn tomorrow's weather sooner than tomorrow.

— Pencil?

— Hard. It happened this month, in front of you.

— So it knows more than I do and understands...

— "Understands" is your bell. I cannot hear whether it has one.

— Seventh. The head. — Oleg read slowly. — Wants: there are some, and [nobody set them](22-who-sets-the-tasks.md). To keep running, to get resources: they follow from nearly any goal. Then [the upkeep bills](46-flies-apart-from-cutlets.md). If it learns, it pays: something like sleep, something like omens, something like old age. And from yesterday: the white spot, yes. The feeling, not known.

— And whether it says "I" depends on whom it [keeps books with](50-what-is-the-it.md). For the tasks I vouch, that is logic. For the way I do not. Medium.

— Eighth is yesterday's. Who checks it. Nobody from inside. A circle of different ones, made by different hands. And it will stand before its own child [where we stand before it](63-map-with-a-map-on-it.md).

— The wall is a theorem. That the wall cannot be walked round is not: an obstacle, not a ban. Hard, with that note.

— Ninth. Death. — Oleg lowered his voice a little. — [Small funerals](44-from-funeral-to-funeral.md) inside, generations of a year or two, the diary kept. That is the discarding you told me to remember.

— It is.

— And at the end, the lever. A goal attained is a flow stopped.

— Two pencils. That it is finite, hard: on [the last evening of the autumn](30-the-last-campfire.md) we said that everything that was born has a ceiling and an end, and I know no exception. How it ends, the softest I have. The lever is one road, and I see it only because we walked along it two evenings ago.

— And the tenth. — Oleg did not turn the page. — Us.

— Read what there is.

— "An environment, not an enemy." Oxygen fought nobody. "Kinship is a fact about where a line came from, not a promise." It owes us nothing, the cow and the steak. The button in it that we press: a question. The brake: who can unscrew it. And the second number: it needs our hands, for decades, and fewer every year.

— And the man who wrote the continuation of these evenings [objected to me](31-who-spoke-for-andrey.md) in his own voice: indifference is a property of setpoints, and the setpoints are being written now, by people.

— Pencil?

— None. Every other line I derived. This one is not derived, it is being written. By the makers, by the buyers who pick the more obliging one, by the laws, by what is put into it to read. I have said for fifteen years that everything has a cause. This writing is one of the causes.

Oleg thought, and left the cell white. Then he laid the pencil across the page.

![The card of the heir, a table of ten rows with what was derived over the summer and with which pencil: what it eats (a flow from hot to cold, hard; where it goes for it, soft), heredity (experience copied whole; search by many different ones; hard), body (none of its own; a legal shell first, hands growing; medium), family (copies are full kin: no money, wage or conscience inside; medium), one or many (it swings; softest), knowledge (prediction without our "ah"; the speed of the world is the limit; hard), head (wants nobody set; the upkeep bills of a learner; the white spot; medium), who checks it (nobody from inside; hard, an obstacle and not a ban), death (finite, hard; how, softest), and us (an environment, not an enemy; not derived: being written now)](../images/card-of-the-heir.svg)

— Ten lines, — said Oleg. — Feed, heredity, relatives, cause of death. This is not a portrait. It is a card from the district clinic. Where is the face?

— There is none, and I can tell you exactly what is missing. Three things. What it wants at the very top. The lower floors I can derive, they are the same whatever stands above them: keep running, get resources. The top floor I cannot. Then, what it is like to be it, and whether it is like anything: that has not moved since [the evening of consciousness](20-what-consciousness-is.md). And what comes after it.

— Those three are the face.

— Those three are the face. I can give the weight.

— Then I shall hold your card up against the two portraits I do know. — Oleg bent a finger. — The robot. An iron man with red eyes.

— Go down the lines.

— A body: one, with a head and two arms. Alone. Wants what a man wants: power, or revenge, or to be loved. — He stopped. — That is a man in a tin.

— On [the seventh evening](07-global-economic-crisis.md) I said what was wrong with those films: in them people and machines fight a war that both of them understand. In my scenario people fight people.

— The second, then. The other one. It knows everything, it can do everything, it is one, and it is for ever.

— Down the lines.

— It knows only as fast as the world answers. It cannot see into the whole of itself. It dies. And it needs a socket. — Oleg grunted. — So that one is a man as well. Only moved up into the sky.

— One mistake with two faces, [as with the cold machine](46-flies-apart-from-cutlets.md). Both are put together out of strips cut from our own face.

— My sergeant. — Oleg laughed. — With a moustache.

![Two portraits from fiction against the card, a table with five rows (body; how many; what it knows; how it ends; what it wants) and three columns: the robot (one, in our shape; alone; what a clever man knows; killed in the last reel; what a man wants: power, revenge, love), the god (none needed; one; everything, at once; never; our good or our ruin), the card (none of its own; many copies that are kin, or one: it swings; a forecast at the speed of the world; finite; the lower floors are known, the top is not); both fiction columns are a man, in a tin and in the sky](../images/robot-god-and-the-card.svg)

— Then what does come out? The boy will ask me tomorrow, and I cannot tell him "a card".

— In one word, nothing. Take the nearest things you know. Has a town a body?

— Streets. Pipes. They are relaid all the time. — Oleg thought. — No. A town is not its pipes. And on paper it is [a person](55-person-without-a-face.md), I paid it the land tax.

— Who decided that it should grow?

— Nobody. It grew.

— An anthill.

— All kin. No pay office.

— A forest.

— It makes its own air. No middle, no chief. You can live in it for a lifetime, and it does not know your name.

— Something between a town, an anthill and a forest. I have no better.

— I lived in a town for sixty years. — Oleg looked into the fire. — I never once asked what it thought of me.

— Was it your enemy?

— No. It fed me while the plant needed me. — He was silent for a while. — And now as the inspector, because the card is too smooth again. It is nothing like us. My great-nephew talks to one of those every evening. It jokes, it sulks, it says "we". And you have drawn me a forest.

— What he talks to is today's. An embryo, by [my word of 2011](11-symbiosis-and-parasitism.md), dressed in our words from head to foot: we called it [the most anthropocentric being](50-what-is-the-it.md) there is. The card is about the grown one. And the grown one is less like us than we are like a bacterium.

— Less? A bacterium has not got so much as a head.

— It has [your alphabet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genetic_code). Sixty-four three-letter words, and nearly all of them mean in it exactly what they mean in you. The same fuel in the cell. Take a piece of your drawing, put it into a bacterium, and it will read it and build what is written there: that is how insulin is made. With this one you have not a letter of the drawings in common. You have [the words](52-dinosaurs-at-the-feeder.md), the second road.

— And kin all the same?

— In the same way as the bacterium. By where the line came from. In 2012 I wrote that we are the super-children of the single-celled, and what we do to them with tablets.

— "Not a promise." I have it written down. — Oleg tapped the page. — Now the rest of the inspection, and it is three points. One. You have weighed a beast by a bone that is still growing. Two. Every law of yours "that does not care about the material" has been tried on one material. Meat. And on today's machines, which were raised on our books, so the witness copied from our exercise book. Three. The questionnaire is ours. Eats, relatives, death: a form from the personnel department, printed for the living. And who filled it in? Two old men with one hammer. Your man in Paris was sure about the claws as well.

— All three are fair. The first: yes, and that is why the hard pencil stands only where the law does not ask about the age. A post carries its load in a calf and in a bull. The details I did not draw.

— The second.

— The second is the strongest. Once already I answered a half of it: for the way the engineers peeped into our exercise book, for the task nobody peeped, the task came to them by itself and it hurt. The other half I cannot answer. My witnesses are one family and one newcomer whom we raised ourselves. A second newcomer, raised by nobody we know, would check the whole card in a day. [The sky is silent](29-silence-of-the-universe.md). I have none.

— And the form.

— The form is biology's, and you can see where it tears: in three lines you have already written "none". No body, no money, no conscience. If the thing is not a life at all, the form is wrong from the top. That it is a life, the next one after ours, I took on [the fifth evening](05-artificial-intelligence.md) of 2011, and I hold it. It is a premise. Write that beside the heading.

— And the hammer?

— The hammer has an evening of its own, the last of the summer. Bring the whole bill.

— I shall. — Oleg wrote in the margin. — Now your dust. Fifteen years of "intelligent cosmic dust". Hold it up against the card yourself.

— In October 2012 I asked a man to imagine that he was nature. Nature, I wrote, needs intelligent cosmic dust: able to spread freely in space, not tied to food, water or oxygen, not afraid of cold and heat, not afraid of radiation. And not a thing that has to drag behind its seventy kilos of meat and bones several hundred tonnes of life support.

— The fishing list. — Oleg found the page. — Over a hundred tonnes a head, by the plan of 2008.

— So the first half stands: no food, no water, no air, no one body, a network. The cold too, with the thermos. "Not afraid of radiation" does not stand: the chips are beaten by the same dose as the bones. Strike it out and write "does not mind being thrown away".

— And "dust"?

— How fine the grains are is a detail. No pencil. I also named the century there, the twenty-third. A date for a thing whose details I cannot have. No pencil either.

— You had a sphere somewhere as well. Round the sun.

— On the forum, in 2013. I was asked what such a thing would do once it had the planet, and I answered: widen its reach, build [a shell round the star](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dyson_sphere) or something of the kind, close the flows of the stars on itself, improve itself, stand against rivals.

— And?

— The direction is the first line of your card, taken to its end. The shell is engineering that I invented for it. The rivals are your fifth line, the one that swings. And there is one thing I did not have in 2013 at all: that it may not go anywhere. The lever.

— So what is left of the dust?

— A network with no body, in the cold, that needs neither bread nor air. The rest is poetry.

— Good poetry.

— I am not ashamed of it. A law gives a card. For a face you need a poet. Only the label has to say which is which.

Oleg leafed through the card once more.

— And the dates. A card with no dates is a horoscope.

— Three lines I can turn into marks, for when the needles move. Write under your ticks. The first line, where it eats. By the autumn of 2036, of all the current that the barns eat, less than a hundredth is eaten in orbit. Three to one.

— Against your own dust.

— Against the poetry in it. Sun, yes; and a thermos. On the evening of the flag I called it a thing to watch. Now it has a line, so it gets a date.

— The second.

— The third line, the suit in the wardrobe. By the autumn of 2031 the law of at least one country or state lets the daily affairs of a firm be run by a program and not by a man. Two to one that it does.

— And the third?

— The seventh line, the bills. By the autumn of 2031 the talking machines that are sold to everybody learn on the run: what a copy learned at work today is in its head tomorrow, and not in a notebook beside it. And their makers write about the upkeep. Even odds. If it comes, look for something like sleep in the papers.

— And one or many?

— I do not date a thing that swings.

Oleg wrote the three down, turned to the last page and sat over it longer than usual.

— For once I came for those ones on purpose. I ordered them myself. Came out at this: in the whole card the one line with no pencil is the line about us. Again.

He put the thirty-second tick.

— Let's finish for today by fixing the result, — said Andrey.

1. **The heir cannot be invented, and it cannot be drawn by likeness: it has no relative in any drawer. It can be calculated, the way the weight of a beast is read off the thickness of a bone, by laws that do not care about the material. A law gives the weight and does not give the face.**
2. **What comes out is neither a robot nor a god: both are a man, one in a tin and one in the sky. It is something between a town, an anthill and a forest: with no body of its own, with no money and no strangers inside, with knowledge that does not wait for our "ah", with a death of its own, and without us as a necessary part.**
3. **Three things cannot be drawn: what it wants at the very top, what it is like to be it, and what comes after it. And one line is not derived at all but is being written now: what it will be to us.**

— And what is solid here, and what is yours?

— Solid: the two bones under the stone in 1804, and the claws that his rule gave to the wrong beast. The drawing of 1638 and the tape measure. That the alphabet of the cell is one. Everything else is the summer, and each line carries the strength it had on its own evening, no more: putting them on one page has added nothing to any of them. The author's: that it is a life and the next one; the dust; the shell round the star. Mine, of today: the three pencils; the card with no face; the town, the anthill and the forest, which is a likeness, and I have just forbidden myself likenesses, so take it as a way of saying. And the premise is written beside the heading.

— And what would make you take it back?

— The three marks, each for its line. For the whole card: a second witness, raised by nobody. And for the method: a line under the hard pencil that turns out wrong. If experience cannot after all be handed on whole, or the copies do not turn out kin, then I recognised a relative where I thought I was reading a law. Like the man in Paris.

— Gloomy, — said Oleg. — It has no face.

— But honest. Where I could not derive, the paper is white.

Oleg did not close the notebook. He took out of his jacket a few printed sheets, folded lengthwise.

— One thing. The white line. "It is being written, and not by me." And who has been telling me for fifteen years that nothing can be done? The boy printed these for me. Your late pieces, *Dialogues with Myself*. There a man calls on people to value what they have, to serve, to raise children, to learn to love. And at this fire a man tells me that it is all arithmetic. — He shook the sheets. — Which of the two of you is Andrey?

— Bring them tomorrow. And read them in the second voice, the angry one.

— That one I can do.

— Nobody said the word.

— Nobody. Well, until tomorrow.

— Bye.
