# A Machine for Making People Good

<!-- import:pending -->

> Andrey remembers a teenage dream: a device that rewrites people's wants and makes everyone good. Why was the dream abandoned, and what does it say about today's hopes of controlling AI? Good imposed against people's will is not good. A control tool outlives its maker. Whether AI is "friendly" matters less than whose interests it serves.
