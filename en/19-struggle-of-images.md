# The Struggle of Images for Existence

<!-- import:pending -->

> Memory is always scarce, and the images in it compete: the weak ones are erased, the strong ones spread. Andrey suggests that images — ideas, skills, knowledge — are a form of life with their own life cycle. Like any life, they can move to a new platform. What happens to human knowledge once machines become its main carrier?
