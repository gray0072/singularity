# Fifteen Years Later

<!-- import:pending -->

> Oleg returns to the campfire with a list of the forecasts made in 2011: human-level AI by 2018, population growth stopping, the human share of value falling to zero. Practice is the criterion of truth, so Andrey checks each forecast against what has happened since. Where did the trend hold, where was the date wrong, and what do large language models say about the dynamics?
