# The Silence of the Universe

<!-- import:pending -->

> Why does SETI hear nothing? Andrey returns to the question he took to astronomers in 2012 and answers the strongest objection to the concept, Panov's. A mind far above ours may not look like "little green men" at all. Like the ant that cannot see the human, we may be looking straight at it. Post-singular life may be intelligent cosmic dust, not a civilisation with radio stations.
