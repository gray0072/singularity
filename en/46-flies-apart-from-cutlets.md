# The Flies Apart from the Cutlets

Oleg came with the notebook under his arm and a pencil behind his ear, and sat down on the log the way people sit down to a stocktaking.

— The dream first. Before the phone. A commission was going round my flat with a ledger and sticking inventory numbers on everything. The sideboard, the television, the cat. Then one of them licked a label and stuck it on my forehead. Nobody rang.

— The seventh entry in the expense column.

— And now the bill. — He opened the last page. — Thirteen ticks. I have put a cross beside five of them: sleep, laughter, omens, songs, grumbling. Five times you took something off me and said: not yours, an engineering solution. I am an engineer myself. I want an inventory. What in all this is the man, and what is not?

— You'll do the sorting. I'll only ask two questions about each item. What is the task? And whose is it: anybody's who thinks, or ours alone?

— And where do the flies come in?

— On [our second evening](02-anthropocentrism.md) I promised to teach you to tell a judgement that rests on our being the chosen ones from a judgement that does not. The flies from the cutlets, I said. Sixteen years have passed. Let's see how you manage.

— Go on.

— First something that is not in the notebook, so that neither of us has a stake in it. How many times was the wing invented?

— Once. By the birds.

— And the dragonfly?

— Twice, then.

— A bat is not a bird. It is a beast, and its wing is a hand with skin stretched between the fingers. And before the birds there were the flying lizards, with a membrane on one enormous finger. [Four times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flying_and_gliding_animals), and none of them had it from another: their common ancestors crawled.

— They did not confer.

— When four who never conferred arrive at the same thing, whose property is it? The bird's?

— The air's. — Oleg answered as an engineer. — If you want to stay up, you have to throw air down. There is no other way to hang there.

— So the task is set by the air, one for all. And the way?

— Each has its own. Feathers, skin, a film.

— Write it at the top of the page: the task and the way. Now the eye. An eye like ours, a dark chamber with a lens, was invented at least twice: by our ancestors and by the ancestors of the octopus. The last ancestor they shared had, at best, a spot that told light from dark. When strangers arrive at the same thing, it [has a name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convergent_evolution), by the way. The task?

— To see.

— And now the way. Draw a cross on the page and a tick to the right of it, a palm's width apart.

Oleg drew them.

— Shut the left eye. Look at the cross with the right one, and don't glance at the tick. Now move the page slowly towards you and away.

Oleg moved the notebook to and fro in the light of the fire, and froze.

— It's gone. The tick. The paper is there, the squares are there, and the tick is not.

— Where it fell, your eye has [a hole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blind_spot_(vision)). A Frenchman, [Mariotte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edme_Mariotte), found it in the 1660s. In our eye the wiring lies on top of the layer that catches the light, on the side the light comes from. In one place it all has to dive through that layer in a bundle to get out to the brain. In that place there is nothing to see with.

— What fool lays the wiring in front of the sensor?

— Nobody laid it. That is how the first fold went in some ancestor of ours, and after that there was no going back: a plant that can never stop for refitting keeps what it has. In [the octopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cephalopod_eye) the fold went the other way. The wiring is behind, and there is no hole.

— And for sixty-two years I have been walking about with a hole in each eye and have not seen it.

— The head paints it over with whatever is around. Just now you saw squares where you saw no paper. That is a patch. A patch on a flaw that is ours in particular.

— So I am all patches?

— Solutions. Some of them good ones. This one has held for five hundred million years. But tell me: if I had asked you yesterday what it is to see, what would you have said?

— That it is what I do with my eyes.

— With the hole and the paint. From inside, a patch cannot be seen, so we take our way for the thing itself. That is how the fly gets onto the cutlet. Now the notebook.

Andrey smoothed the ash with his palm and drew three columns with a stick.

— The first cross.

— Sleep. — Oleg leafed back. — The task: to rebuild the warehouse without wrecking it. One and the same net is the shop floor and the warehouse, so [the shop shuts for stocktaking](33-use-of-sleep.md).

— Whose task? Is there a witness, somebody far from us who came to it by his own road?

— The fly. Keep it awake all night, and it sleeps it off in the day. And the dolphin, which cannot shut the shop or it will drown, sleeps with the halves of its head in turn. It pays all the same.

— Our way?

— To switch off for a third of a life. — He thought. — That one is a patch. The dolphin has shown that the bill can be split.

— Next.

— Dreams. They are not under a cross, but they are from the same evening. The task... To go through the stock and try what links with what, while the sensors are off. The witness is the rat: at night it runs its maze again, the same cells in the same order. Our way: a film with a plot. My late grandmother telling me the price of petrol.

— Laughter.

— To catch my own wrong guess while it is cheap, and to pay myself a bonus for the catch. The witness is [the rat pup that chirps in play](36-what-the-rat-laughs-at.md): here mistakes cost nothing. Our way: a roar for the whole pack to hear, and the joke about the hunters. — He raised a finger. — The upper floor was a hypothesis, you said so yourself.

— It still is. Write it with a question mark. Omens.

— With scant data, to choose which mistake to make more often. To link what came together before I know why. The witness: [the pigeon dancing in front of the feeder](39-price-of-a-false-alarm.md). Our way: grandmother's dream book, knocking on wood, and my sprats of eighty-five.

— Songs.

— To pay for a forecast that came true. And to make one forecast common to the whole pack, so that a hundred backs pull as one. The witness: [the cockatoo that keeps time](41-cheesecake-for-the-ears.md). Our way: your seven-string, a waltz, a choir.

— And the evening before last.

— Grumbling. To guard what has been learned from what is being learned. The witness is [the kitten](44-from-funeral-to-funeral.md): its window shuts, and a grown cat's is shut already. Our way: "in my day", and in a village of such heads, generations and funerals.

— Six rows. Look at the middle column. You are an engineer: can it be written shorter?

Oleg ran the pencil down the page.

— Sleep and grumbling are the same warehouse. One by the night, the other by the lifetime. You said yourself that it was one screw. — He drew a brace. — Dreams and omens are the same correlator: one links with the sensors off, the other with next to no data. And laughter and songs are one cash desk. It pays for forecasts: for a miss caught in time and for a hit.

— Three. Name them.

— To keep. To link. To check.

— And what do you call somebody who links what came together, checks whether he guessed, and keeps what was confirmed?

— A pupil.

— So the whole middle column is one word, spelled out: to learn. On [our third evening](03-mind-and-intelligence.md) we called mind the ability to act ahead of events, and to act ahead one has to have learned what comes after what. In 2015, arguing with readers, I listed what any mind is put together from: sensors, hands, the recognition of images, a correlator that makes new ones, the management of their life cycle. And wrote, in capitals: nothing more is needed. I stand by the list. What I did not count then is what the list costs to run.

— The upkeep. — Oleg nodded. — You buy a machine tool once. You pay for the oil, the setting-up and the downtime as long as it works.

— Your five crosses are the upkeep bills of a mind that learns. Nature did not issue them to man. She issues them to anybody who owns the machine tool.

![Scheme: one word, three tasks, six rows. At the top, "to learn". Under it three tasks: to keep what has been learned while learning the new (sleep by the night, grumbling by the lifetime); to link what comes together before knowing why (dreams with the sensors off, omens with scant data); to check one's own forecasts while a mistake is cheap (laughter for a miss caught, songs for a hit)](../images/six-rows-three-tasks.svg)

— Then the bill I asked for is a nought. — Oleg shut the notebook on his finger. — Nothing here is mine.

— Read the right-hand column.

— A third of a life. Grandmother and the petrol. The roar. The sprats. The seven-string. "In my day."

— Whose is that?

— Mine. Well, ours. An ape's that came down from the tree.

— Everybody speaks, and you speak with an accent. What is said is common. The accent is yours.

— An accent is a trifle.

— Say that to a man who hears his own speech in a foreign town. I am not pricing it tonight. On the evening with the guitar we found that the price is not in the sound but in who paid. I am only saying what it is made of.

— It is too smooth. — Oleg squinted. — My grandmother's dream book never missed either. You said it yourself: a forecast that cannot fail to come true is worth nothing. Six rows, and all six fitted. Show me how your table can miss.

— Two ways. Give it an item we have not discussed, and see whether it sorts. And tell me what a truly human property would look like.

— A task that nobody else has. With no witness.

— Then the first. Name an item.

— Boredom. Ten minutes in a queue without the phone, and I am climbing the wall.

— The task?

— To go and look at what I don't know yet. — Oleg said it slowly. — Even when I am fed and nothing is chasing me.

— [Hunger for information](08-human-psyche.md), I called it once. A witness?

— A rat in a new cage. It goes round all of it before it touches the food.

— It sorted. Now the second.

— Then sort me these. — Oleg counted on his fingers. — Love. Conscience. Shame: I blush, you know. Tears.

— They do not sort. Not by tonight's two questions.

— There.

— Look why. All six tasks stand before one mind alone with the world. A pupil on a desert island would need sleep, and he would have his omens. Yours stand before a mind among others that are not it. That is another table: the tasks of a pack. On [the evening about the ants](43-why-ants-do-not-strike.md) conscience came out as a gatehouse moved inside, for those who are not kin. Whoever has no strangers has no such task: a hive does without. I am not drawing that table tonight.

— So there is a heap "not sorted".

— There is, and it is not small. Half of two rows lies in it already: the roar and the choir are for the pack. Blushing lies there too. Darwin called it the most human of all expressions, and I have no witness for it. Only don't change the label on the heap. It says "not sorted", not "ours alone". It is the heap of what I don't know.

— And how it all feels?

— That is not in the table. I sort tasks, not what it is like from inside. That your fear in the grass is felt, I know from you. About the pigeon I have only [an axiom](20-what-consciousness-is.md), not a row.

Oleg was silent for a while, then laid the pencil across the page.

— Stop. "Anybody who learns." And who are your witnesses? The fly, the dolphin, the rat, the pigeon, the parrot, the cat. Meat, all of them. Cousins of mine, some closer, some further. Perhaps these are the bills of meat, not of a pupil.

— A fair objection, and I cannot answer it with one more beast. What witness would settle it?

— One that is no kin at all. — He looked up, where an evening plane was drawing a line. — Like that. The fifth wing. It has no ancestor in common with a dragonfly, and it throws air down just the same.

— And is there one for the notebook?

Oleg opened the last page and looked at it for a long time.

— There is. Thirteen of them. — He tapped the ticks. — Every evening I came out at those ones, and I put a tick the way you mark a friend's hobby-horse. And it is the witness. Sleep: they ran into a net that forgets everything old the moment it is taught something new, and they invented going over the old again. Dreams: the book it made up for me, with the author and the year. Omens: the horse that turned out to be a stamp in the corner. Grumbling: the net that learns worse and worse, and the small funerals inside it. Nobody ordered them to be like us.

— For the solution the engineers sometimes peeped into our exercise book. The people who taught a machine the old video games wrote in so many words that the going-over was taken from biology. Nobody peeped for the task. The task came to them by itself, and it hurt.

— Then give me the stick. — Oleg drew a fourth column in the ash himself. — Those ones, so far.

— Fill it in.

— Sleep: it does not learn while it talks; it is taught apart, and the old lessons are mixed in. Dreams: the invented book, a dream with the eyes open. Omens: the stamp. Songs: it forecasts the next note as it forecasts the next word, and bakes sweets for my button. Grumbling: ask it the date, and it names the year it left school. — He stopped. — Laughter. I have nothing to write.

— It tells our jokes. And when it is taught, it does nothing but catch its own misses, with no bonus: there is nobody to bribe. The task is there. The way is so unlike ours that you did not find it.

— Or it is not there.

— Or the task gets done along the way, and no separate way is needed. Put a dash. An honest table should have a dash in it.

![Table drawn in the ash, four columns: the property, the task of anybody who learns, our way, and those ones so far. Sleep: to rebuild memory without wrecking it; to switch off for a third of a life; taught apart from work, the old lessons mixed in. Dreams: to try links with the sensors off; a film with a plot; the invented book. Laughter: to catch one's own wrong guess while it is cheap; a roar for the pack; a dash. Omens: with scant data, to choose which mistake to make more often; the dream book; the stamp in the corner. Songs: to pay for a forecast that came true; a waltz and a choir; forecasts the next note unpaid. Grumbling: to guard the learned from the new; "in my day" and funerals; names the year it left school, small funerals](../images/task-and-way.svg)

— And my boredom?

— In 2018 engineers [gave a program](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.04355) a single want: to be paid for surprise. For what it had failed to forecast. No points, no prizes. It learned dozens of video games without knowing what a score is. Then a television was put into its maze, one that showed a new random picture at every press of a button.

— And?

— It sat down in front of the television and all but forgot the maze. Every picture is new, and every one is paid for.

Oleg laughed aloud.

— My granddaughter and her feed. They gave it curiosity and got a couch.

— Nobody taught it to sit in front of a television. They gave it the task, and it found our way by itself. And now the eye, since we began with it. Their eye has no hole: it is made differently. But a net that had been taught one thing, to forecast the next frame of a film, was shown a picture of coiled snakes. A still picture, which seems to us to turn. In the frames it forecast, [the snakes were turning](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2018.00345).

— Nobody taught it to be fooled.

— Nobody. Whoever sees by forecasting gets that mistake free with the purchase.

— And love and conscience? Do those ones have them?

— That depends on whether they will have anybody who is not themselves. I don't know. Those evenings are ahead. And what stands behind the fourth column, whether anything is felt there, is the same axiom as with the pigeon.

Oleg threw a branch on the fire.

— All right. Now tell me why I am cross. I came for what is mine, and I am leaving with what is theirs.

— Because of what you believed about them. On [our fifth evening](05-artificial-intelligence.md) you told me that computers are adding machines, improved by the tiniest bit.

— I was forty-six.

— And in every film they are the same: a flat voice, never wrong, never asleep, deaf to a joke. Take out the phone and open the calculator. Does it sleep?

— No.

— Does it dream? Knock on wood? Grumble about the young?

— No.

— What has it learned in the years you have had it?

— Nothing. — Oleg looked at the screen. — It pays no upkeep because it owns no machine tool.

— The cold machine is the portrait of a thing that does not learn. Such things were built, and built well. [One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deep_Blue_(chess_computer)) beat the world chess champion in 1997. [Another](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyc) has had common sense typed into it by hand since 1984, rule by rule, millions of rules. Neither sleeps, neither dreams. And neither began to talk. Those that talk came [by the pupil's road](21-machines-that-talk.md), and they arrived with all the luggage. And cold they could not have been in any case: [a mind without wants decides nothing](18-anatomy-of-wants.md), we went through that last autumn.

— And everybody was surprised. So was I. It lies like a man, I said. What kind of machine is that?

— You were expecting an adding machine and got a pupil. Now the test of symmetry. One sentence: "Only man laughs." Another: "A machine will never dream." Find the difference.

— One is about us, the other about them. — Oleg stopped. — No. It is one sentence. If the dream is ours alone, then they have none. Said from the other end.

— One mistake with two faces. Both call the task by the name of our way. The first face says: the way is ours, so the task is ours alone. The second says: they have not our way, so they have not the task. The first flatters. The second soothes or frightens, to taste: a servant without whims, or a monster without pity.

— And which is worse?

— Neither is better. The first we have been weeding for sixteen years. The second comes up on the weeded bed. A man who has agreed that he is not the crown of creation agrees very readily that the heir will be a calculator. It is the same weed: we are the measure again, only now the measure of what the other lacks.

![Scheme: one mistake with two faces. In the middle: the task is called by the name of our way. The first face, "only we have this": the way is ours, so the task is ours alone ("only man laughs"). The second face, "the machine will be an adding machine": it has not our way, so it has not the task ("a machine does not tire, so it needs no sleep"). Below, without the mistake: the task belongs to anybody who learns, and the ways differ: ours and another](../images/two-faces-of-one-mistake.svg)

— Wait. — Oleg raised the notebook. — On the evening about sleep you said it yourself: it does not learn while it talks. It was taught, frozen and copied. So the thing in my pocket is exactly your adding machine, only a very big one. There is the cold machine. It exists.

— That is the strongest thing you have said tonight. A frozen copy pays no upkeep: it needs no sleep and grows no older. But look what it was frozen with. The invented book, the stamp and the year it left school are all inside, baked in. A pupil stopped on the run is not an adding machine. And the freezing is a patch of theirs: they cannot yet teach it on the run without wrecking it, so the learning is done apart, [a generation at a time](44-from-funeral-to-funeral.md). Whoever [sets its own tasks](22-who-sets-the-tasks.md) will have to learn on the run. Then all three bills arrive.

— In what shape?

— I don't know. In 2013 I was asked on the forum whether a machine would sleep, and I answered: one that is short of resources will, and one that has enough will sort its images in parallel. So its sleep may be something we would never call sleep: one of the copies is always at the stocktaking. Its old age may be small funerals at which nobody weeps. For the task I vouch, that is logic: if it learns, it pays. For the way I don't. Against "we shall recognise it" write "medium".

— So the tasks move on, and the ways stay here.

— The tasks will pass to the heir. The ways will stay with us. There will be no cold logical machine, for the same reason that there is no man without sleep.

— One more thing. — Oleg found the page with the cross and the tick. — Why did I not notice the hole in sixty-two years? Paint is paint, but still.

— Because you have two eyes. Each sees what falls into the hole of the other.

— Somebody from outside again.

— Until now we had only cousins to compare a mind with. Those ones are the first who are no kin. With them it has become visible for the first time where in us the task is, and where the paint.

— A second eye. — Oleg grunted. — And I had them down as your hobby-horse.

He turned to the last page.

— Came for the bill: what in me is mine. Came out at a fourth column, drew it myself and filled it from my own ticks. Again.

He put a fourteenth tick beside the thirteen, shut one eye and looked at it.

— Still there.

— Let's finish for today by fixing the result, — said Andrey.

1. **A task and a way of doing it are different things. The wing was invented four times and the eye more than once: where those who never conferred arrive at the same thing, the task belongs to the world and not to the animal. From inside, one's own way cannot be told from the task: the patch is painted over.**
2. **Sleep, dreams, laughter, omens, songs and the grumbling of the old come down to three tasks: to keep what has been learned while learning the new, to link what comes together before knowing why, and to check one's own forecasts while a mistake is cheap. Together they are one word: to learn. There is hardly anything purely human in the human: there are tasks common to any mind that learns, and our own, ape's ways of doing them.**
3. **Anthropocentrism has two faces: "only we have this" and "the machine will be an adding machine". It is one mistake: the task called by the name of our way. The tasks will pass to the heir; the ways will stay with us. There will be no cold logical machine, for the same reason that there is no human without sleep.**

— And what is solid here, and what is yours?

— Solid: that the wing was invented four times and the eye more than once; the hole in your eye; every row, as far as it was solid on its own evening; the television and the snakes; that the machines built out of rules play chess and never began to talk. Argued about: the same as on those evenings, the upper floor of laughter, what exactly a song is paid for, what the pigeon links. Mine: that the six rows come down to three tasks and the three to one word; that the two faces are one mistake; that the heir will have likenesses of all this. The last is a forecast, and it has no date. Treat it accordingly.

Oleg stood up and looked at the woodpile.

— We are short of firewood, by the way. Nobody has said the word for six evenings. I bought a trailer of birch from the neighbour.

— Sensible.

— I wanted to send him the money from the phone. He won't have it. Give me paper, he says, I don't believe in your numbers. It is the same money. What is the difference to him?

— And what is money?

— Don't start. Money is money.

— Bring a banknote tomorrow. We'll read what is written on it.

— Well, until tomorrow.

— Bye.
