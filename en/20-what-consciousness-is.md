# What Consciousness Is

<!-- import:pending -->

> In 2011 Andrey defined consciousness as a system's ability to build a model of itself in the world. Oleg brings the question back: can a machine be conscious, or does it only imitate? Andrey answers in his usual way: the question is badly posed. Consciousness, like mind, comes in degrees, and the line between "real" and "imitated" is drawn by anthropocentrism.
