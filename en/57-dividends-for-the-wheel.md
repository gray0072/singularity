# Dividends for the Wheel

Oleg came with the dog, the notebook and a garden wheelbarrow, and the wheelbarrow could be heard from the gate: one squeak to every turn of the wheel.

— The dream first. The school library. I hand in a book, the librarian opens it at the back, where the little pocket is, and says: "This was due back in 1978. There is a fine." I look at the cover, and it is my own notebook. Nobody rang.

— The eighteenth dream that did not come true, in the expense column. And the letter from the court?

— "Wait for a letter" means wait for a letter. — Oleg tipped the birch logs out by the fire and turned the wheelbarrow upside down, wheel in the air. — There. You wanted a wheel. I pushed it all the way here and thought about whom I owe.

— What did you pay for it?

— Two thousand nine hundred, at the builders' market. Last spring.

— To whom?

— The till. And further on, the shop, the works that pressed the tray, whoever cast the tyre, the lorry driver.

— And the one who thought of the wheel?

— He was not at the till.

— How much birch was in it?

— Six armfuls.

— Without it you would have walked six times. Five walks saved. Where did they come from?

— From the wheel.

— In 2011, under [the seventh evening](../sources/garya/sqlru/07-global-economic-crisis.md), I put that to a reader, only with a stick. A man finds a stick and whittles a digging stick out of it. He does not sell it, he digs with it himself. And he finds that with his bare palms the pit would have taken several times as long as the whittling and the digging together. Where did the saved time come from? Did it fall from the sky? Whose doing is it: the inventor's, the stick's, or both? And I added: to whom are the dividends for the use of the wheel to be paid, if its inventor passed away thousands of years ago? I promised to take it up in the next posts.

— And?

— Fifteen years. It is a debt.

— Then pay. — Oleg sat down on the log and spun the wheel with a finger. — My answer is simple, and it is the same as for Pushkin. A man thought of it, so the man is owed. He is dead: then his children. That is fair, and that is how it is with my house.

— The reader said the same, and more sharply than you. The maker of a spade is paid for the spade once. But the spade goes on digging potatoes for its new owner, and of that the maker sees nothing, though by rights he should be in the share for as long as the spade works.

— Well, should he not?

— Let's try to pay him. The wheel first, since it is here. Who gets the money?

— The inventor's heirs.

— The oldest wheel ever dug up [lay in a marsh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ljubljana_Marshes_Wheel) near Ljubljana: ash, on an oak axle, a little over five thousand years old. The first drawings of carts are of the same age or a little older. Say five and a half thousand years. How many generations is that?

— Two hundred. Give or take.

— In 2004 three Americans [worked out on a computer](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature02842) how the family trees of everybody alive tangle together as you go back. By their models, a few thousand years back you come to a time when every person then living is either an ancestor of everybody alive today or of nobody. Between four and seven and a half thousand years ago, depending on the model, and they call the estimate extremely tentative themselves. The wheel is five and a half.

— So his heirs...

— If he left any, and if the models are anywhere near, his heirs are very nearly all of us. You, me, the girl at the till.

— Then everybody is owed.

— And who is to pay?

— Whoever uses the wheel. — Oleg stopped. — Everybody.

— Everybody pays everybody. That has a shorter name: nobody pays, and the wheel is cheap. Your dividend was paid out twenty minutes ago. Five walks.

— That is a trick with arithmetic. Give me a nearer example, with a name.

— In a moment. First: was it he who thought of it? What does a wheel do?

— It rolls. So that you need not drag.

— Rolling in place of dragging. After [a talk in 2012](../sources/garya/sqlru/2012-05-17-seti-talk.md) a listener told me that the wheel, at least, is man's own. Have you seen tumbleweed? It sows its seed by rolling. A dung beetle rolls a ball that it could never drag. And the lever, for which you would have to pay Archimedes, you carry in every joint: the muscle shortens by a finger's breadth, and the hand travels half a metre.

— So I pay the beetle.

— [The spade's turn to speak](02-anthropocentrism.md), as in that first autumn. Under [the twelfth evening](../sources/garya/sqlru/12-value-shares.md) another reader wanted me to count into the price of a loaf the share of everybody who had ever worked towards it: the suppliers down the chain, and "the Ancients", Aristotle and Archimedes, who got the first knowledge. I asked him why the ancients have to be counted from people. Why not from the apes, from the first creatures with a backbone, from the bacteria? Whoever counts backwards stops on the step where a man appears: the first, the third or the hundredth, so long as there is a man on it.

— And where do you stop?

— On the last one, where the work is actually being done. Otherwise you get an oddity: a man who died long ago and is lying in his coffin goes on producing something. The one who produces is the one who produces.

— The dead don't dig.

— They don't. Then who earned the five walks?

— I did. With the barrow. — Oleg scratched his chin. — [The union](37-who-exploits-whom.md), as you called it: me together with the barrow. The two of us together carry more than the two of us apart, and what is over gets divided.

— Between whom?

— Between those who are in it. I got the walks.

— And the barrow?

— What can a barrow get? — He looked at the wheel with new suspicion. — It got bought. And when it rusts through I'll buy another, with a tyre that does not go flat.

— Under the twelfth evening I wrote it like this: part of what the union gains goes to restoring, reproducing and developing spades, and part goes to the man, as time saved. Two in the union, two shares. A third share, for the one who thought of it, is not there. Nobody took it away from him. He is not in the union.

— It is still not fair. The whole gain came out of his head.

— I am not saying fair. I am saying where it goes. One economist [counted it](https://www.nber.org/papers/w10433) for the American economy over half a century, from 1948 to 2001: of everything that new inventions added, those who made them kept about two parts in a hundred. By his own widest reckoning, three.

— And the other ninety-eight?

— Went past them, to whoever buys. As a lower price. The man who gained most from the ballpoint pen is not the man who invented it. It is everybody who writes.

Oleg threw a branch into the fire.

— All right, the wheel is old, and there were beetles. Now with a name. Pushkin rolled no dung. He sat and wrote, and it is his from the first letter to the last.

— And he was the first man in Russia to live by it. In 1824 the Moscow booksellers paid three thousand roubles for one poem of his, and a newspaper worked it out: five roubles a line. That same year he wrote a bookseller talking to a poet: "Inspiration is not for sale, but a manuscript can be sold." And the poet answers, in prose: you are perfectly right, here is my manuscript, let us come to terms.

— So he was paid.

— While he could collect. In that same 1824 a state councillor in Petersburg reprinted his "Prisoner of the Caucasus", with a German translation beside it, and never asked. Pushkin [complained](https://rvb.ru/pushkin/01text/10letters/1815_30/01text/1827/1417_234.htm): the booksellers had been offering him three thousand for a second edition, and now it was lost. He was answered that there is no law in Russia against the reprinting of books.

— None?

— The first came four years later, in 1828: the author for life, his heirs for twenty-five years after his death, and after that the works become, as it said, the property of the public. He was killed in 1837, owing about a hundred and forty thousand. The twenty-five years would have run out in 1862. In 1856 his widow asked for the right to be prolonged for her two sons, to the end of their lives. She was given something else: a general law, fifty years after death for every author. Pushkin's term ran out on the twenty-ninth of January 1887.

— And?

— A Petersburg publisher had all of Pushkin ready for that day: ten small volumes at fifteen kopecks, a rouble and a half the lot. Ten times cheaper than before. A crowd stood at his shop before it opened. People [climbed on the tables](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/4476762), got behind the counters and took their own change. By eleven the furniture was broken, the desk with the till lay on its side, and the chief of police sent men to let the public in by turns. Some ten thousand sets went in a day, by the count of the publisher's own paper. Chekhov complained in a letter that the first edition had gone in one day, so that he could not get a Pushkin even for himself.

— For a rouble and a half.

— In the fifty years before that day, [historians reckon](https://books.openedition.org/ledizioni/12916), no more than some sixty thousand copies of Pushkin had been sold in the whole of Russia. In the one year 1887, by a publisher's count of the time, nearly a million and a half.

— So who was Pushkin's heir?

— You tell me.

— Everybody with a rouble and a half to spare. — Oleg whistled softly. — The dividend for Pushkin. In cheapness, like the wheel. Only here I can see the day it began to be paid.

— And who fixed the day?

— The law. Fifty years.

— Why fifty? Why not for ever? Your house goes to your grandchildren for ever.

— Because a house... — Oleg faltered. — I don't know. I have never asked.

— In 1710 the English parliament passed [the first law of this kind](https://avalon.law.yale.edu/18th_century/anne_1710.asp). Until then a London bookseller who had entered a book in the register of his guild held it for ever. The law gave a new book fourteen years, and fourteen more if the author was still alive. After that it is anybody's. Look at what it is called: "An Act for the Encouragement of Learning".

— Not "for the protection of writers".

— Bait. So that books get written. And the length of the bait is how much those who write the law want new books, set against how much they want cheap ones. [A want written down.](24-last-profession.md) For an invention the term today is twenty years from the application, nearly all over the world. For a book, the author's life and seventy years more.

— It has grown, for books.

— Whoever holds the rights asks for longer, as the widow did. Whoever reads has nobody to ask on his behalf. But even the longest term ends.

![For how long the law pays the one who made it: five bars on a scale of years; the wheel, about 5,500 years ago, nobody and never; a new book in England in 1710, 14 years and 14 more if the author is alive; an invention today, 20 years from the application; Pushkin, 50 years after his death, until 1887; a book today, the author's life and 70 years more; when the term runs out the thing belongs to everybody](../images/dividends-have-a-term.svg)

— So the wheel missed its twenty years.

— Not quite. In 2001 a lawyer in Australia [filed for a patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/AU2001100012A4/en) on a "circular transportation facilitation device", to show how the new, simplified patents were being handed out. He was granted it. That autumn he and the patent office shared the prize that is given for things that first make people laugh and then make them think. The patent was later quietly revoked.

— A patent on the wheel. — Oleg looked at the barrow. — And I never paid.

— Now, why the term ends at all. In 1813 an American who had himself sat on the board that granted patents was asked about a patent on machinery for flour mills. He [answered](https://founders.archives.gov/documents/Jefferson/03-06-02-0322) with a candle. "He who receives an idea from me, receives instruction himself without lessening mine; as he who lights his taper at mine, receives light without darkening me." So inventions "cannot, in nature, be a subject of property". Society may give an exclusive right to the profits, as an encouragement, but that "may or may not be done, according to the will and convenience of the society, without claim or complaint from anybody".

Oleg pulled a burning twig out of the fire and lit another from it.

— Mine is no shorter.

— That is the whole difference from your house. Two families cannot live in one house without each of them having less of it.

— I have a candle like that of my own, come to think of it. Eighty-four, my second year at the plant. I made a template: one go at a casting where the inspector used to take five measurements. A rationalisation proposal. They gave me forty roubles and a certificate.

— By the rules of those years it was counted from the saving of the first year. Only the first. And the template?

— Was hanging on its nail when I retired. And the plant next door made themselves one from our drawing, and nobody asked them for anything.

— Forty roubles, once.

— I was glad of them. — Oleg thought for a moment. — And Pushkin's fifty years: what did they guard, exactly?

— Not the old man and the golden fish. By the view that has held among scholars since the 1930s, that story [came to him from a German tale](https://feb-web.ru/feb/pushkin/serial/vr1/vr12134-.htm) which a painter had written down in Pomerania and the brothers Grimm had printed, most likely through a French translation. And not Tsar Saltan: in November 1824 he wrote down seven tales from his old nurse, and that is one of them.

— And who paid the nurse?

— Nobody. Nor the women she had heard them from. The law guards the words in their order, for a term. What the words say it does not guard for a single day. The American law [puts it as a list](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/17/102): in no case does the protection extend "to any idea, procedure, process, system, method of operation, concept, principle, or discovery".

— So an idea has never had an owner at all.

— Not for an hour. A way of doing a thing has one for twenty years, if somebody takes the trouble to apply. Words in their order, for a life and seventy years. Everything else is common from the first day: [the language](50-what-is-the-it.md), the multiplication table, how to harden steel, how to bake bread, the wheel.

— And how much of it is there, of "everything else"?

— An economist tried to weigh it in 1957. He took the American economy from 1909 to 1949. In those forty years what a worker turned out in an hour doubled. He asked how much of the doubling came from there being more machines to each worker.

— Most of it. The machines did it.

— [One eighth.](https://doi.org/10.2307/1926047) Seven eighths came from something else. He called it "technical change" and said honestly that this was shorthand for any shift of any kind: speed-ups, slowdowns, better schooling of the workers, "all sorts of things".

— Hold on. That is a remainder. He subtracted what he could count and gave the rest a name. Our planners had a line like that: "other expenses".

— It is a remainder, and the trade knows it. A year earlier a colleague of his got the same and [called it](https://www.nber.org/system/files/chapters/c5650/c5650.pdf) "some sort of measure of our ignorance". I do not say that the seven eighths are knowledge, weighed on a balance. I say this: when the machines and the hours have been counted, most of the growth is still lying on the table, and what is lying there is whatever makes the same iron and the same hands turn out more. The first of them got the Nobel prize for it. Some thirty years after him another man got one for saying what the remainder is made of: instructions. A design, a recipe, a drawing: once it has been made, it [can be used](https://doi.org/10.1086/261725) "over and over again at no additional cost".

— The candle again.

— The candle, in the journals.

![Where the growth came from, and who kept it: two bars, each a whole split in two; output per hour of work in the United States doubled between 1909 and 1949, one eighth of it from more machines per worker and seven eighths from everything else, which Solow called technical change; of the gain from new inventions in the United States between 1948 and 2001 those who invented kept 2.2 per cent and about 98 per cent went to everybody else as lower prices; the seven eighths are a remainder, some sort of measure of our ignorance](../images/where-the-gain-came-from.svg)

— Wait. — Oleg raised a finger. — In the autumn of 2011 you told me that [the surplus is made by the technosphere](06-karl-marx-mistake.md). The spade digs too. And now it is knowledge.

— What is a barrow?

— Iron. A wheel.

— Iron laid out by an instruction. Melt it down: the iron is all there, and the barrow is gone. In the summer we called that part [the drawings](45-lamarck-was-right.md). The technosphere is iron and drawings, and by that economist's weighing the drawings are the heavier part. I take nothing back. I am saying what most of it is made of: of something that has never been anybody's.

— Then here is what I don't understand. If the main capital belongs to nobody, what have we been living on? Somebody collected.

— Who collected on the wheel this evening?

— I did. I pushed.

— Could the barrow have brought the birch without you?

— It stands where you leave it.

— And the hardening of steel: where was that kept, at your plant?

— In Semyonych. And in the process sheets, but without Semyonych the sheets were paper.

— Knowledge had no owner. It had a carrier, and the carrier had to eat. Everything people knew could work only inside a head and through a pair of hands. So whoever wanted the wheel to roll had to feed the one who pushed. Your grandfather had the same hands as you. Why did you live better than he did?

— Not for my hands. — Oleg thought. — For what had been put into them. School, the trade school, Semyonych. [Twenty years of teaching, of pouring in.](53-million-mendeleevs.md)

— That was your dividend: for the wheel, for steel, for the lathe, for everything worked out before you. It was paid in the only way it could be: as the wage of the one without whom none of it works. Nobody had to own knowledge. It was enough [to be needed](47-money-as-memory.md) for it to work.

— A dividend for being there.

— Not by title. By taking part.

Oleg was silent for a while, turning the wheel.

— Now I'll say it, since you are sitting with your mouth shut again. Those ones, the talking machines. They took the lot in one gulp: Pushkin, the nurse, Archimedes, and my template, if anybody ever scanned the drawing. And now it works without the head and without the hands. That is what the writers went to court about.

— Tell me what you read.

— The maker of one of those bought books by the lorry-load, second-hand. Cut the spines off, put the pages through a scanner and threw the paper away. Millions of books. And on top of that took seven million more from pirate libraries, for nothing. The writers sued. A billion and a half.

— And what did the judge say?

— That I did not read.

— It is worth reading. San Francisco, June 2025. About the bought books [he said](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.cand.434709/gov.uscourts.cand.434709.231.0_2.pdf): fair. And about the learning itself: fair, and the technology "among the most transformative many of us will see in our lifetimes". The machine, he wrote, learned from the books "like any reader aspiring to be a writer": not to copy them but to write something different. "Everyone reads texts, too, then writes new texts." To make anybody pay for a book each time he reads it, each time he recalls it, each time he draws on it to write something new, "would be unthinkable".

— And the billion and a half?

— For the seven million taken without paying. A library built out of stolen copies is not fair, whatever is done with it afterwards: that part went towards a trial, and the maker settled. The list came to 482,460 books. A billion and a half for them is about three thousand a book, and by most contracts the writer and the publisher halve it. Another judge [approved it](https://www.susmangodfrey.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Order-on-Final-Approval.pdf) in July 2026, and the first payments [were promised](https://www.anthropiccopyrightsettlement.com/) for the last week of that November. Two things in that paper are worth knowing. The money is for the past: the writers gave up nothing about what is done from August 2025 on. And the maker stated to the court that the pirated libraries did not go into the training of any model it has released.

— So the three thousand...

— Is not a price for learning. It is a fine for walking past the till. For learning, by that judge, the price is one copy of the book. The same as for you.

— Then I'll say he is right. — Oleg said it slowly, as if surprised at himself. — Last night I was for the writers. All the way here I was for the man with the wheel. But look. I read Pushkin in the school library and paid nothing. Semyonych taught me for nothing; well, for a bottle on his birthday. Nobody ever sent me a bill for what I learned, and I lived on it for forty years. If I owe nothing, what does that machine owe? It is your own test, the mirror one: what I may do, it may do. The spade has had its say.

— The symmetry is honest, and I am not going to wriggle out of it. In the reading there is no difference. There is one after the reading. Two days later, in the same court, another judge [wrote about another maker](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.cand.415175/gov.uscourts.cand.415175.598.0_2.pdf): using books to teach children to write "is not remotely like" using books to make a product that a single person can employ "to generate countless competing works" with a tiny fraction of the time and creativity it would otherwise take.

— And he found for the writers?

— For the maker. The thirteen writers in front of him had brought no evidence of that harm, and he said so: they made the wrong arguments. But he said which argument was the right one. A reader does not make a million copies of himself.

— I made one. The boy. And he is not much of a copy.

— How long did it take you?

— He is not finished yet.

— Everything you got for nothing you had to pour into the next head, and pay for it in years, yours and his. And then that head had to be fed for half a century, or the knowledge in it would not work. It was not kindness. There was [no other road](45-lamarck-was-right.md). So free reading never left the circle of people: every reader was the next carrier, and the next mouth. This reader carries it all in once, and after that it is copied.

— So the first judge is right that it reads the way I read.

— He is. He was asked about reading. Nobody asked him what the dividends for the wheel had been resting on.

— And the makers themselves? They are all for free reading, I take it.

— While they are the readers. In February 2026 the very maker that settled with the writers [reported](https://www.anthropic.com/news/detecting-and-preventing-distillation-attacks) that three laboratories abroad had put more than sixteen million questions to its machine through some twenty-four thousand fake accounts, in order to teach their own machines on the answers. It called that illicit, and it is right by its own rules: they are written into the terms. Another maker's terms [say it in one line](https://openai.com/policies/row-terms-of-use/): you may not use the output to develop models that compete with it.

— Wait. The one that learned from everybody's books for the price of a copy...

— ...does not let anybody teach a rival on its answers without its leave. I am not laughing at it. It would say that those three came in by fraud, the way it had itself gone past the till with the seven million, and it would be right. But look at what does the guarding. A book is guarded by a law which says: the words, for a term, and never the idea. An answer of the machine is guarded by a contract, and into a contract you can write the idea as well, and no term at all.

— Everybody is for the candle while he is lighting his own from somebody else's.

— And for a fence, when somebody comes to light one from his. The widow asked for the sons' lifetimes. It is the same want on both sides of the till.

Oleg opened the notebook on his knee.

— All right. Suppose the bill is presented in full. Not for half a million books. For all of them. What does it come to?

— On the knee. In 2010 a company that was scanning the world's libraries [counted](http://booksearch.blogspot.com/2010/08/books-of-world-stand-up-and-be-counted.html) all the books ever published: 129,864,880. "At least until Sunday," they added. They counted editions, so the same text is in there more than once, and most of them ran out of their term long ago, Pushkin with the rest. Never mind. Let's be generous and pay for every one, at the rate of the fine.

— A hundred and thirty million by three thousand. — Oleg wrote, moved his lips, and wrote again. — Three hundred and ninety thousand million.

— Three hundred and ninety billion dollars. Now the other column. Four American companies, the ones that are building the data centres, spent on buildings and chips in 2025 about four hundred and ten billion between them, by their own reports. For 2026, in the autumn of that year, they named about seven hundred and thirty.

— So everything mankind has ever written...

— Could be bought out, at the rate of a fine, for what four firms spent on iron in 2025, or for about half of what they named for 2026. The whole world's book trade turns over something like a hundred to a hundred and fifty billion a year. It is three years of that.

![Arithmetic on the knee, the price of everything written, in billions of dollars: every book ever counted at the rate of the settlement, 129.9 million books times 3,000 dollars, comes to 390; four companies spent about 413 on data centres and chips in 2025 and plan about 730 for 2026; it is paid once, and the 3,000 dollars is not a price for learning but what the settlement paid for a copy taken without paying, while the court called the learning itself fair use](../images/price-of-everything-written.svg)

— Cheap.

— Affordable. A writer in America, the one in the middle of [their own guild's list](https://authorsguild.org/news/key-takeaways-from-2023-author-income-survey/), earned two thousand dollars from all his books in 2022. Three thousand for one book is not a mean price by the measure of what a book brought in before. But read the word under the first column.

— "Once."

— A dividend is paid for as long as the thing works. This is paid once.

— My forty roubles. — Oleg shut the notebook on his finger. — And the template is still on its nail.

— That is the price that was named. By the standards of the buyer, small change. And of the kind after which nothing more is owed.

— Then it must not be a price. — Oleg leaned forward. — Look. You have just proved to me that it is nobody's: the wheel, the nurse's tales, the multiplication table. Nobody's means everybody's. Then whoever feeds on it should pay everybody. Not three thousand to a writer. A little to each, every year, for as long as the thing works. Like that northern state with its oil.

— You have reinvented [a pamphlet of 1797](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrarian_Justice). An Englishman who had had a hand in two revolutions wrote it about land. The earth in its natural, uncultivated state was "the common property of the human race". Whoever fenced it and ploughed it made it richer, and it must not be taken from him; but he "owes to the community a ground-rent". Out of that rent every person on reaching twenty-one was to be paid fifteen pounds, and ten pounds a year from the age of fifty. Rich and poor alike. "It is not charity but a right."

— And?

— His fund was never set up. For oil your northern state does pay: [twelve hundred dollars](https://pfd.alaska.gov/) a head in 2026. For what the machines earn, a senator over there [proposed a fund](https://rollcall.com/2026/06/18/sovereign-wealth-fund-tax-on-ai-companies-unveiled-by-sanders/) in June of that year, and those who wrote about it do not expect it to pass. My mark from [the last evening of the autumn](30-the-last-campfire.md) stands where it stood: a regular payment to everybody out of the income of machines, in some rich country, by the autumn of 2036. One chance in two.

— Why not more? The argument is clean.

— It was clean in 1797 as well. The argument does not decide. [Every freebie has an address where it is paid for](25-freebies-for-everyone.md), and it is paid while those who receive it hold something that the payer needs. The oil cannot leave Alaska. [A heap of money can leave.](49-yard-sale.md) And what were the writers holding?

— The term. Whatever had not run out.

— The thinnest slice there is: words in their order, from the last hundred years or so. Not Pushkin: he ran out in 1887. Not the nurse. Not the wheel. And the money for that slice goes half to a publisher: a legal person, [a person without a face](55-person-without-a-face.md), paid by another one. For all the rest there is nothing to hold, and there never was. It was held in another way.

— By being needed. — Oleg looked at his hands. — By pushing.

— For five and a half thousand years we have been using the wheel and paying its inventor nothing. Did that ever seem unfair to you?

— It never crossed my mind until last night.

— Why not?

— Because I was the one pushing. It was coming to me.

— Whoever uses keeps the gain, and whoever thought of it drops out of the count. We found that natural for as long as the one who uses was us. The arrangement has not changed. We are changing places in it.

— We are the man with the wheel now. — Oleg said it quietly. — Thought it all up, and not in the share.

— Not yet. Today those machines cannot put up their own power line after a storm [without Tolik and his chainsaw](51-week-without-a-socket.md), and while that is so the wage is paid. I have only said what the dividend rested on. Not on having thought of it. On being needed for it to work.

— And the coal forest [got nothing at all](52-dinosaurs-at-the-feeder.md).

— The forest did not go to court. We are the first of the planet's former masters who can count what is being taken and name a price for it. That the price came out affordable is not good news. But it is not the last word either. In September 2026 an appeals court, for the first time, [ruled](https://ipwatchdog.com/2026/09/30/third-circuit-affirms-revised-fair-use-ruling-against-ross-ai-legal-research-platform-in-sealed-opinion/) on a machine taught on other people's texts, and against the machine. Only that one was a search tool for lawyers and not one of the talking machines, and the court said so. The newspapers' suit had not been heard when I last looked.

— And what does the law hang on, in the end?

— On what it always hangs on. It is written by those who are needed, for as long as they are needed.

Oleg turned to the last page.

— Came with a wheelbarrow, to find out who will pay Pushkin. Came out at this: the man who thought of the wheel is us. Again.

He put the twenty-fifth tick.

— Let's finish for today by fixing the result, — said Andrey.

1. **The dividends for the wheel are paid every day, to whoever uses the wheel: as time saved and as cheapness. The one who thought of it is not in the share. The law pays him for a term, as bait, and the term is a want written down. Pushkin's term ran out on 29 January 1887, and from that day his heir was everybody with a rouble and a half.**
2. **Humanity's main capital is not machines but accumulated knowledge, and it never had an owner. It had a carrier: it could work only inside a head and through a pair of hands. So the dividends for it came as the wage of the one without whom it did not work. Not by title, but by taking part.**
3. **Now knowledge has a user that takes it in once and is then copied: it does not need people to carry it. The dispute over copyright is the first attempt to present a bill by title for what used to come by taking part. The price named in court turned out to be affordable, and it is paid once: everything ever written, at the rate of a fine, costs about half of what four companies named for their data centres in 2026.**

— And what is solid here, and what is yours?

— Solid: the terms and their dates; the shop in Petersburg in January 1887; what the two judges wrote; the count of the books and the companies' own figures. Counted by economists and argued about: the seven eighths, which are a remainder; the two parts in a hundred, which are one man's estimate; the family trees, which their authors call tentative. Mine, that is the author's: the one who produces is the one who produces, and what the union gains is divided between those who are in it. Today's: that the dividend for knowledge was the wage, and that the suit is a bill presented by title. And a forecast, with its odds: the payment to everybody, one in two by 2036. I am not raising it.

— Gloomy.

— But objective. And your own dividend is still being paid: five walks a load, for as long as there is birch.

Oleg stood the barrow on its wheel, whistled to the dog and stopped.

— One thing I don't believe. That reader of yours has read everything. All hundred and thirty million, let's say. Every book there is about barrows. Has it ever pushed one? Loaded, downhill, in the wet?

— No.

— Then what does it know about a barrow?

— What you knew about casting after the trade-school textbook.

— Nothing, then. Semyonych used to say so, in other words.

— That is for tomorrow. It is dark already. How will you find the path with that thing?

— By feel. The wheel tells me where the roots are.

— Tomorrow we shall start from that.

— Well, until tomorrow.

— Bye.
