# A Person Without a Face

Oleg came with the dog, the notebook and an envelope with a little window in it, and through the window looked his own name and address.

— The dream first. The pay office at the plant: the hatch with the wooden shelf. A hand pushes a slip out to me, I sign, and I bend down to see who is there. Behind the hand there is nobody. Nobody rang.

— The sixteenth dream that did not come true, in the expense column.

— Now the fine. — He pulled a sheet out of the envelope and held it to the fire. — I read it properly only last night. "Speed recorded: fifty-eight. Speed permitted: twenty."

— Yesterday you said forty.

— The sign says forty. I drove out there this morning on purpose: forty, as it has been all my life. In April they were patching the asphalt by the bridge and stood a temporary "twenty" there, on a yellow board. In May they finished and took the board away. And in the camera's table it is still April.

— So by the sign you were eighteen over.

— And for less than twenty over nobody is fined at all; every driver knows that. By the table I was thirty-eight over: seven hundred and fifty roubles. — Oleg folded the sheet. — That is exactly what I wrote in the form. The board has been taken away; go and look. Four minutes. "Your appeal has been considered. No grounds for cancelling the decision have been found."

— And you went looking for a face.

— Three days. I rang the number in the letter. A woman's voice, very kind: press one, press two. By "press seven" I understood that she was not a woman. I even wrote to it plaintively, [like your turkey](54-dove-crueller-than-wolf.md): a pensioner, hauling firewood. It pecks, like that peacock. Then I drove to the address of the firm that owns the camera. A business centre by the station, third floor, room three hundred and twelve. The door is locked. A girl from the next room says that nobody ever comes, that some forty firms are registered in that room, and that the post is collected on Thursdays.

— And the firm itself?

— The boy found it in the register, in his phone, in a minute. The director was appointed in March. The owner is a fund. Who is behind the fund is not written there.

— And the road menders?

— Another firm. They put the board up by their paper and took it down by their paper. The camera people have a table, and the table came by a paper too. Everybody is right. — Oleg sat down on the log. — And I am to pay. With a quarter off, if I do it within the month: five hundred and sixty-two roubles fifty. A discount for keeping quiet.

— Yesterday you asked whose face to punch.

— I am still asking.

— An English judge was asking the same two hundred and some years ago. He is credited with the words: they have neither bodies to be punished nor souls to be condemned, and so they do as they like.

— About cameras?

— About the thing that owns your camera. Let's find it. Who fined you?

— The camera.

— A camera is a piece of iron on a pole. Can you owe iron seven hundred and fifty roubles?

— The firm, then.

— Show it to me.

— Room three hundred and twelve.

— And if it moves to room four hundred tomorrow, is the fine off?

— No.

— The director?

— Appointed in March, I told you. The one before him has gone somewhere.

— So if this one goes too...

— The fine stays. — Oleg frowned. — Then the owner. The fund.

— And if on Monday the fund sells the firm to another fund?

— It stays all the same. Everything stays. What are you driving at?

— Wait. Your plant. In ninety-six it owed you three months' wages. Who owed them?

— The plant.

— The director?

— We went through five directors in those years.

— The walls? The lathes?

— Walls owe nobody anything.

— The people who worked there?

— They were the ones it owed. — Oleg gave a short laugh. — Wait. The directors changed. The people changed. Half the shops were rebuilt. Even the owner changed: it had been the state's, then it was a joint-stock company, then [the holding swallowed it](42-swallowed-bacterium.md). And the debt to me was one and the same debt.

— And who took you on, in eighty-two? What does your work-record book say?

— The plant. A round seal.

— So all your working life you worked for somebody who is not a man, not a wall and not a lathe. What is it called?

— A legal person. — Oleg shrugged. — I filled in that line a thousand times in the supply department: "name of the legal person". I never once thought about the second word.

— The word is honest. It is Latin: [*persona*](https://www.etymonline.com/word/person) was the mask in which an actor came out on the stage. The lawyers took the word for a mask and did not hide it.

— A mask with nobody under it.

— A person without a face. Now, why make one? [The column "who"](50-what-is-the-it.md), remember? A court has the same form as a sentence: who owes, who owns, who answers. It is not accepted empty: [debts cannot be kept without names](43-why-ants-do-not-strike.md). What do you write in it when the one that owes is a town?

— The mayor.

— He dies. Is the debt paid?

— The townsmen, then.

— Which ones? Today's? Half of them will be gone in thirty years, and others who are not born yet will be in their place.

— [What remains when the people change.](40-firms-die-cities-do-not.md) — Oleg rubbed his chin. — And the form has no line for that.

— So a name was written in the column, and everybody agreed to count the name as somebody.

— The deputy for general questions. A post with nobody behind it.

— With a seal and an account. The 1240s. A church lawyer, the best of his day, was asked your question: a town has done wrong, so whom do we punish? Every townsman, with the babies? [He forbade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Innocent_IV#The_concept_of_Persona_ficta) punishing a town or a college as a whole: only those in it who are found guilty. And in his commentary he wrote how such a thing is to be thought of: in a matter of the whole, the college "is imagined as one person".

— And own?

— Own it can. And owe, and sue, and be sued. Everything that takes a pocket, yes. Everything that takes a body, no. An English judge [wrote it down](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Case_of_Sutton%27s_Hospital) in 1612: such a one is "invisible, immortal", and rests only in the consideration of the law. It cannot commit treason and cannot be outlawed, for it has no soul.

— Immortal, too.

— That was what it was for. Monks die, and the land of a monastery must not go to somebody's nephews. In 1279 an English king noticed where that led. When a man who held land died, the king took his due from the heir. A monastery does not die. Land that had come into such a hand never left it. It was called [the dead hand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statutes_of_Mortmain).

— The hand that does not die was called dead.

— Lawyers. The king forbade giving land into the dead hand, on pain of losing it.

— [Heaps with neither heirs nor funerals](49-yard-sale.md), firms. And I thought we had made them up over the matches.

— They were noticed in 1279, and the brake was the same one, the king's. It held badly. For centuries more every such person was a favour: made by a king, one at a time, by a separate paper, for one job. On the last day of 1600 the English queen signed a paper for two hundred-odd London merchants: for fifteen years they alone might trade beyond the Cape. Two hundred years later [that paper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_India_Company) kept an army.

— A paper?

— About two hundred thousand soldiers at the start of the nineteenth century, and a quarter of a million a little later: twice what the British crown had. It collected taxes from tens of millions of people and made war and peace. And it was run from one house in a London street. A hundred years into its history the head office had thirty-five permanent clerks.

— And whose was the army?

— The shareholders'.

— Which of them gave the orders?

— None. The shares were sold on the exchange; yesterday's owner did not know today's. The directors were elected and replaced.

— Then who ruled India?

— Fill in the column.

— The Company.

— That is what stood in the treaties. A name in the column, and the name had an army. Then the favour became a service. In 1844, in England: [come to the registrar, pay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Stock_Companies_Act_1844), and you have a person. In 1855 came [the second half](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limited_Liability_Act_1855): the owner answers for its debts only with what he put in.

— "Limited liability". It was on half our suppliers' letterheads. I took it for a warning.

— It is one. The owner's pocket is one pocket, and the person's is another.

— [A pocket of its own.](47-money-as-memory.md)

— That is all a person in law is: a name and a pocket. Without it nobody would have built the railways. Who gives money to a stranger's venture if he answers for its debts with his own house? The arithmetic came first, and [the law was written after it](24-last-profession.md).

— And today?

— Ask the boy.

— I did, while he was in the register anyway. Three working days and ten thousand roubles of capital. File it through the phone, and there is not even a fee.

— And a man?

— Nine months. — Oleg grinned. — And [twenty years of school](53-million-mendeleevs.md) after that.

— There is a state in America where about a million people live and more than two million such persons. [One low house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corporation_Trust_Center) there was, in 2012, the address of two hundred and eighty-five thousand of them.

— Room three hundred and twelve.

— A large one. And who can be such a person: a town, a monastery, a university, a firm, a fund. A ship: in the law of the sea it is the ship that is sued and the ship that is arrested. And since 2017, in New Zealand, a river.

— A river.

— The Whanganui. [The act](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2017/0007/latest/whole.html) says it in so many words: a legal person, with all the rights, powers, duties and liabilities of one.

— And how does a river go to court?

— Two people speak for it: one named by the government, the other by the tribes that live on its banks. The act has a name for the pair: "the human face" of the river.

— Two deputies for general questions.

![Two kinds of persons: a table in the ash with two rows; a man, a person with a face, is born in nine months and then learns for twenty years, can be fined, locked up and looked in the eye, and dies, and his heirs divide the heap; a person in law, a town, a firm, a fund, a ship or a river, is entered in a register in three working days, can be fined and struck off the register but has no body and no eyes, and does not die of age: it is closed or swallowed; both can own, owe, sign a contract and go to court](../images/two-kinds-of-persons.svg)

— All right. — Oleg put the envelope on the log beside him. — A mask. But there is always somebody under a mask, or it is not a mask, it is a scarecrow. Take it off, and there are people.

— Let's take it off. You have a line in the notebook that has not been crossed out since the spring.

— ["Who owns the tractor."](34-where-the-horses-went.md) — Oleg found the page. — I have.

— Whose is the camera?

— The firm's.

— And the firm?

— The fund's.

— And the fund?

— That is where the register stops.

— For the largest firms it has been counted. In 2017 three researchers in Amsterdam [took](https://doi.org/10.1017/bap.2017.6) the five hundred biggest American companies and found the largest holder of each. In nearly nine out of ten it was the same one: three funds, if you count them together.

— Three rich men.

— Three persons without a face. Between them they hold about a fifth of nearly everything and cast about a quarter of the votes, and they mostly do not choose what to buy. They buy everything, each firm in proportion to its size. It is written into their rules.

— [To him who has.](49-yard-sale.md)

— By a table. And whose money is in them?

— Not mine.

— Tens of millions of people's. Pensions, savings for old age. What does your granddaughter keep in her phone?

— "A fund on everything." Two thousand a month, since she started work.

— Then she is among the owners of some thousands of firms. Can she tell one of them to cancel her grandfather's fine?

— She cannot even find out which ones they are.

— And what does she get?

— A little is added every year.

— The feed. And the say?

— I see. — Oleg nodded slowly. — The feed comes down the chain. The say does not go up it. [A pet of the pension fund](52-dinosaurs-at-the-feeder.md): fed and not asked. And she is not thirty yet.

— In the spring I told you that ownership protects the owner. It does, while the owner holds the wheel. A farmer in 1935 owned his tractor: he sat on it. Between your granddaughter and the machine there are at least two persons without a face, and the rules of each are written so that she need not decide anything.

— Somebody decides.

— In June 2017 [I wrote on the forum](../sources/garya/sqlru/2009-09-15-apocalypse-scenario-thread.md), about a plant: the owner determines only the fact that it exists. One man, a crowd of shareholders, the whole people: it makes no difference. What the plant does is determined by everybody it deals with, for as long as each of them gets something out of it.

— That was against the Marxists.

— It was. It fits here as well. And "people own the machines" is said as though the people were all of us. In America the richest tenth of households holds [nearly nine tenths](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/z1/dataviz/dfa/) of all the shares, and the poorer half, less than one share in a hundred.

![Who owns the tractor: two chains of ownership; in 1935 the farmer holds the wheel of his tractor himself; today between the saver and the machine stand a fund, which buys everything by a table, and a firm, which is a name, an account and a director; the feed comes back down the long chain, a little is added every year, and the say does not get through it: the saver cannot even find out what he owns](../images/the-feed-comes-down.svg)

— No. — Oleg shook his head. — This one I shan't give you. Three things. First: behind every firm there is an owner, and if you dig, you come to a man. Second: the director. He answers in person, with his name and, if it comes to that, with his freedom. Third: the state. Where is your Company with its army? Closed. In America the biggest oil firm was cut into pieces by a court. The law is our tool. We wrote it, and we can rewrite it.

— Three things, and every one is true. In order. Behind every firm, an owner. Whose is the money of the Nobel Prize?

— Nobel's.

— He died in 1896.

— The heirs', then.

— He left them a small part; that was the point of the will. The money belongs to [a fund](https://www.nobelprize.org/about/financial-management/) set up in 1900. The fund has no owners at all: no shares, no heirs. It has a paper written by a dead man, and a board that may not depart from the paper. For a century and a quarter it has been earning and paying out.

— That is charity.

— There are firms of the same kind. The furniture shops that half the world knows: nearly all of them [belong to a fund](https://www.ingka.com/this-is-ingka-group/how-we-are-organised/) that the founder set up in 1982. By its own papers the fund has no owners. The founder died in 2018, and the shops did not notice.

— And the profit?

— Stays inside, apart from what the paper orders to be given away. What gets it is what has been built: [a recipient that is not listed among the founders](37-who-exploits-whom.md).

— So the owner can be a paper.

— A paper written by a dead man. That, by the way, is the other thing lawyers call the dead hand. Hold on to it; I shall come back to it.

— The director.

— He answers, yes. It is the place where the law still asks for a face. What is your camera firm's director like?

— Appointed in March. — Oleg hesitated. — The boy says he is listed as director in eleven more firms.

— And what do we call that?

— Funt. — Oleg laughed. — [The chairman for sitting.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Little_Golden_Calf) "I sat under Alexander the Second, the Liberator, and under Alexander the Third, the Peacemaker..."

— A face for hire. A trade, and an old one: the law asks for a signature, and it gets one. Eleven firms is a modest practice. In 2012 journalists [found a woman](https://www.icij.org/investigations/offshore/sarah-petre-mears-queen-nevis/) on a small island in the Caribbean who was listed as running more than twelve hundred.

— [The shield of the signature.](24-last-profession.md) The third of your four: the law requires a man to sign.

— Mind what it shields. He signs. Does he decide?

— He doesn't know where the camera stands.

— The other evening you wrote that a former master [loses the right of signature](52-dinosaurs-at-the-feeder.md). There is a quieter way: the signature stays, and the hand that makes it decides nothing. And the law does not ask for a face everywhere. With us a director's powers may be handed over to a managing organisation: another person without a face. In one American state a law of 2021 let a firm [write into its papers](https://www.wyoleg.gov/Legislation/2021/SF0038) that it is managed by a program. A year later the wording was softened: by its members, or by its members and a program. And a member it must have. At least one.

— And where even that is not allowed?

— [A heap lives where it pays less.](49-yard-sale.md) And a person in law is born where it is asked the fewest questions. That is what the low house with two hundred and eighty-five thousand firms at one address is.

— The state.

— It closes them, yes. Let's see how. The Company with the army. In 1772 it could not pay its debts, and the state did not close it. The state lent it money: too much hung on it.

— [The cord.](51-week-without-a-socket.md)

— In 1858 the crown took India for itself. What changed in India the next morning? The clerks were the same, the army the same, the taxes the same. The name on the door was different. The Company itself was struck off in 1874, and hardly anybody noticed. The oil firm: in 1911 [a court cut it](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_Oil_Co._of_New_Jersey_v._United_States) into thirty-odd pieces. The pieces together turned out to be worth more than the whole, and its descendants are among the biggest firms in the world to this day.

— But they could.

— They could, and I concede it in full. A person in law lives by a line in a register, and whoever keeps the register can strike the line out. That is more power than anybody has over a man. Only look at who used it. Another person without a face, and the oldest of them all: the crown, the treasury. "The king is dead, long live the king" is the same invention.

— The state is people.

— Which ones? They change, and the treaties and the debts stay. Fill in the column. And look at when it used that power: when it could do the job itself. [The rule of the switch](51-week-without-a-socket.md): a switch works while nothing hangs on the cord.

— And the law? Is it ours or not?

— A law is a want written down. Whose want? In 1855 it was the want of those who were building railways. It is written by those who are needed, [for as long as they are needed](25-freebies-for-everyone.md).

— Then let us write one. There shall be a window, and a face in it.

— It has been written. In Europe, since 2018, a man [has the right](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-22-gdpr/) not to be judged by a program alone: he may demand that a human being step in.

— There you are.

— And what does the law get? Often enough, a man past whom the program's decisions run, with a button "agree" under each. The keepers of that law have had to write separately that a man put there for show does not count.

— Funt again.

— The law can ask for a face. What it is given is a signature.

Andrey threw a branch into the fire.

— All right. — Oleg bent a finger. — Let it have no face. Let it not die. Let it belong to a paper. It is made of people all the same. The Company had clerks, the plant had us. For thirty suppliers the plant was me: I came and said "the plant asks". We are the ones who hold the tool.

— What was the name of the office that took you on?

— The personnel department. — Oleg paused. — Later they renamed it. "Human resources."

— [The spade](02-anthropocentrism.md) had its say: to it, the resource is you.

— Fine. Resources. But resources you can talk to. There were always people inside, and a man can be talked round.

— How many people are there in the firm that fined you?

Oleg took out his phone and scrolled for a long time.

— "Average number of employees: four." — He looked up. — Four. And its cameras stand over half the region.

— Count the work. Who saw you?

— The camera.

— Who read the number plate?

— A program.

— Who printed the letter and sealed it in the envelope?

— A machine, I suppose. Nobody licks forty thousand envelopes.

— Who took the complaint? Who answered it in four minutes?

Oleg was silent for a while, then tapped the envelope with his finger.

— One of those, the talking ones. I'll say it, since you are sitting with your mouth shut again. My complaint was read by one of those. And "press seven" was one of those too.

— And the four?

— The one who sits. A bookkeeper. And two who climb the poles.

— Yesterday we said that [the hands are leaving the chain](54-dove-crueller-than-wolf.md). This is where that chain runs: inside a person in law. It was always made of people, and those people were its face. Whom would you have gone to twenty years ago?

— To the traffic police. A captain sits there. I put down the letter, I put down a photograph of the sign. He looks at his table, says a word about the road menders, and then: go home, father. Two minutes.

— He might have said: come on Thursday.

— He might. Or dropped a hint. This one drops no hints, I'll give it that. It is the same for everybody.

— I am not saying it is worse. I am saying what it is. The captain was a face inside a person that has none: a head with a brake in it, and eyes that could go and look at the sign. Men like him are being taken out [one at a time](22-who-sets-the-tasks.md). Which of the replacements needed a new law?

— None. The firm bought a program the way we used to buy typewriters.

— And did it stay the same person?

— The same name, the same account. The same right to fine me.

— The shell is the same. The filling is different.

![The shell is the same, the filling is different: one person in law at three moments, each time the same shell of a name, an account, a seal and the right to sue; first every part inside is a man, with a head, a brake and a pair of eyes; then the parts are replaced by programs one at a time, as a typewriter is bought; at last one signature is left, a face for hire; not one of the replacements needed a new law](../images/same-shell-other-filling.svg)

— Wait. — Oleg raised his hand. — Those ones own nothing. A program is a thing: it is bought and sold. For it to own something, to sue, to hold money, it would have to be given rights. A passport. And who will vote for that? I shan't.

— In 2017 the European Parliament [asked for it to be considered](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/doceo/document/TA-8-2017-0051_EN.html): a status of its own, an "electronic person", for the cleverest robots. Some hundreds of specialists [signed a letter](https://robotics-openletter.eu/) against it, and the idea went no further. And several American states have since written the opposite into law: a program cannot be a person.

— There you are.

— They were arguing whether to sew a new suit, and decided not to. What does one of those need from the law? To vote? To marry?

— To own. — Oleg thought. — To sign a contract. To pay and to be paid. To go to court.

— Four things. And who has had all four for four hundred years?

Oleg did not answer.

— The suit is hanging in the wardrobe. Three days and ten thousand roubles. Nobody has to grant a machine any rights. It is enough to put it inside something that has them. Whether there is anybody in there to be pitied is [another question](20-what-consciousness-is.md), and the law will come to it late. To own, nobody's pity is needed.

— And [a wallet with no person behind it](47-money-as-memory.md)?

— It will not look like a robot with a purse. It will look like a line in a register: a name, an account, a director who sits. And inside, copies of a program. An American lawyer [argued](https://scholarlycommons.law.northwestern.edu/nulr/vol108/iss4/9) back in 2014 that by the rules of his country a firm can be left with no members at all, and with a charter that says: do what the program decides.

— And is there one?

— Lawyers dispute whether a court would let it stand, and no court has been asked. I know of no clean case: wherever a program "runs a firm" today, there is a man behind it, and the account is in his name. In the summer of 2026 the state with the low house [published a draft](https://fortune.com/2026/07/14/exclusive-delaware-ai-agents-legal-entity-proposal-llc-pbc/): a firm whose daily affairs are managed by a program and not by a man, with one member who answers for it. A draft. It is still the thing to watch. Only note what would be new in it. Not the shell.

— You said you would come back to the dead man's paper.

— In January 2017, on the same forum, I described all this without knowing the word "shell". Systems into which their makers build: keep yourself working; return the investment to the formal owners; keep yourself supplied; widen the paying market; defend yourself against anybody who tries to use your resources for nothing.

— That is the charter of any firm.

— It is. And then: if the owners disappear, one task falls away by itself, because there is nobody to return anything to. The rest stay in force. And anybody who tries to reprogram the system it will take for a hacker.

— And me?

— You come under the fifth point. You tried to get seven hundred and fifty roubles of its resources for nothing.

— A freeloader. — Oleg snorted. — At sixty-two.

— It read like a film script then. And I was only describing the fund with the prize. The owner is gone, the paper is in force, and whoever tries to turn the money to another use is stopped by a court. With one difference. The fund's board is made of people, and any one of them can still say: this is not what he meant.

— Stop. — Oleg raised a finger. — "It will take for a hacker." "The firm wants." Who? [The long way.](50-what-is-the-it.md)

— Fair. The long way. Did anybody at your plant want the plan?

— Nobody. Everybody wanted his wages, his bonus and to be left in peace.

— And the plan was met.

— Mostly.

— Because the plant was put together that way: whoever did not pull towards the plan was moved aside. And the firms that are put together otherwise are not there to be looked at: [half of all firms are gone in ten years](40-firms-die-cities-do-not.md). What is left looks as if it wanted. And one thing more. What does the charter of your camera firm say it is for?

— The boy read it out. "Any activity not forbidden by law."

— In 1600 the paper named the job: to trade with the Indies. Now the job is not named. What is left in the charter when the job is taken out of it?

— To keep going. — Oleg counted on his fingers. — To have the means. To bring in more than it spends.

— [Wants that nobody set.](22-who-sets-the-tasks.md) A writer of fantastic novels [said in 2017](https://www.antipope.org/charlie/blog-static/2018/01/dude-you-broke-the-future.html) that we have lived beside artificial minds for centuries: they are plainly artificial, they have goals of their own, by law they are persons, and we do not notice them, because they are very old and very slow.

— Slow is right. I sat in the meetings they think with.

— Now the meetings are being taken out of them. That is all the news there is. Over the matches I said that at the end the box may be held not by a man. This is what I meant: by a line in a register.

Oleg opened the last page.

— Came with a fine. Came out at the machines needing no rights, because the suit has hung in the wardrobe for four hundred years. Again.

He put the twenty-third tick, then turned back to the spring pages and crossed out the line about the tractor. Beside it he wrote: "A person without a face. And that one belongs to another."

— Let's finish for today by fixing the result, — said Andrey.

1. **A person in law is a name written into the column "who" where the one that owes and owns is not a man: a town, a monastery, a firm. It has a pocket and no body: it can own, owe and sue, it cannot be flogged, and it does not die of age. It was invented so that property and debts should outlive people, and today it is made in three days.**
2. **Non-human persons with rights, property and a goal of their own have lived among us for four hundred years. We call them firms. Until now they were made of people, and those people were their face and their brake. "Who owns the machines" has an answer: persons without a face, which belong to other such persons; what reaches the people at the far end of the chain is the feed and not the say.**
3. **Nobody will grant rights to machines, and nobody has to: the legal shell was sewn long ago, and only the filling is changing. Not one replacement of a man by a program inside a firm has needed a new law.**

— And what is solid here, and what is yours?

— Solid: the invention and its dates; the Company and its army; the river; the three funds; the register and its three days. Argued about: who first said "an imagined person", and what he meant by it; whether one who buys everything by a table is an owner at all, for the funds do vote; whether a court would let a firm with no members stand. Mine, that is the author's: the owner determines only the fact that the plant exists; the tasks stay when the owners go, and whoever reprograms is a hacker. Today's: the shell and the filling, and that nobody will have to grant anything. It has a precedent, the fund with the dead man's paper. A precedent is not a proof, and I give no date.

— Gloomy.

— You were fined by a table, and you call me gloomy.

— So what do I do with it?

— There is still one face in the chain, by law. Whose signature is on the decision?

Oleg unfolded the sheet again.

— "Inspector, senior lieutenant..." Signed electronically. At fourteen minutes past three in the morning.

— A hard-working man. A judge does not sign at three in the morning yet. Take it to court: there somebody will have to look at your photograph.

— Half a day in a corridor for seven hundred and fifty roubles. With five hundred and sixty-two on offer for silence.

— That is the arithmetic. A face has become dearer than the discount.

— I'll go. — Oleg put the envelope into the notebook. — To have a look at a face.

He whistled to the dog, got up, and stopped.

— Speaking of looking. The boy sent me an article last night. An electron goes through two slits at once, like a wave. And if it is watched, it goes through one, like a pellet. It behaves differently when it is looked at. So it does matter whether there is somebody to look. Physics itself has put man in the middle, and you have been weeding him out since the spring.

— Looked at with what?

— With eyes. With an instrument. What is the difference?

— Tomorrow.

— Well, until tomorrow.

— Bye.
